Timeline B · Campaign B10

The Quiet Rule of Brevair

Brevair Era

146–147 ARV Primary location: Brevair, House Valecour

Core Premise

Roughly ten years after Campaign B9. A story of private corruption within a noble house that ends as the quiet remaking of an entire city — a city does not fall to conquest if it can be taught to prefer its chains. Timeline B's first close look at Brevair, previously only mentioned as one of the world's major powers.

Party & Key Figures

  • Delryn Vale / Valecour PC Alive; becomes public face of House Valecour
    Human Shadow Sorcerer; an acknowledged bastard raised within House Valecour, transformed over the campaign into the keystone conduit of Rhalen's rise. The player character.
  • Rhalen Valecour Alive; becomes uncrowned ruler of Brevair
    The hidden architect of House Valecour's engineered fall and rebirth.
  • Seran Mordane Killed by Rhalen
    Veil captain and Delryn's early handler; briefly seizes command of the house before being outmaneuvered and removed by Rhalen.
  • Malrec Killed by Seran's overreach
    Former Veil master within the house.

Act-by-Act

Seran's Hold

Seran Mordane begins a program of private conditioning built through escalating control and dependency, reshaping Delryn from a politically vulnerable noble remnant into a highly tuned instrument of the house's ambitions.

The Revelation of Rhalen

Hidden letters reveal Rhalen Valecour, not Seran, as the true architect behind House Valecour's engineered collapse — including the deaths that cleared Delryn's path and even the terms of his own conditioning under Seran.

Malrec's Fall

A public demonstration meant to test Delryn's training becomes a succession event: Seran uses Delryn's altered abilities to kill Malrec and briefly seizes command of the house.

Rhalen's Rise

Rhalen eliminates Seran and the surviving chapter structure, outgrowing the chapter itself to become the effective ruler of Brevair without a royal title. He marries Delryn, publicly naming what was already true privately.

World State After Campaign B10

Geography. Brevair's capital and House Valecour are established as major Timeline B locations for the first time. (This site names Brevair's capital Corvane — see the Canon Ledger — since the source atlas leaves it explicitly undetermined.)

Magic. No new magical category, but a clear demonstration of how Timeline B's Veil-conditioning mechanics (first seen in Campaign B5) can be weaponised deliberately for political engineering, not just accident.

The Durrel Line. Not directly involved — this campaign centres entirely on House Valecour and the Veil chapter operating within Brevair, geographically and narratively separate from the Durrel Ridge storyline of Campaign B9.

Institutions. Rhalen becomes Brevair's quiet administrative ruler — civic, widow, merchant, and chapel channels all function through him. House Valecour is restored as a legitimate public mask for a darker internal order. The campaign closes not with battle or divine rupture, but with something colder: a city that works, a ruler who needs no crown, and a marriage built on a foundation neither partner is free of.

Canon status: follows the source account directly.
