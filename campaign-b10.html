Timeline B · Campaign B9

The Renewed Oath of the Ridge

—

136–137 ARV Primary location: Durrel Ridge, Silver Bastion

Core Premise

Taren Durrel begins at Durrel Ridge, where family legacy and hidden rites intensify. He undergoes Warden induction and is drawn into deeper Durrel history and secret architecture beneath the estate — where structures respond to bloodline, words, and witness, not mere ceremony.

Party & Key Figures

  • Taren Durrel
    20, Paladin on the Oath of the Ridge, later Heartwarded and publicly aligned to Luminara. The player character of this campaign.
  • Mara
    Archer and Taren's closest confidant; later appointed Captain of the Wardens.
  • Cassien
    Arcane specialist; later appointed Arcane Warden under witness rules.
  • Arden (Ridge-era)
    Initially a close companion, later revealed as the campaign's tyrant, using coercive systems and Veil methods to control the Ridge — a different figure from Arden Vale of the Six Travellers.

Act-by-Act

The Vault Opens

Taren descends repeatedly into Tarenus's Vault beneath the Ridge, confronting an ancient truth-mechanism — script chambers and seal logic that demand witness and reject coercive authority, responding directly to the Warden bloodline.

Arden's Regime

Arden's influence becomes systemic: a constructed "hold" architecture designed to make obedience feel like safety, spreading control rituals beyond the Ridge into Bastion's civic machinery.

The Breach and Arden's Death

A breach reveals two bodies outside Taren's door. Mara kills Arden with a single arrow. His death doesn't instantly free Taren — the hold architecture still searches for a handler — but it breaks the central authority.

The Kill-Switch

Taren, Mara, and Cassien void Arden's hold under true witness and destroy the Root Engine chamber's central structure. The Ridge is cleansed of collaborators, and Taren formally reclaims Tarenus's Vault, turning decisively toward the Light.

Liberating Bastion

Taren leads a unified force to Bastion's Tribunal Undercroft, invoking Heart and Luminara under true witness to break the lingering Veil/Shah civic anchor — returning the stone to neutral legitimacy rather than conquering it.

Final World State

Durrel Ridge is reclaimed, cleansed, and publicly Light-aligned, rebuilt as a Warden institution anchored in witness law. Taren swears the Renewed Oath of the Ridge — sanctuary, witness, consent, and "Light with teeth" — with Mara as Captain of the Wardens and Cassien as Arcane Warden. Bastion is liberated at its civic anchor point, though the chain is confirmed as a broader phenomenon that could resurface elsewhere.

Canon status: "Taren Durell" in the source is normalized to Taren Durrel here. Note the Arden of this campaign is a distinct figure from Arden Vale of the Six Travellers — same name, unrelated character.
