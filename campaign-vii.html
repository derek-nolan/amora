Timeline A · Campaign VII

The Warden Executor

Age of Theocracy I

c. 5800 ARV Primary location: The Gate

Opening World State

The world swings back from the Reawakening's scholarly ambiguity toward religious certainty. The uncertainty of distributed custody frightened many people more than the old darkness had. The Gate becomes capital of the Wardens' Theocracy.

Party & Key Figures

  • Elian Thorne
    Paladin and early Executor; founds the Executor office at the Gate with Dorn Kael.
  • Dorn Kael
    Explorer and Executor figure; helps transform expeditionary necessity into permanent institution.

Act-by-Act

Founding the Executor Office

Elian Thorne and Dorn Kael answer the age's fear by founding the Executor office at the Gate, believing discipline can preserve the fragile return of magic. Their circuits of investigation protect settlements from relic cults and predatory sorcery.

Power Gathers

Elian becomes both spiritual and political authority while Dorn helps turn expeditionary necessity into permanent institution. Inquisitors, archivists, and local officials learn to speak of salvation through law.

Discipline Without Limits

The campaign's central struggle isn't whether law is needed, but whether any institution built to save people can accept limits on its own certainty. By the end, the Wardens have become a theocracy.

Final World State

The Warden state hardens into a rigid system that will later be codified by Sirian in Campaign VIII. Archives and edicts from this time survive long after the theocracy falls, haunting later republics with "precedent."
