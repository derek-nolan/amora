Timeline A · Campaign VII

The Warden Executor

Age of Theocracy I — "Through Law, Salvation"

c. 5800 ARV Primary location: The Gate

Opening World State

The world swings back from the Collegium's scholarly ambiguity toward religious certainty. The Gate rises as capital of the new Wardens' Theocracy, and Elian Dorn becomes its spiritual and political leader — the Warden Executor, whose creed "Through Law, Salvation" comes to define an entire age.

Party & Key Figures

  • Elian Dorn PC "The Warden Executor." Spiritual and political leader of the Wardens; founds the Executor office that becomes capital of the Theocracy.
  • Inquisitors, archivists, and underground dissidents
    The full spectrum of a young theocracy: enforcers, record-keepers, and the resistance quietly forming beneath both.

Act-by-Act

Consolidation

Elian's motto — "Through Law, Salvation" — summarises the entire age taking shape around him. The campaign follows the consolidation of theocratic power in real, procedural detail: codifying sins into formal doctrine, building the first inquisitorial circuits, and deciding, case by case, how far "salvation by discipline" can reasonably be pushed before it curdles into something else.

Reform or Resistance

As Warden decrees begin echoing old Dominion patterns more and more closely, the campaign forces a genuine choice: reform the theocracy from within its own structures, or resist it openly from outside them. Neither path is clean, and the campaign doesn't pretend otherwise.

Hardening

By its end, the Warden state has hardened into a rigid system — one that Sirian will later codify even further in Campaign VIII. Archives and edicts written during Elian's tenure survive long after the theocracy itself eventually falls, haunting later republics as cited "precedent" whenever old certainties threaten to return.

World State After Campaign VII

Geography. The Gate is confirmed as the theocracy's capital, transformed from whatever it was before into the institutional seat of the Wardens' Theocracy.

Magic/Law. The Executor office is founded here, establishing a formal structure of inquisitorial law that will define the age's entire second phase under Sirian.

The Durrel Line. No Durrel figures appear this campaign — the Theocracy age represents one of the timeline's periods where institutional religious authority, rather than any individual bonded pair, drives the central conflict.

Institutions. The Wardens' Theocracy is founded and consolidated under Elian Dorn's Executor office, setting the stage directly for Campaign VIII's Gate Codex.

Canon status: follows the source account directly.
