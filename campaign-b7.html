The Lantern Crew

Chosen family over inherited cages, and the cost of maintaining independence

Core Premise

A grounded, classic-adventuring campaign of civic cleanup and family drama, built around a new youth squad growing up in The Lantern's orbit. Mark "Mark Val" (Marcus Valmere), a human Shadow Sorcerer, arrives in Evermere under an alias — exiled from a minor Valmere branch after a forbidden relationship with Darian Corvett, Dain's younger brother, and seeking refuge and purpose far from the shame and political blowback of home.

Party & Key Figures

Act-by-Act

Act I — Mark Arrives / The Youth Squad Is Born

Mark reaches The Lantern under his alias, and Maera sees through the noble polish but admits him to work under supervision. Mark and Bren start as rookies on cellar, underworks, smuggler, and dockside jobs. His Shadow magic becomes visible early, and he begins learning not to panic-cast it. By the end he is accepted as a youth-squad member rather than a tolerated guest, while Maera is confirmed as the Lantern's operational anchor and gatekeeper.

Act II — Evermere Cleanup

The Lantern Crew stabilises Evermere by cutting corrupt harbour chains and underworks smuggling routes. Cassian builds evidence through civic channels rather than unsanctioned violence — paper kills more effectively than random bloodshed. The crew buys and converts the Lantern Annex into bunkhouse, training space, and operations centre, letting the network grow without turning the public tavern into a militia headquarters.

Act III — Family Drama: the Valmere/Corvett Thread

Mark's true identity and exile surface among leadership. He confesses the arranged-marriage politics, his relationship with Darian, and the way both families tried to manage succession through him. Dain recognises echoes of his own father's control tactics rather than turning Mark's confession into leverage. House Corvett later cancels the sister's marriage. Mark becomes family-adjacent to Dain and Joryn — trusted and protected, never owned.

Act IV — Lucan Returns

Lucan reappears in Evermere and targets Mark using attention and private rituals framed as comfort. Mark keeps the relationship secret at first. Lucan attempts a conditioning-like ritual designed to bind Mark emotionally and psychologically; Bren discovers enough to force the problem into the open, and Mark eventually confesses. Dain and Joryn answer with containment and boundaries rather than chaotic retaliation. Lucan is excluded from Evermere and Mark regains the crew's trust.

Act V — Nyssa Calder & the Network Ping

Nyssa, a Shah-trained paladin turned toward Luminara, arrives as a test of whether Evermere is still safe and independent. Shah tails connected to Marshal Kestrel Vane are identified and dismissed without escalation; a Luminara network ping confirms the wider network is still watching. The Lantern's answer is simple: safe, independent, not a cell. Nyssa becomes a frontline member and her relationship with Bren deepens.

Act VI — Durrel Ridge Re-enters Amora

A Durrel cousin, Caelan, arrives to reclaim a stolen Heartwarden relic. A professional shot attempts to destroy it mid-exchange; the Lantern secures it, traces the attackers to the residual Sable corridor, and escorts the relic to Durrel Ridge. There the party explores the Element Hall, Heart Vault, and Tarenus Durrel's tomb. The campaign generation interprets the Ridge material as evidence that Heartwarden stewardship historically coordinated several Seal traditions with Heart acting as a balancing axis. Later B16–B17 research narrows that interpretation: each Great Seal has its own Warden lineage, while Heart moderates rather than governs them. The relic reacts to lingering Shah consecration residue in Dain and Mark, loosening compulsive pressure without rewriting Mark's Shadow Sorcery. A mutual-aid pact forms between the Lantern and Ridge.

Act VII — Shadow Instability & the Umbral Collegium

Mark's Shadow power begins escalating involuntarily. After a dangerous flare at the Lantern he travels with Bren to Gloamford and the Umbral Collegium, training Darkness anchoring and dismissal, presence-based interrogation discipline, and control under distraction. He returns with real stable technique and a permanent subtle shadow-glow in his eyes.

Act VIII — The Ridge Leak

Durrel Ridge calls in the pact when a route leak starts getting couriers taken alive. The Lantern answers through a sting operation, uncovering patrol-tag manipulation, forged gatehouse logs, and a dead-drop niche inside the Element Hall. Clerk Iven Marr is caught altering records and Steward Alren Durrel exposed as the collector behind him, with Sable branding beneath both. Ridge justice strips Alren of authority; the pact is proven by action rather than promise.

Epilogue

Mark meets Darian secretly at their old waterfront rendezvous, confirming their relationship as an ongoing choice under pressure. The Lantern closes early for a controlled private celebration: Evermere is cleaner, the Ridge pact is real, and the youth squad has become competent and trusted.

World State After Campaign B7

Geography. Durrel Ridge becomes a living on-screen seat of the Durrel Heart tradition; the Umbral Collegium in Gloamford enters the world; the Lantern Annex expands Evermere's operational geography.

Magic. Mark's Shadow Sorcery is formally trained rather than suppressed. The Heart relic loosens coercive residue without overwriting his underlying magical identity. The campaign's wider Seal interpretation remains historically important but is later refined by B16–B17.

The Durrel Line. First major return of the Durrel name in Timeline B: Seren, Caelan, and Alren belong to a later surviving family branch, not descendants of Taren and Zethyr. The recurring name Taren is established as remembrance rather than prophecy.

Institutions. The Lantern and Durrel Ridge establish a real mutual-aid pact; the Umbral Collegium becomes a separate institutional model for magical training outside the Order of Anchors.

Canon status: follows the campaign events. Later understanding: B7 records what the characters then believed about Durrel Ridge and multi-Seal stewardship. B16–B17 later establish eight distinct Great Seals, each with its own Warden lineage, and Heart as moderator rather than sovereign or umbrella owner. Tarenus Durrel is securely established as a pre-Setting Heartbinder and the first permanent Heart watch after Heart was set; he did not invent Heartbinding itself. The B7 discoveries remain real — their institutional interpretation is what later research refines.
