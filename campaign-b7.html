Timeline B · Campaign B7

The Lantern Crew

Evermere Era

85 ARV Primary location: Evermere, Durrel Ridge

Core Premise

Roughly 80 years after Caen, Lio, and the execution of the Six in Campaign B5, and immediately following the founding of The Lantern in Campaign B6. A grounded, classic- adventuring campaign of civic cleanup and family drama, built around a new youth squad growing up in The Lantern's orbit.

Party & Key Figures

  • Mark "Mark Val" (Marcus Valmere) PC Alive; player character
    Human Shadow Sorcerer; exiled from a minor Valmere branch after a forbidden relationship with Darian Corvett, Dain's younger brother.
  • Youth Squad — Bren, Tomas, Edrin, Nyssa Alive; grow from rookies to trusted crew
    The Lantern's next generation, alongside Mark.
  • Dain ("Daeron") Corvett, Joryn, Maera, Cassian, Rafe Alive; running Evermere's civic operation
    Lantern leadership, carried forward from Campaigns B6.
  • Durrel Ridge — Seren, Caelan, Steward Alren, Clerk Iven Marr, Captain Haleven, Orren Vey Alive; standing allies

Act-by-Act

Mark Arrives

Mark reaches The Lantern under an alias, is tested and accepted by Maera, and begins ordinary jobs alongside Bren to earn the crew's trust — including learning not to panic-cast his Shadow magic under pressure.

Evermere Cleanup

The Lantern Crew commits to cutting off corrupt harbour chains and underworks smuggling. The approach becomes consistent: paper kills more than unsanctioned murders. The crew converts a warehouse into the Lantern Annex to grow without destabilising the public tavern.

Family Drama and Lucan's Return

Mark's exile story surfaces — he was to be married into Corvett politics while secretly loving Darian Corvett. Dain chooses to protect him rather than use him as leverage. Lucan, the antagonist from Campaign B6, resurfaces, targeting Mark with a manipulative "comfort ritual"; the crew intervenes with containment rather than violence, echoing the lesson B6 established about coercive dependency.

Durrel Ridge Re-enters Amora

A Durrel cousin, Caelan, arrives to reclaim a stolen Heartwarden relic. The Lantern secures it, escorts it to Durrel Ridge, and forms a mutual-aid pact between the two houses — the first time Timeline B's Durrel line (a later, separate branch from Taren and Zethyr, who were erased in Campaign B1) is directly named and given a location. Mark's Shadow power later flares out of control, sending him to the Umbral Collegium in Gloamford for formal training.

The Ridge Leak (Finale)

Durrel Ridge calls in the pact when a route leak starts getting couriers taken alive. The Lantern closes the leak through a sting, exposing corrupted Ridge staff and proving the pact real through action rather than promise.

World State After Campaign B7

Geography. Durrel Ridge is introduced for the first time in Timeline B — a distinct location from the Silver Bastion/Evermere axis the timeline has focused on so far. The Lantern Annex expands The Lantern's physical footprint in Evermere.

Magic. No major new magic system, though Mark's Shadow Sorcery being sent for formal training at the Umbral Collegium establishes that Timeline B has developed institutional magic education outside the Order of Anchors' resonance framework.

The Durrel Line. First appearance of the Durrel name in Timeline B: Seren, Caelan, and Steward Alren at Durrel Ridge. This is explicitly a later, separate branch of the family — not descendants of Taren and Zethyr, who were erased before they could have children in Campaign B1.

Institutions. Evermere is clean, the Durrel Ridge pact is proven active and callable, and the youth squad has become competent and trusted. Mark's relationship with Darian Corvett continues as an ongoing choice under family pressure — set up as a live thread for future campaigns.

Canon status: the Durrel Ridge cast (Seren, Caelan, Alren, and the founding Tarenus) appear as "Durell" in the source material — normalized to Durrel here per the site's spelling decision.
