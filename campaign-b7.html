Timeline B · Campaign B6

The Lantern and the Severance

Post-Campaign B5 (+80 years)

84–85 ARV Primary location: Greymount to Evermere

Core Premise

A tonal shift for Timeline B. What begins as a Luminara-aligned resistance investigation into Shah ritual influence in Greymount evolves into a deeply personal arc of autonomy, severance, and rooted independence.

Party & Key Figures

  • Dain Corvett
    Twilight Cleric leading a small Luminara resistance cell; survives a Shah saturation-rite conditioning arc and an attempted Ley-Heir overwrite.
  • Joryn
    Co-leader of the cell; stabilises Dain during the overwrite crisis and becomes his conscious partner.
  • Lucan
    Shah-aligned former lover of Dain; carries out the saturation rites that make him vulnerable to metaphysical manipulation.
  • Maera Holt
    Becomes permanent manager of The Lantern, the coastal tavern Dain purchases in Evermere.

Act-by-Act

Ritual and Fracture

Dain's resistance cell investigates Shah-aligned ritual saturation practices in Greymount. Lucan's repeated saturation rites on Dain function as slow conditioning mechanisms despite their intimate, voluntary form. Serio is exposed as an infiltrator and expelled.

The Ley-Heir Threshold

Dormant Ley-Heir architecture, legacy of Campaign B5, activates at a personal level around Dain and Lucan. The overwrite attempt fails — Dain's conscious resistance, Twilight's counter-field, and Joryn's grounding are enough to stop it.

Rooting Instead of Running

Rather than continuing as fugitives after a targeted killing raises exposure risk, the cell withdraws to Evermere. Dain purchases a coastal tavern, The Lantern, marking a shift from resistance cell to civic anchor.

House Corvett and House Valmere

Dain lets the truth of his exile — refusing an arranged political marriage — circulate openly rather than confronting his estranged noble family privately. House Corvett formally severs him; a maritime trade agreement with House Valmere is bound to the Evermere Harbor Authority rather than to Dain personally.

Final World State

B6 resolves without conquest or cataclysm: autonomy over inheritance, rooted belonging over reaction, partnership over domination. Evermere gains strengthened maritime autonomy and The Lantern is established as a stable anchor node. No war, no purge, no regime change — for once in Timeline B, nothing is collapsing.
