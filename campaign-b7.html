Timeline B · Campaign B7

The Lantern Crew

Chosen family over inherited cages, and the cost of maintaining independence

Core Premise

A grounded, classic-adventuring campaign of civic cleanup and family drama, built around a new youth squad growing up in The Lantern's orbit. Mark "Mark Val" (Marcus Valmere), a human Shadow Sorcerer, arrives in Evermere under an alias — exiled from a minor Valmere branch after a forbidden relationship with Darian Corvett, Dain's younger brother, and seeking refuge and purpose far from the shame and political blowback of home.

Party & Key Figures

  • Mark "Mark Val" (Marcus Valmere) PC Human Shadow Sorcerer; exiled from a minor Valmere branch after a forbidden relationship with Darian Corvett. His shadow magic develops from panicked reflex into a disciplined teamwork tool, and later into unstable flares that force him to seek formal training.
  • Youth Squad — Bren, Tomas, Edrin, Nyssa
    The Lantern's next generation, alongside Mark. Nyssa is a Shah-trained paladin who has turned toward Luminara; she and Bren develop into a couple over the course of the campaign.
  • Lantern Leadership — Dain ("Daeron") Corvett, Joryn, Maera, Cassian, Rafe
    Run Evermere's quiet-authority civic operation. Dain recognises troubling patterns in Mark's story — echoes of his own father's control tactics and the way Corvett/Valmere tensions get manipulated.
  • Durrel Ridge — Seren Durrel, Caelan Durrel, Steward Alren Durrel, Clerk Iven Marr, Captain Haleven, Orren Vey
    The Heartwarden seat that becomes a standing ally. (Source spells this family "Durell"; normalized to Durrel here per the site's spelling decision.)
  • Lucan, "Sable," Marshal Kestrel Vane
    The campaign's antagonist threads — Lucan returns and targets Mark directly; a residual countinghouse corridor operates under the "Sable" brand; Marshal Vane runs Shah-aligned tails that get identified and dismissed without escalation.

Act-by-Act

Act I — Mark Arrives / The Youth Squad Is Born

Mark reaches The Lantern under his alias, and Maera tests him personally — sees straight through the noble polish, and admits him to work under supervision anyway. Mark and Bren start as rookies, taking small jobs to earn the crew's trust: cellar and underworks problems, minor smugglers, dockside threats. Mark's Shadow magic becomes visible to the crew early, and he begins the slow work of learning not to panic-cast it. By the act's end, he's accepted as a youth squad member in his own right, not a guest being tolerated — and Maera is confirmed as the Lantern's real operational anchor and gatekeeper.

Act II — Evermere Cleanup

The Lantern Crew commits to stabilising Evermere itself, cutting off corrupt harbour chains and underworks smuggling routes. The youth squad clears out nesting creatures and smuggler-used under-cellar corridors while Cassian quietly builds an evidence trail behind them, delivering it upward through proper civic channels rather than going straight to violence — the approach that becomes the Lantern's signature: paper kills more than unsanctioned murders. The crew invests in infrastructure too, buying and converting the Lantern Annex — a warehouse turned bunkhouse, training space, and operations centre — letting the operation grow without destabilising the public tavern above it. By the act's end, Evermere's harbour is measurably safer, and the Lantern's reputation settles into "quiet authority," not a public militia.

Act III — Family Drama: the Valmere/Corvett Thread

Mark's true identity and the full circumstances of his exile surface among leadership. He confesses everything to Dain: he was meant to be married into Corvett politics through an arranged match with Dain's own sister — a match that, after Mark's exile, was set to pass to Mark's older brother instead — all while Mark secretly loved Darian Corvett. Dain absorbs the weight of that rather than treating it as leverage, recognising in it his own father's old control tactics and the way Corvett/Valmere tensions get manipulated by people above both families. House Corvett later cancels the sister's marriage entirely, giving Dain a rare, quiet satisfaction that his own exile indirectly spared her from being traded away the same way he was. Mark becomes family-adjacent to Dain and Joryn from here forward — trusted and protected, never owned. House Corvett remains a long-horizon threat regardless, and Darian stays a live pressure point.

Act IV — Lucan Returns

Lucan reappears in Evermere and targets Mark this time, not Darian — using charm, attention, and private rituals framed as comfort to position himself as relief for Mark's loneliness. Mark keeps the relationship secret at first, drawn in by the attention and structure it offers; Lucan attempts to introduce a conditioning-like "comfort ritual" designed to bind Mark to him emotionally and psychologically. Bren finds out before Dain does; eventually Mark confesses on his own. Dain and Joryn respond with containment and boundaries rather than chaotic violence — Lucan is warned off and denied access to Evermere outright, and Dain applies Twilight protection to Mark along with protocol-level safeguards going forward. Lucan is fully removed as an active vector in Evermere, and Mark regains the crew's trust once the truth is out.

Act V — Nyssa Calder & the Network Ping

Nyssa, a Shah-trained paladin who has turned toward Luminara, arrives as a quiet test from the wider world — is Evermere still safe, and has Dain quietly become a node again without meaning to. She's admitted cautiously and proves her reliability quickly. Shah tails connected to Marshal Kestrel Vane are identified and dismissed without escalation, and a single Luminara network "ping" confirms the wider network is still watching. The Lantern's answer is unambiguous: safe, independent, not a cell. Nyssa becomes a default frontline member of the squad, and her dynamic with Bren develops into something more.

Act VI — Durrel Ridge Re-enters Amora

A Durrel cousin, Caelan, arrives to reclaim a stolen Heartwarden relic — pulling ancient lineage directly into present-day civic reality. A professional shot tries to shatter the reliquary mid-exchange; the Lantern secures it, moves it to the Annex, and prioritises containment over confrontation, tracing the attackers back to a residual countinghouse corridor operating under the "Sable" name. The Lantern then escorts the relic to Durrel Ridge itself, where the party explores the Element Hall (a seal-linked stewardship tradition), the Heart Vault with its statues of the first Heartwardens, and Tarenus Durrel's own tomb — the founding Heartwarden and Heartbinder. New lore surfaces here: the Wardens have always safeguarded multiple seals, elemental ones plus the Heart as a balancing axis; the Durrel line keeps a tradition of recurring "Tarens" as a vow of remembrance rather than prophecy; and the last Taren in this timeline was murdered at Highever, with the house still watching for whoever bears the name next. The Heart Seal relic itself reacts to lingering Shah consecration residue in both Dain and Mark, loosening the sticky compulsion pressure and restoring some interior balance without touching or rewriting Mark's Shadow Sorcery identity. A mutual-aid pact is formed: the Lantern answers Durrel Ridge's calls, and Durrel Ridge answers the Lantern's.

Act VII — Shadow Instability & the Umbral Collegium

After the Ridge, Mark's Shadow power starts escalating involuntarily — darkness flaring well beyond his intent. He refuses help at first out of pride, but repeated incidents force his hand after a major flare inside the Lantern itself threatens crowd safety, with Dain only just managing to stabilise it in the moment. Mark travels with Bren to Gloamford, a new town, and the Umbral Collegium, a discreet school built around Null Hall containment. There he trains Darkness anchoring and dismissal, a presence-based interrogation discipline nicknamed "Tap & Leash," and control under active distraction and emotional provocation. He returns with real, stable discipline — and a permanent, subtle shadow-glow left behind in his eyes.

Act VIII — The Ridge Leak

Durrel Ridge calls in the pact for real: a route leak has started getting couriers taken alive. The Lantern answers to prove its word means something, and closes the leak through a sting operation. The investigation uncovers missing patrol-tag manipulation, forged after-the-fact gatehouse logs, and a dead-drop niche hidden inside the Element Hall itself. The sting catches Clerk Iven Marr as the hand actually altering records, and Steward Alren Durrel as the collector behind him, applying "stewardship" pressure under cover of his own office — with the residual countinghouse corridor's "Sable" branding running underneath both of them. Ridge justice strips Alren of his authority; the courier contact is contained; Cassian gets what he needs to cut the remaining paper links for good. The pact is proven real, not through promise, but through action.

Epilogue

Mark meets Darian secretly at their old waterfront rendezvous — "the same stone" — confirming their relationship as an ongoing choice made under real pressure, with Dain watching from a careful distance as brother and protector both. The Lantern closes early for a private, controlled celebration of survival: Evermere is clean, the Ridge pact is real, and the youth squad has become competent, cohesive, and genuinely trusted.

World State After Campaign B7

Geography. Durrel Ridge is fully established as a living Heartwarden seat with on-screen anchors — Seren, Caelan, the Element Hall, the Heart Vault — for the first time in Timeline B. The Umbral Collegium in Gloamford enters the world as a standing control and training location. The Lantern Annex expands The Lantern's physical footprint in Evermere.

Magic. Mark's Shadow Sorcery is formally trained and stabilised rather than suppressed, establishing that Timeline B has developed institutional magic education (the Umbral Collegium) entirely outside the Order of Anchors' resonance framework. The Heart Seal relic's reaction to Shah consecration residue in Dain and Mark confirms it can loosen coercive compulsion pressure without overwriting a person's underlying magical identity.

The Durrel Line. First appearance of the Durrel name in Timeline B: Seren, Caelan, and Steward Alren at Durrel Ridge. This is explicitly a later, separate branch of the family — not descendants of Taren and Zethyr, who were erased before they could have children in Campaign B1. New lore establishes the line keeps a tradition of recurring "Tarens" as vow and remembrance, not prophecy, and that the last Taren in this timeline was murdered at Highever, with the house still watching for whoever carries the name next — directly setting up Campaign B9.

Institutions. Evermere is clean, the Durrel Ridge pact is proven active and callable, and the youth squad has become competent and trusted. Mark's relationship with Darian Corvett continues as an ongoing choice under family pressure — set up as a live thread for future campaigns. The campaign closes at a Level 4 milestone: Bren shifts from Rogue 3 (Assassin) into Fighter 1 with Two-Weapon Fighting; Mark reaches Shadow Sorcerer 4 with trained control and his permanent shadow-glow eyes; Nyssa, Tomas, and Edrin all advance to Level 4 in their own classes.

Canon status: the Durrel Ridge cast (Seren, Caelan, Alren, and the founding Tarenus) appear as "Durell" in the source material — normalized to Durrel here per the site's spelling decision. Lucan's "comfort ritual" content follows the site's established PG-13 approach to coercion/consent themes — see the Canon Ledger.
