Timeline A · Campaign XIX

The Dominion Reborn: The Crown and the Anchor

—

8550–8555 ARV Primary location: Highever, Aderyn's Gate, Tarenus Reclaimed

Opening World State

The long calm after Concord: cities remember the Veil but run by habit. Legal and ritual civics from Campaigns XV-XVI have become background habit; the last heartline node sleeps beneath Highever's Sanctuary of Balance until a student dyad wakes it.

Party & Key Figures

  • Ravel Morn
    Taren echo and bearer of the Heart Crown; Purity initiator. Crown split at Severance; bonded to Thalen.
  • Thalen Ro
    Zethyr echo and the Anchor; paladin cadet who takes a Luminara fragment and triggers Severance.
  • Preceptor Senn
    Dissident Preceptor carrying a Luminara text/fragment; creates the dissonance that enables Severance.
  • Veyra Merenth
    Steward-clerk who reactivates doctrine tubes that let pipelines crown dyads by statute.

Act-by-Act

Cadence

A student bond rekindles the Heart Crown under Highever. Ravel pursues; Thalen accepts the first vow; the Sanctuary node re-lights.

The Purity Edicts

Highever links to Aderyn's Gate, then Tarenus Reclaimed, forming a three-city rhythm. Peace returns as Purity — order by subtraction — until dissonance is quietly erased and a "civil-engine" dyad is readied by statute.

Severance

Senn escapes the rhythm; Thalen secretly acquires a Luminara fragment. At the moment of final fusion, the Anchor lies to the Crown and invokes Severance at the peak — splitting the Crown and dissolving the network without war.

Final World State

The Veil-like network dissolves without open war; the Sanctuary floor bears two scorch silhouettes, one standing, one reaching. Highever keeps unnervingly precise time, and each night a faint Vault "off-beat" thump begins beneath Tarenus — the seed for Campaign XX.

Canon status: follows the character register directly.
