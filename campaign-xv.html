Timeline A · Campaign XV

Neon Concord: The Regent and the Voice

Third Cyberpunk Age

8210–8215 ARV Primary location: Highever, Inheritance Reclaimed (Tarenus)

Opening World State

Decades after the Cataclysm of the Freed Veil reshaped maps and institutions. After centuries oscillating between crown, creed, and clause, the city learns to keep order by proof on camera rather than prophecy or panic.

Party & Key Figures

  • Valen
    Aegis Knight turned Concord Regent, later Warden-Prime (penitent). Perimeter craft turns visibility into rule.
  • Dareth
    Rogue/fixer of the Red Lattice, elevated as Civic Consort & the Voice — the public ritual witness who makes consent visible.
  • Pela, Lys, Mara
    The Red Lattice's sanctuary architect and colleagues; family and protection network around Dareth.
  • Warden Kade, Advocate Rell, Vesc, Provost Varra
    City liaison, legalist, Old House counsel, and Vigil instructor navigating the new civic order.

Act-by-Act

The Black Ledger

A Verris Blackbook surfaces exposing Dripline payments and a House attempt to legalize remote audit copies. The Red Lattice faces a compliance squeeze; Dareth works rooms on camera while Valen holds the perimeter.

Card + Law + Aegis

The Concord Charter passes and badge unification aligns Wardens and Aegis. Valen becomes Regent de facto; Dareth is named Civic Consort & Voice as the Addendum formalizes proof-first ritual.

The Golden Knife & the Crown

An assassination attempt is beaten back on rooftops and canals. A plaza resonance test splits the crowd between Voice and Regent — Valen's cadence carries, and the Highever Crown is seized under shared registry by optics, not decree.

Neon Concord Finale

The Chorus-Key binds to the Voice alone; the plaza holds to law without a throne. Valen steps down, accepting lifetime penitent service as Warden-Prime emeritus. The Office of Regent collapses into procedure — Concord law survives.

Final World State

The Concord Charter and Addendum (Five Optics) become baseline municipal law. Warden-Prime is reframed as procedural service, not throne. Concord law becomes the standard for crowd safety and civic power — the idea of a Voice, someone whose consent can redirect law, enters canon.

Canon status: follows the character register; this campaign inaugurates the mature Third Cyberpunk Age.
