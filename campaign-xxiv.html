Timeline A · Campaign XXIII

The Quiet Recursion

4CE

9670–9680 ARV Primary location: East Vanmere, Vanmere city

Opening World State

A cyberpunk world, but quieter than the ages before it — more about the recursion of trauma and law than cinematic collapse. A flicker of will recorded a millennium earlier at Caelwick is about to wake again.

Party & Key Figures

  • Arden Vale
    Time-displaced rogue and shard-imprint survivor; former partner to Caelen, later partner of Rian Thale.
  • Caelen Marrith
    Paladin and shard-imprint primary axis; former partner of Arden. Deceased (tower incident) by the campaign's end.
  • Rian Thale
    Civic cartographer and stabiliser; helps Arden reclaim autonomy after Caelen's death.
  • Sen
    VANMERE first-contact responder; witness to the anomaly's emergence.

Act-by-Act

The Awakening of the Fault

Arden and Caelen materialise beneath East Vanmere, reinstantiated by the forgotten shard performing its only remaining instruction: restore the dyad whose will-pattern it once mirrored — axis and anchor.

Dominion Logic Without Dominion

4CE technology doesn't understand Dominion law, but it understands Dominion structure. Modern uniform-interfaces interpret the imprint as behavioural schema; Arden leans into Caelen's decisions, Caelen leans into control. The recursion grows.

The Abduction

Fearing the pattern taking shape, a rebel faction kidnaps Arden and holds him for seven days. Separated from Caelen, Arden confronts the truth of his conditioning — his devotion was genuine, but his dependency had been shaped.

The Spirefall

Unable to separate Arden from Caelen, the rebels strike Caelen instead — a single round kills him on the Grid-Spire balcony. Arden reaches him moments too late. The recursion ends; his grief does not.

Final World State

Arden survives and becomes the first documented Safe Anchor — a survivor of Dominion-derived behavioural logic who rebuilds autonomy through intentional partnership with Rian Thale. His experience is later formalised into the discipline of Anchorwork. He stands ready to mentor the next generation in Campaign XXIV.
