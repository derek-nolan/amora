Timeline A · Campaign XXIV

The Resonant Veil

4CE, Academy of Resonant Studies

9700–9720 ARV Primary location: Academy of Resonant Studies

Opening World State

Several years after the Shattered Quiet and the Quiet Recursion. With Caelen dead and the Dominion's last behavioural shard believed neutralised, the world thinks it's finally free of repeating patterns. It's wrong — resonance itself is evolving, and it no longer needs artefacts or dynastic spells to arise.

Party & Key Figures

  • Zeth Caden
    Resonance prodigy and first Living Dyad anchor; bonded to Talren Vale, mentee of Arden Vale.
  • Talren Vale
    Stabiliser trainee and grounding Living Dyad anchor; bonded to Zeth Caden.
  • Arden Vale
    Safe Anchor mentor and dyadic ethics architect; identifies Zeth's premature descent and formalises doctrine around the new dyad.
  • Ley-Shard Residue ("The Presence")
    The last fragment of the 4CE Quiet Fault behavioural shard line; latches onto Zeth's second-layer resonance before being dissolved.

Act-by-Act

First Pull

Zeth Caden, a new trainee with an unusually sensitive aura, meets Talren Vale. Their auras recognise each other before they do — Zeth's magic flares, Talren's composure cracks.

The Courtyard Explosion

A hidden ley-shard remnant latches onto Zeth mid-training, amplifying a burst of gold-white energy that endangers Talren and the Academy. Arden identifies it as the last fragment of the old shard logic.

A Controlled Dissolution

Rather than destroy the shard by force, Zeth draws it into a controlled second-layer fold and dissolves it as an act of kindness — ending active shard influence in this era for good.

Engagement and Fused Anchor

During an intense fold, Zeth proposes to Talren; Talren pulses his answer back through the bond. The Academy Council formalises them as the first Fused Dyad Anchors of the era and rewrites doctrine around them.

Final World State

Whatever remained of the shard logic that produced Caelen and the Quiet Recursion is gone — not by force, but by empathetic dissolution. Living Dyads become a recognised civic and magical structure: proof that two people can generate spell-equivalent power purely from emotional connection, consent, and practice. The world doesn't reset; it grows.
