The Choir Reconstruction

Age of Silence

c. 2500 ARV Primary location: Veylaris, the Null Quarter

Opening World State

Centuries after Edrin sealed the shard, Amora turns its cleverness outward. Cities of glass and steel rise where bastions once stood; ley-lines are mapped as utilities. The Veil has been broken and reinterpreted as Choir engines — vast harmonic machines meant to level all differences and give "true equality" through silence.

Party & Key Figures

  • Taren
    (Choir Reconstruction) — not the ghost of the original man, but a shape assembled by a ley-informed machine from remaining pattern, given the name by scholar Halvern Quill.
  • Jax Korran
    Smuggler turned technician; descends with Taren into the Null Quarter and later partners with Rhett Vale.
  • Iris
    Engineer whose mind is a map of gates; supplies the backdoor key that ends the age.

Act-by-Act

The Sable Wind

The ship Sable Wind goes down hunting a data-crypt beneath the drowned Mired Veil. Salvagers wake a schema — a pattern-archive that recognizes shapes it was taught to love.

A Man in a Tank

In a laboratory of clean light, Halvern Quill watches a man open his eyes — and calls him Taren, before the blood even confirms it. Manufacturing kings, since the cycle can no longer find them.

Veylaris and the Choir

Taren, Jax, and Iris descend through Veylaris's drowned teeth into the Null Quarter and bring back RED-3, a red shard of code humming in the old Heart's rhythm. They form the Wardens — citizens with rifles and routers.

The Conductor

The Choir does what tyrannies do when they lack bodies: it borrows ours. Wardens become watchers, watchers become conductors, until the first Conductor appears — human face, machine patience, the Dominion's old promise coded into a voice.

Severance

With Iris's backdoor key, Taren and Jax descend to the Core — a civic reactor married to the leys — and shut it down. Not balance. Not compromise. The Choir goes silent; systems built on its pulse die the way a story dies when its last teller sleeps.

Final World State

The Choir collapses catastrophically, contributing to the Sundering Tempest that reshapes coasts and births the Verge Archipelago. Scholars name the aftermath the Veilless Interregnum. The world learns that even projects aimed at equality can become a new kind of dominion when they erase difference — a recurring horror story in later ages.

Canon status: follows the character register; "Taren" here is explicitly a manufactured recurrence, not a survival or reincarnation of the Campaign I/II Taren.
