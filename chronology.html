Working canon

Master Chronology

ARV (the principal historical dating system) runs continuously from 0 ARV, the epoch both timelines share, through the Fracture. Campaign B17 pushed the dated record earlier still: the Ancient Chronology below uses negative ARV for material that predates 0 ARV entirely — a modern scholarly convenience Professor Halden applied when normalising an ancient calendar nobody living still used; people at the time didn't call their own dates negative ARV. "CE" means Cyberpunk Era, not Common Era. Amora's history isn't a straight climb toward technology — it swings between medieval and futuristic states more than once (Ages of Machines and Inheritance push toward cyberpunk infrastructure well before the Third Cyberpunk Age proper, which then collapses back into the Age of Disconnection). The Fourth Cyberpunk Era (4CE), reached via Campaign XXII's 1,000-year jump, is the last and longest-lasting of these swings.

Ancient Amora

Recovered in Campaign B17, this sequence predates Campaign I and B1 entirely — it belongs to neither timeline specifically, since both share the same history up to the moment they diverge at 0 ARV. The negative-ARV dates are a modern scholarly convenience; nobody at the time called their own dates negative ARV. Professor Halden cross-dated the ancient Ridge calendar against astronomical and historical anchors to produce this normalisation. Historical-interlude rows mark dated history with no played campaign behind it yet — a linked row leads to a full campaign account. See the Magic Codex for the Binder/House/Setting terminology this timeline establishes, and Tarenus Durrel for the individual at its centre.
