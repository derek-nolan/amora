Timeline A · Campaign IV

The Emperor Reforged

Age of Renewal

c. 1900–2000 ARV Primary location: The Confluence, rebuilt Silver Bastion

Opening World State

Long after Edrin: the world has tried republics and reforms, and old symbols are returning in new clothes. The Age of Renewal begins with a promise that Amora can recover the strength of empire without recovering its cruelty.

Party & Key Figures

  • Coren Veyne
    Veil-crowned ruler at the Confluence; rises on the promise of "order without cruelty."
  • Feran Durrel
    Heartbearer; explorer, guardian, and human counterweight to Coren.
  • Halvern Quill
    Collegium scholar and archivist; chronicles the era.
  • Selora
    Resistance-leaning scholar who counterbalances state power.

Act-by-Act

The Confluence Rises

Coren Veyne becomes the public face of a promise that old symbols can be made clean by better intentions. Feran Durrel stands beside him not as a mere consort but as an explorer and guardian, the human counterweight through which Coren tries to prove power and love can rebuild rather than consume.

Chronicle and Resistance

Halvern Quill chronicles the return of Silver Bastion and the strange convergence of old Heart and Veil structures, while Selora and other resistance-minded scholars watch the new order for signs of repetition. The rebuilt Evermere kingdom prospers; Highever becomes a pilgrimage ruin.

The Pattern Recognized

Coren seeks order without cruelty; Feran seeks discovery without surrendering his conscience. Their bond is pragmatic and deeply personal, but carries the old danger — Coren increasingly treats unity as proof of legitimacy, while Feran keeps hoping devotion can steer ambition.

Final World State

Coren's project half-succeeds: infrastructure and peace improve, but the pattern is recognisable — one will, many lives shaped around it. Later eras read him as a warning: even benevolent dominion can calcify into oppression. The Confluence of old symbols he assembles becomes raw material for later ages' experiments in law and magic.

Canon status: follows the character register directly; no unresolved names in this campaign.
