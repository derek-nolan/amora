Timeline A · Campaign IX

The Techno-Explorer

Age of Machines

c. 5950 ARV Primary location: The Ring of Amora

Opening World State

The world has turned toward ring-tech and proto-cyberpunk infrastructure; shards and leylines are packaged as engines and networks. The machine age begins with curiosity rather than conquest.

Party & Key Figures

  • Rhett Vale
    Explorer, rogue, and tinkerer; co-founds the modern Wardens. Partner to Jax Korran.
  • Jax Korran
    Smuggler and Warden operative; brings field instincts to dangers scholars keep trying to classify.
  • Iris
    Engineer and Choir interface; understands every interface as also a gate through which the past might act.

Act-by-Act

Into the Ring

Rhett Vale enters the Ring of Amora as an explorer, accompanied by Jax and Iris. Commercial sponsors want usable discoveries; machine cults want revelation; the expedition itself wants to know what the old world built beneath its ruins.

A Machine That Alters Memory

The Ring doesn't merely preserve information — it alters memory, causality, and the stories people tell about themselves. Rhett's pursuit of truth puts him in direct conflict with those who believe engineered certainty is more useful than human doubt.

Accelerating the Future

Their discoveries accelerate the rebirth of Veylaris and the return of Choir-derived infrastructure. Rhett's journals become foundational texts, warning again and again that a mechanism able to reveal truth can also decide which truth survives.

Final World State

Rhett's journals become scripture for later technocrats and reformers. The machine age accelerates, laying foundations for later cyberpunk eras and the Concord systems — and the sense that "truth" can be engineered becomes a spectre hanging over future civic orders.
