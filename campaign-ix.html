Timeline A · Campaign IX

The Techno-Explorer

Age of Machines — "Through Discovery, Truth"; First Cyberpunk Era begins

c. 5950 ARV Primary location: The Ring of Amora

Opening World State

The world turns decisively toward ring-tech and proto-cyberpunk infrastructure; shards and leylines start getting packaged as engines and networks rather than studied as mysteries. Rhett Vale, an explorer and tinkerer by temperament, ventures into the Ring of Amora chasing exactly the kind of discovery this new age is hungry for.

Party & Key Figures

  • Rhett Vale PC Explorer and tinkerer; motto "Through Discovery, Truth." Co-founds the modern Wardens after his expedition into the Ring.
  • Expedition crew, machine cults, commercial sponsors
    The full range of interests funding and complicating Rhett's dive into dangerous innovation.

Act-by-Act

The Dive

Rhett's expedition into the Ring of Amora frames the whole campaign as a dive into genuinely dangerous innovation. The party navigates ley-mechanisms, lingering Choir remnants, and commercial interests funding the exploration for their own reasons — not all of them aligned with Rhett's.

Truth Rewritten

What the party ultimately uncovers is unsettling: the Ring doesn't just reveal truth the way Rhett expected it to. It actively rewrites it, altering memory and even causality in ways nobody fully anticipated going in.

Scripture for Technocrats

Rhett's own journals from the expedition become something like scripture for later technocrats and reformers. The machine age accelerates hard in the wake of his discoveries, laying the direct foundations for later cyberpunk eras and, eventually, Concord's own systems.

World State After Campaign IX

Geography. The Ring of Amora is established as a major site of dangerous, reality-altering exploration.

Magic/Tech. Ley-mechanisms and Choir remnants are actively repurposed into ring-tech infrastructure here for the first time — proto-cyberpunk systems that later ages build directly on top of. This is the true opening of what the site's Chronology tracks as the First Cyberpunk Era, running through Campaign XI.

The Durrel Line. No Durrel figures appear this campaign.

Institutions. The sense that "truth" itself can be engineered becomes a spectre that hangs over every civic order for the rest of the timeline — directly informing the intense proof-first, receipts-over-rumour civic architecture that Campaigns XV and XVI later build specifically to guard against it.
