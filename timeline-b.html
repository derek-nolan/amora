Arc IV · Campaigns B16–B17

The Great Seal Arc

A new historical phase, roughly 180 years after the Ridge Arc closes — and a new named age. The Age of Founding, Reforged ends with the Ridge Arc in late c. 162 ARV; B16 opens the Age of Rediscovery, a historiographical shift rather than a technological one. Moves away from the immediate Light-Veil struggles of the Ridge/Brevair era and toward the Great Seals themselves, their individual Warden lineages, and the deep history of the Oath Wars and the War of Ash and Glass — opening with Nicholas Soren's discovery of a forgotten, unfallen Western branch of the Icewardens, and closing (for now) with B17's recovery of how the Great Seals themselves were actually set.
