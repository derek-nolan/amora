Timeline B · Campaign B3 / XXVIII

The Frostborn Inheritance

Age of Founding, Reforged

1–2 ARV Primary location: Silver Bastion, Vyrn's Spring

Core Premise

Weeks after the Hollowrest expedition, unnatural frost begins forming inside Caen and Lio's private quarters — clean geometric sigils unlike the chaotic Hollowrest anomalies. The frost responds not to Lio, but to Kellan, revealing a truth erased in Timeline A entirely: Kellan is of the Frostborn, a sub-line of Icebinder magic predating the Seals.

Party & Key Figures

  • Lio Serrin Alive; Anchor
    First Anchor of Timeline B.
  • Caen (Kellan) Vance Alive; Thread; revealed Frostborn
    First Stabiliser Thread; revealed as a surviving Frostborn descendant. This is the campaign where the source material's own naming shifts from Kellan to Caen.
  • The Six Travellers Alive
    Arden, Rian, Zeth, Talren, Solan, Marrek — act as stabilisers and witnesses.
  • Seraphine Vale Alive
    High Paladin of Silver Bastion; guides the political and historical integration of the lost Binder line.

Act-by-Act

The Frost-Sigil in Silver Bastion

Frost appears overnight in Caen and Lio's quarters, forming deliberate patterns. Resonance tests confirm Caen is the source, not Lio — a direct inversion of the expected dynamic, since Lio is the Anchor and would normally be assumed the source of any anomaly. A dormant Binder door opens beneath their bedchamber, revealing the Frostborn Vault.

The Road North & Vyrn's Spring

Frostborn sigils lead the party into the northern highlands to Vyrn's Spring, a hidden enclave of surviving Frostborn descendants — proof the Icebinder diaspora identified in Campaign B2 has living remnants, not just ruins. The settlement recognises Caen's lineage instantly, offering history and ancestral memory long lost to the Mind's predations back in Campaign B1.

Recognition & Return

Caen formally claims his Frostborn heritage with Lio as his stabilising partner. The Spring entrusts him with ancestral sigils and a watcher-stone, forming the first stable Bastion-Frostborn diplomatic link in history — something Timeline A never achieved in any of its nine millennia.

World State After Campaign B3

Geography. Silver Bastion gains the Frostborn Vault beneath the Anchor quarters. Vyrn's Spring is confirmed as a hidden, living Frostborn enclave in the northern highlands.

Magic. The Frostborn are confirmed as a third distinct magical lineage alongside the Heart/Anchor line Lio carries and the proto-Binder line from Hollowrest — Seraphine updates Order doctrine to state that Binder lines may awaken through living inheritance, not relics alone. This directly complicates the simple Anchor/Thread model established in Campaign B1.

The Durrel Line. Still absent — but this campaign establishes that Timeline B's magic system is developing multiple independent lineages (Heartborn, Stabiliser Thread, Frostborn) rather than everything routing back through a single Durrel-descended mechanism the way Timeline A's Ley-Heirs did.

Institutions. Silver Bastion becomes the first tri-lineage centre in Timeline B — housing Heartborn (Lio), Stabiliser Thread (Caen), and now Frostborn heritage all in one institution. This is also the campaign where the source material's own naming convention shifts permanently from "Kellan" to "Caen."

Canon status: follows the character register directly; this campaign is where the Kellan → Caen renaming becomes explicit in the source material.
