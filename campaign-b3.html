Core Premise

Weeks after the Hollowrest expedition, unnatural frost begins forming inside Caen and Lio's private quarters at Silver Bastion — clean geometric sigils, entirely unlike Hollowrest's chaotic environmental bleed. Until this point Caen was considered a senior paladin and stabiliser-thread with no recorded lineage of magical inheritance at all. Resonance testing changes that overnight: Lio's field produces no reaction whatsoever, while Caen's produces an immediate frost-response, marking him and only him the source. The frost reveals a truth erased in Timeline A entirely — Caen is Frostborn, a surviving Icewarden steward-line (not the fallen Icebinder branch), custodian of early Binder magic that predates the Seals themselves.

Act-by-Act

Act I — The Frost-Sigil in Silver Bastion

Frost appears overnight in Caen and Lio's quarters, forming deliberate geometric patterns rather than the chaotic frost-bleed Hollowrest produced. Resonance tests confirm the sigils react only to Caen's emotional pulses and stay completely inert to Lio or anyone else. A dormant Binder door beneath their bedchamber opens, revealing the Frostborn Vault — sealed since the Bastion's earliest construction. Caen triggers ancestral sigils that identify him specifically as a "returning steward," and the Order recognises immediately that this is lineage-level magic, not an anomaly to be contained: the emergence of a second Binder lineage in Timeline B.

Act II — The Road North & Vyrn's Spring

Frostborn sigils lead the party into the northern highlands, with old Binder markers responding to Caen alone and forming a path toward the mountains. The Six travel as stabilisers throughout, making sure Caen isn't overwhelmed by resonance he's inheriting faster than he can process it. They reach Vyrn's Spring, a hidden enclave of surviving Frostborn descendants — proof the Icewarden diaspora identified back in Campaign B2 has living remnants, not just ruins. The settlement recognises Caen's lineage instantly, offering him history, training, and ancestral memory long lost to the Mind's predations. There, Caen performs the Frostborn Imprint Rite, anchoring his lineage safely within the timeline's contemporary resonance paradigm rather than letting it destabilise him.

Act III — Recognition & Return

Caen formally claims his Frostborn heritage with Lio as his stabilising partner, choosing integration over isolation. The Spring entrusts him with ancestral sigils and a watcher-stone; Caen leaves one Sending Stone behind and carries its twin back with him, forming the first stable Bastion-Frostborn diplomatic link in history — something Timeline A never achieved across any of its nine millennia. Back at Silver Bastion, the Frostborn Vault fully accepts Caen's imprint, and Seraphine formally updates Order doctrine: Binder lines may awaken through living inheritance, not relics alone. Caen and Lio settle back into quarters now layered, harmlessly, with Frostborn geometry woven directly into the existing Anchor wards.

World State After Campaign B3

Geography. Vyrn's Spring is added to the Atlas as a hidden ancestral enclave. Silver Bastion gains a sealed Frostborn Annex beneath the Anchor Quarters, tied exclusively to Caen's access — Lio only with Caen present, Seraphine only under emergency override.

Magic. The Frostborn are confirmed as a third distinct magical lineage in Timeline B, alongside the Heart line Lio carries and the proto-Binder line from Hollowrest — Binders are now formally classified as Heartbinders (the Durrel line, its fate sealed in B1), Flamebinders (Arienna's lineage, known only as historical precedent), and Icewarden/Frostborn (Caen's reawakened line — distinct from the fallen Icebinder branch). Three new mechanics emerge directly from this campaign: Frostborn Line Recognition (a resonance test requiring genuine emotional sincerity — false intent collapses the sigil flow outright, and it currently works only on Caen), Resonance-Frost Synergy (Caen can stabilise frost constructs instinctively, though untrained spikes risk involuntary frost-surges), and Lineage Pulse Reading (sending controlled cold-pulses to interpret Binder sigils directly, first demonstrated during the vault investigation itself).

The Durrel Line. Still absent from the narrative directly — but this campaign establishes that Timeline B's magic system is developing multiple independent lineages (Heartborn, Stabiliser Thread, Frostborn) rather than everything routing back through a single Durrel-descended mechanism the way Timeline A's Ley-Heirs did.

Institutions. Silver Bastion becomes the first tri-lineage centre in Timeline B — housing Heartborn (Lio), Stabiliser Thread (Caen), and now Frostborn heritage all in one institution. The Order of Anchors formally expands its mandate to include cataloguing Binder bloodlines, supporting stabiliser-lineage hybrids beginning with Caen, and integrating Frostborn architecture into the citadel's own records. Highever deliberately keeps the discovery contained for now — there's no external political ripple yet. This is also the campaign where the source material's own naming convention shifts permanently from "Kellan" to "Caen."

Canon status: follows the source account directly; this campaign is where the Kellan → Caen renaming becomes explicit in the source material.
