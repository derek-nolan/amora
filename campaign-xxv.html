Timeline A · Campaign XXV

The Broken Anchor

Fracture → Temporal Reboot

9750–9800 ARV Primary location: Academy of Resonant Studies → Silver Bastion, Age of Founding

Opening World State

Twelve years into an age convinced the Severance, the Quiet Recursion, and the Resonant Veil had finally stabilised magic into ethical resonance. That illusion is about to end. Historians agree that Campaign XXV marks the end of Amora's first continuum — Timeline A — and the birth of a new world. Resonance anomalies beneath the Academy of Resonant Studies are dismissed as background noise — until Arden recognises the signature.

Act-by-Act

The Fracture Beneath the Academy

What scholars now call the Marrek-Thread Event began as resonance noise detected near the Academy's lower sigil arrays. Pulse recordings show Dominion-era voice command structures, Widow-era coercive cadence patterns, Neon-age uniform logic, and a distinct behavioural imprint — Caelen's — interlaced with temporal displacement signatures. Arden identifies it immediately, though no one believes him until Solan momentarily vanishes from the present and appears, consciously, before a Dominion-era Marrek, who recognises him as if they shared a life. The truth emerges: the fracture is targeting Marrek across all ages.

The Caelen Echo

The echo isn't Caelen resurrected — it's worse. Dominion hunger, Widow conditioning scars, Neon circuitry feedback, and 4CE behavioural architecture, all fused into a single, collapsing will. It wants Marrek. It needs a stabiliser. It seeks a bond. Solan's refusal — his instinctive act of stepping between them — breaks the echo's logic more effectively than any spell.

The Six-Point Resonance Formation

This era gives the world its most unusual magical discovery: a six-person anchor formation, balanced around Solan, stabilised by Marrek, and reinforced by Zeth, Talren, Arden, and Rian. This formation allows Arden to deploy a medieval-era Severance Rite meant to cut command-logic from living souls. The echo collapses. The fracture roars open.

Temporal Collapse

The unity seal they perform on the dying shard produces a full seal for one heartbeat, catastrophic overload on the next, a resonance inversion on the third, and total temporal implosion on the fourth. All six are expelled from the fracture and arrive — physically, alive — in the Age of Founding, at the training grounds of Silver Bastion. Chrono-forensics confirm this is not illusion, echo, or recursion. It is relocation. Timeline A is sealed behind them. Timeline B begins the moment they open their eyes.

World State After Campaign XXV

Geography. The Academy of Resonant Studies is the last location of Timeline A proper. The six arrive at Silver Bastion during the training years of Taren and Zethyr Durrel — the exact starting point of Campaign I, now revisited from the far end of the timeline.

Magic. This is the eighth and final major Severance-adjacent event of Timeline A, and the only one to cause a full temporal collapse rather than simply ending a magical structure. The Caelen line is permanently concluded — no shard, no imprint, no behaviour-engine, no command-logic residue survives it. The six carry resonance theory itself into the world's beginning, seeding Timeline B's entire magic system before it's even invented independently.

The Durrel Line. Timeline A ends: the events of Campaigns I–XXIV form a complete historical continuum now sealed by the fracture correction — real, but existing in a closed temporal layer. Timeline B begins the moment before the rise of the Heartwardens and the Dominion: Silver Bastion breathes, and Zethyr and Taren are teenagers again, this time with six outsiders from the future standing at the beginning of their story.

Institutions. The consequences are unknowable. Arden, Rian, Zeth, Talren, Solan, and Marrek are now part of the foundational era — see Campaign B1 for what happens next.

Canon status: follows the character register directly. This is the hinge point of the entire chronicle — see Timeline B, Campaign B1, for what happens next.
