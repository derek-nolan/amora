Timeline A · Campaign XXV

The Broken Anchor

Fracture → Temporal Reboot

9750–9800 ARV Primary location: Academy of Resonant Studies → Silver Bastion, Age of Founding

Opening World State

In the years after the Resonant Veil, the world believes it finally understands resonance: dyads are stable, behavioural magic is ethical, shards are gone, history is linear again. This campaign proves all of that wrong. Deep beneath the 4CE Academy, a temporal fracture begins manifesting threads of Dominion-era patterning — half echo, half imprint — pulling individual lives across centuries. Every anomaly points to one origin: the behavioural shard-core Caelen Marrith carried into death, its echo clinging to the fracture like broken code.

Party & Key Figures

  • Solan PC Emergent anchor; carried through to the Age of Founding
    An emergent anchor whose instincts are strong but untrained — naturally stabilises others' emotional resonance, unusually resistant to fracture targeting, and forms a tether with Marrek strong enough to warp temporal geometry. Becomes the "centre" of a six-person resonance formation. Carries the story's moral axis: choice, not destiny.
  • Marrek
    Stabiliser whose thread the fracture hunts across every era — protector, grounding force, emotional centre for Solan. His presence becomes the fracture's target; his bond with Solan becomes its downfall.
  • Arden Vale
    First Safe Anchor of 4CE; survivor of Dominion behavioural recursion. Mentor to the group — his history with Caelen gives him unique insight into the echo's architecture. Leads the final severance spell.
  • Rian Thale
    Civic cartographer; Arden's counterbalance. Arrives voluntarily to support Arden through the encounter with Caelen — the emotional ballast that keeps him from slipping back into old patterns when the echo manifests.
  • Zeth Caden & Talren Vale
    The first recognised Living Dyad (Campaign XXIV). Their fused-anchor discipline allows the group to maintain formation in the fracture — proof that magic succeeds where machinery failed.
  • Caelen's Echo
    Not a ghost, not alive — a behavioural shard-core held together by Dominion logic, Widow-era conditioning scars, and Neon-era circuitry. Unstable, hungry, singularly focused on reclaiming Marrek's thread. Destroyed.

Act-by-Act

Act I — Arrival & Resonance Instability

Solan and Marrek meet at the 4CE Academy. Instant pull; uncontrolled resonance flares; dyad-like behaviour without an established bond.

Act II — First Temporal Slip

Solan briefly falls into a Dominion-era shard line. A Dominion-era Marrek recognises him from that era, confirming the fracture is targeting Marrek across all ages.

Act III — The Fracture Tests

Arden runs containment rituals; fracture pulses intensify. The group learns Caelen's behavioural shard-core is alive inside the anomaly.

Act IV — The Dyadic Anchor Formation

Solan instinctively forms a geometric stance that stabilises all five allies. The fracture begins responding to Solan's geometry, not Caelen's.

Act V — The Caelen Echo Appears

The echo manifests as a Dominion-Widow-Neon composite and targets Marrek. Solan steps physically between them — a subtle gesture that destabilises the entire echo.

Act VI — Severance

At Solan's command, Arden executes a medieval-era Severance Rite — a sigil cadence predating Dominion magic — that exposes the core. Caelen's echo collapses.

Act VII — The Six-Person Seal

Solan (centre), Marrek, Zeth, Talren, Rian, and Arden perform a unity seal on the dying shard fragment together. The seal works — but the multi-thread resonance overwhelms the fracture.

Act VIII — The Temporal Collapse

The mirrored plane shatters; light implodes inward. All six are thrown through the fracture — not as echoes, but physically.

Act IX — Reboot to the Age of Founding

They awaken at Silver Bastion, Highever, and hear Taren and Zethyr training. They realise the future is gone, the cycle reset, and they are alive at the beginning of Amora's history.

World State After Campaign XXV

Geography. The Academy of Resonant Studies is the last location of Timeline A proper. The six arrive at Silver Bastion during the training years of Taren and Zethyr Durrel — the exact starting point of Campaign I, now revisited from the far end of the timeline.

Magic. Caelen's echo is fully destroyed — no shard residue, no behavioural imprint, no further Dominion recursion possible. This is the final Severance-adjacent event of Timeline A, and the only one to cause a full temporal collapse rather than simply ending a magical structure. The Age of Founding has no resonance vocabulary; the six must rebuild magic from nothing, seeding Timeline B's entire system before it's independently invented there.

The Durrel Line. Timeline A ends: Campaigns I–XXIV form a complete historical continuum, now sealed in a closed temporal layer as "Timeline A — The Old Cycle." A new timeline begins the moment the six open their eyes: Taren and Zethyr exist as teenagers, unaware of their future roles, and this becomes Timeline B — The Reforged Cycle.

Institutions. The six become temporal refugees — identity, magic adaptation, and survival become the next age's concerns. See Campaign B1 for what happens next.

Canon status: follows the source account directly. This is the hinge point of the entire chronicle — see Timeline B, Campaign B1, for what happens next.
