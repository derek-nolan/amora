Timeline A · Campaign XXV

The Broken Anchor

Fracture → Temporal Reboot

9750–9800 ARV Primary location: Academy of Resonant Studies → Silver Bastion, Age of Founding

Opening World State

In the years since the Resonant Veil, the Academy believes it's finally figured resonance out: dyads are stable, behavioural magic is ethical, the old shards are gone, and history is finally moving in a straight line again. This campaign proves every part of that wrong, one piece at a time. Deep beneath the Academy of Resonant Studies, a temporal fracture begins throwing off threads of Dominion-era patterning — half echo, half imprint, neither one fully — and pulling individual lives across centuries in ways nobody has instruments to properly measure. Every anomaly traces back to a single origin point: the behavioural shard-core Caelen Marrith carried with him into death back in Campaign XXIII, its echo still clinging to the fracture like broken code that never got a chance to finish executing.

Party & Key Figures

  • Solan PC Emergent anchor; carried through to the Age of Founding
    An emergent anchor whose instincts are strong but untrained — naturally stabilises others' emotional resonance, unusually resistant to fracture targeting, and forms a tether with Marrek strong enough to warp temporal geometry. Becomes the "centre" of a six-person resonance formation. Carries the story's moral axis: choice, not destiny.
  • Marrek
    Stabiliser whose thread the fracture hunts across every era — protector, grounding force, emotional centre for Solan. His presence becomes the fracture's target; his bond with Solan becomes its downfall.
  • Arden Vale
    First Safe Anchor of 4CE; survivor of Dominion behavioural recursion. Mentor to the group — his history with Caelen gives him unique insight into the echo's architecture. Leads the final severance spell.
  • Rian Thale
    Civic cartographer; Arden's counterbalance. Arrives voluntarily to support Arden through the encounter with Caelen — the emotional ballast that keeps him from slipping back into old patterns when the echo manifests.
  • Zeth Caden & Talren Vale
    The first recognised Living Dyad (Campaign XXIV). Their fused-anchor discipline allows the group to maintain formation in the fracture — proof that magic succeeds where machinery failed.
  • Caelen's Echo
    Not a ghost, not alive — a behavioural shard-core held together by Dominion logic, Widow-era conditioning scars, and Neon-era circuitry. Unstable, hungry, singularly focused on reclaiming Marrek's thread. Destroyed.

Act-by-Act

Act I — Arrival & Resonance Instability

Solan and Marrek meet as trainees at the 4CE Academy, and the pull between them is immediate — not romantic exactly, or not only that, but a resonance draw so strong that neither of them can keep their pulses steady for the first several sessions they share. Dyad-like behaviour surfaces between them before either has consciously chosen a bond, which is, on its own, already unusual enough to draw instructor attention.

Act II — First Temporal Slip

Without warning, Solan slips — briefly, disorientingly — into what reads on instruments as a genuine Dominion-era shard line, not a vision or a projection but contact. On the other side, a Dominion-era version of Marrek recognises him instantly, by name, with no hesitation at all. It's the first hard confirmation that whatever this fracture wants, it's been hunting Marrek specifically, across every era it can reach.

Act III — The Fracture Tests

Arden runs formal containment rituals, borrowing methods he half-remembers from his own Quiet Recursion. Every attempt intensifies the fracture's pulses instead of calming them. What the group confirms, piece by piece, is the worst possible answer: Caelen's behavioural shard-core didn't die with him. It's alive inside the anomaly, and it's aware enough to want something.

Act IV — The Dyadic Anchor Formation

Under pressure, Solan does something nobody taught him — instinctively arranging the group into a geometric stance that stabilises all five of the others at once. The fracture's behaviour visibly shifts in response, no longer orienting around Caelen's old signature but around Solan's new one. For the first time since the fracture opened, the group has the advantage.

Act V — The Caelen Echo Appears

The echo manifests fully — a composite thing, part Dominion command logic, part Widow-era conditioning scar, part Neon-circuitry reflex, none of it quite Caelen and all of it recognisably him. It goes straight for Marrek. Solan doesn't hesitate; he steps bodily between them, a small and almost unremarkable gesture that, against a pattern built entirely around targeting one specific thread, destabilises the whole echo at once.

Act VI — Severance

On Solan's single-word command, Arden executes a medieval-era Severance Rite — a sigil cadence that predates Dominion magic entirely, older than anything the echo's composite logic was ever built to resist. The rite exposes the shard-core directly, and Caelen's echo collapses in on itself.

Act VII — The Six-Person Seal

With the core exposed, all six — Solan at the centre, Marrek on the right flank, Zeth as front-left anchor-light, Talren as rear-left blade, Rian holding the group's emotional line, Arden casting the seal itself — perform a unity rite on the dying fragment together. The seal works exactly as intended. What nobody accounts for is what six simultaneous resonance threads do to a fracture already destabilised past the point of safe containment.

Act VIII — The Temporal Collapse

The mirrored plane the fracture had been operating inside shatters outright. Light collapses inward instead of out. All six of them are thrown through what's left of the fracture — not as echoes, not as projections, but bodily, all at once.

Act IX — Reboot to the Age of Founding

They are thrown bodily into Silver Bastion in Highever, during the training years of Taren and Zethyr Durrel. The older XXV dossier remembers the endpoint as the Bastion's training grounds; the later, played opening of Timeline B fixes the six travellers' arrival more precisely in the Heart chamber during early rites, with Taren and Zethyr still paladin candidates. The stable fact across both records is physical relocation to Silver Bastion before the events that once became Campaign I — not a return to the beginning of Amora's much older history.

World State After Campaign XXV

Geography. The Academy of Resonant Studies is Timeline A's final 4CE theatre. The six are displaced to Silver Bastion during the training years of Taren and Zethyr Durrel, before the events later recorded as Campaign I. The exact landing point is source-layered: the older XXV dossier says the training grounds, while later Timeline B play places their arrival in the Heart chamber and therefore takes priority for the precise location.

Magic. Caelen's echo is destroyed completely — no shard residue, no behavioural imprint, no possibility of further Dominion recursion. This is the final Severance-adjacent event of Timeline A, and the only one to cause a full temporal collapse rather than simply ending a magical structure. The Age of Founding has no resonance vocabulary at all; the six will have to rebuild the entire discipline from nothing, effectively seeding Timeline B's magic system centuries before it would otherwise have been independently invented there.

The Durrel Line. Campaigns I through XXIV form the pre-fracture historical continuum the six leave behind; Campaign XXV is itself the final Timeline A campaign, the hinge that closes that continuum and carries the six into Timeline B. Taren and Zethyr are still young paladin candidates, unaware of the roles the Old Cycle once gave them. Timeline B begins with their future no longer guaranteed.

Institutions. The six become temporal refugees overnight — identity, magic adaptation, and sheer survival become the concerns that carry directly into what comes next. Their resonance vocabulary and techniques enter an era that has none of them. See Campaign B1 for the later played account of their arrival and the first five days of the Reforged Cycle.

Canon status: Campaign XXV remains the final Timeline A campaign and the hinge into Timeline B. The dedicated XXV dossier establishes physical displacement to Silver Bastion during Taren and Zethyr's training years; later Campaign B1 play fixes the precise arrival in the Heart chamber rather than the older dossier's training-ground image, so B1 takes priority on that detail. See Timeline B, Campaign B1, for the played continuation.
