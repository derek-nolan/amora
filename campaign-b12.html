Timeline B · Campaign B12

The Warden of Meaning

Durrel Ridge Era

150 ARV Primary location: Durrel Ridge, Tarenus's Vault

Core Premise

Jack Durrel returns to the Ridge in the aftermath of Marcus's death — no longer trapped beneath the threat that shaped his recent life, but not yet fully restored to ease. With Silver Bastion lost to Cameron in Campaign B11, Durrel Ridge's importance as a Light stronghold has never been higher. The campaign opens not with a fortress assault but with return: can Jack truly inhabit his own inheritance rather than merely stand near it?

Party & Key Figures

  • Jack Durrel Alive; named Warden of Meaning
    Durrel heir, Ridge operative, and survivor of Marcus's shadow. The player character.
  • Taren Durrel Alive; Ridge leadership, carried from Campaign B9
    His distance from Jack begins to close once Marcus is confirmed dead.
  • Jorah Alive; confirmed real by the Vault revelation
    Arrives during the campaign and is gradually allowed into Jack's private orbit.
  • Marek & Dena Alive; Jack's core circle
    Dena becomes a key emotional anchor and continuity figure for the household.
  • Marcus Confirmed dead in the true line
    Dead before the campaign's present, but structurally important as the road Jack's life might have taken.

Act-by-Act

The House Reopens

Jack reopens his inherited house at the Ridge, offering it as a shared home to his team. What could have remained a private mausoleum becomes a communal house — the campaign's first answer to everything Timeline B has lost so far: home must be reclaimed in truth before it can be defended in war.

Scouting and Household Life

The Ridge functions as both resistance centre and lived household. Jorah becomes central not through spectacle, but through the ordinary: meals, rooms, jokes, trust, and the possibility of chosen intimacy that doesn't feel like manipulation — a deliberate contrast to the coercive dynamics that defined Campaigns B10 and B11.

Marcus's Road Closes

Marcus's death is confirmed in the true line, breaking the old route of manipulation and fear. Jorah can exist in Jack's life without being overshadowed, and Taren's distance from Jack begins to lose its old justification.

Tarenus's Vault

Jack descends into the Vault with Jorah and is shown a true possible future: Marcus surviving, taking Jorah's place, and corrupting Jack through home and false intimacy. Tarenus — the same Vault entity Taren Durrel reclaimed back in Campaign B9 — confirms Marcus's death and Jorah's reality, then names Jack a Warden of Meaning: heir to the Durrel line's power to name and defend truth inside bonds.

Taren and Jack Align

Jack tells Taren the truth of the Vault vision. Taren finally stops treating Jack as a lingering risk and starts treating him as vital to the mission. A surviving seven-year-old cadet-branch Durrel child is brought to the Ridge, and the household becomes visibly the place where the next generation will be raised.

World State After Campaign B12

Geography. Durrel Ridge re-centres as command-centre, lived household, and the most plausible surviving Light stronghold after Bastion's fall in Campaign B11 — its importance has grown directly proportional to what's been lost elsewhere.

Magic. Tarenus's Vault demonstrates a new capability — showing true possible futures, not just responding to bloodline and witness as it did in Campaign B9. This expands what's understood about the Vault's nature significantly.

The Durrel Line. Jack Durrel is named Warden of Meaning — a title distinct from Taren's Warden role established in Campaign B9, suggesting the Durrel line is developing multiple specialised roles rather than a single hereditary office. The cadet-branch Durrel child brought to the Ridge at the campaign's end represents the next generation directly.

Institutions. Taren inherits the Ridge and Warden-seat; Jack inherits the truth-knife inside corrupted bonds — their roles no longer compete. The arrival of the cadet-branch child locks in the next generational handoff: the Ridge is now raising what comes next, not merely defending what remains.
