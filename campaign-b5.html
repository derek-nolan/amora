Timeline B · Campaign B4 / XXIX

The Veil That Never Was

Age of Founding, Reforged

2 ARV Primary location: Silver Bastion, Anchor Quarters

Core Premise

In the quiet weeks following the Frostborn revelations of B3, a new anomaly takes shape inside Silver Bastion — not in the Heart Seal, not in Hollowrest, but in the private quarters of Anchor and Thread. A Veil-class echo, unknown in Timeline B, manifests inside Lio and Caen's room.

Party & Key Figures

  • Lio Serrin
    First Anchor of the Reforged Cycle.
  • Caen Vance
    First Stabiliser Thread; Frostborn lineage.
  • The Six Travellers
    Arrive only after the purge concludes, assisting in stabilising the remaining resonance.
  • Seraphine Vale
    High Paladin; oversees the post-incident review and institutional response.

Act-by-Act

The Quiet Stirring

Lio and Caen wake to subtle resonance disturbances until a full echo-shadow forms in their room. The echo studies them, not attacking but attempting to "complete" an imagined triadic structure — a pre-Dominion entity drawn to resonance, not ideology.

Corruption of the Stabiliser-Thread

The echo misreads Caen's stabiliser role as an invitation to bind, partially overlaying his stabiliser-thread with a corruption-layer. Lio descends into a deep-drop inversion — a controlled emotional plunge — to reach beneath the corruption.

The First Inversion Purge

Lio overloads the echo with chosen resonance and intimate alignment; Caen uses the stabiliser-thread to anchor the backlash. The echo collapses and dissolves into inert resonance dust — the first dyad-led echo purge in Timeline B.

Final World State

Bastion architects classify the room as Resonance Zone 14 — safe but intensely imprinted — and install new warding protocols for Anchor quarters. Veil Echoes are officially recognised as an independent metaphysical phenomenon in Timeline B, not Dominion-related but predating or paralleling it, seeking triadic resonance rather than control.

Canon status: follows the character register directly. New rite recorded: the Deep-Drop Inversion Purge.
