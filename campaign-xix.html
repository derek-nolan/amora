Timeline A · Campaign XIX

The Dominion Reborn: The Crown and the Anchor

—

8550–8555 ARV Primary location: Highever, Aderyn's Gate, Tarenus Reclaimed

Opening World State

c. 8550 ARV, in the long calm after Concord: cities remember the Veil but run by habit. Legal and ritual civics from Campaigns XV–XVI have become background habit; the focus returns to Highever's Sanctuary and a new attempt to turn devotion into infrastructure. The last heartline node sleeps beneath Highever's Sanctuary of Balance until a student dyad wakes it.

Party & Key Figures

  • Ravel Morn Alive; Crown split at Severance; tri-node dissolved
    Veil-leaning; Taren echo, bearer of the Heart Crown; Purity initiator. Bonded to Thalen.
  • Thalen Ro PC Alive; triggered Severance
    Light/Balance; Zethyr echo, the Anchor; paladin cadet who takes a Luminara fragment. Bonded to Ravel.
  • Preceptor Senn Survived; created dissonance that enabled Severance
    Light (Luminara); dissident Preceptor carrying a Luminara text/fragment.
  • Veyra Merenth Active; stewardry persists post-Severance
    Neutral/bureaucratic steward-clerk; doctrine tubes reactivated under her — pipelines can crown dyads by statute.

Act-by-Act

Cadence

A student bond rekindles the Heart Crown under Highever. Ravel pursues; Thalen accepts the first vow; the Sanctuary node re-lights.

The Purity Edicts

Administration links Highever to Aderyn's Gate; harbour clocks and sleep cycles fall into step. Dissonance is quietly erased; Merenth paperwork readies a "civil-engine" dyad by statute. Peace returns as Purity — order by subtraction — until Senn slips the beat with a Luminara text.

The Third Link and Severance

Tarenus Reclaimed accepts the link; the Veil declares "Dominion pattern restored." Senn escapes the rhythm; Thalen acquires a Luminara fragment in secret. The Anchor lies to the Crown, pockets the Fragment, consents to final integration — and invokes Severance at the peak, splitting the Crown and dissolving the network without war.

World State After Campaign XIX

Geography. Highever's Sanctuary of Balance is confirmed as an academy annex — site of both awakening and Severance. Aderyn's Gate, a new republican port founded on the drowned sub-structure of Marinth, becomes the first external city to harmonise. Tarenus Reclaimed completes the three-node pattern.

Magic. The Veil-like network dissolves without open war — the sixth recorded Severance. Highever keeps unnervingly precise time afterward; the Sanctuary floor bears two scorch silhouettes, one standing, one reaching. Aderyn's Gate stays echo-tuned (perfect harbour clocks, matched sleep cycles) even after the fall. Each night at Tarenus Reclaimed, a faint Vault "off-beat" thump begins — the seed for Campaign XX.

The Durrel Line. Ravel Morn and Thalen Ro are explicitly named as Taren-echo and Zethyr-echo — the pattern's most direct restatement since Campaign XVI's Kaeso/Coren pairing, and the first to use the exact "Crown and Anchor" framing that recurs in Campaign XX's terminology.

Institutions. Merenth Stewardry procedures persist; doctrine tubes can crown dyads as civil engines by statute if unopposed — the direct institutional seed for Campaign XX's civil-engine governance. This campaign bridges the civic-law focus of Campaigns XV–XVI to a post-network world where habit outlives miracle, and hands off directly to Campaign XX via the Tarenus Vault's nightly stirring.
