Timeline B · Campaign B6

The Lantern and the Severance

Post-Campaign B5 (+80 years)

84–85 ARV Primary location: Greymount to Evermere

Core Premise

A tonal shift for Timeline B. What begins as a Luminara-aligned resistance investigation into Shah ritual influence in Greymount evolves into a deeply personal arc of autonomy, severance, and rooted independence — the first Timeline B campaign to end in genuine peace rather than crisis or collapse.

Party & Key Figures

  • Dain Corvett Alive; establishes The Lantern
    Twilight Cleric leading a small Luminara resistance cell; survives an escalating conditioning arc and an attempted Ley-Heir overwrite.
  • Joryn Alive; Dain's partner
    Co-leader of the cell; stabilises Dain during the overwrite crisis and becomes his conscious partner.
  • Lucan Exposed and removed from Dain's life
    Shah-aligned former lover of Dain; carries out repeated conditioning rites that make him vulnerable to metaphysical manipulation.
  • Maera Holt Alive; becomes Lantern manager
    Becomes permanent manager of The Lantern, the coastal tavern Dain purchases in Evermere.

Act-by-Act

Ritual and Fracture

Dain's resistance cell investigates Shah-aligned ritual influence in Greymount. Lucan's repeated private rites on Dain function as slow conditioning, gradually eroding his sense of self despite their intimate, voluntary form — the campaign's central lesson is that consent given under escalating dependency isn't the same as consent given freely. Serio, a cell member, is exposed as an infiltrator and expelled.

The Ley-Heir Threshold

Dormant Ley-Heir architecture, legacy of Campaign B5, activates at a personal level around Dain and Lucan. An attempt to overwrite Dain's identity through the conditioning structure fails — Dain's conscious resistance, Joryn's counter-presence, and Joryn's grounding are enough to stop it.

Rooting Instead of Running

Rather than continuing as fugitives after a targeted killing raises exposure risk, the cell withdraws to Evermere. Dain purchases a coastal tavern, The Lantern, marking a shift from resistance cell to civic anchor — a deliberate choice to build something stable rather than keep running.

House Corvett and House Valmere

Dain lets the truth of his exile — refusing an arranged political marriage — circulate openly rather than confronting his estranged noble family privately. House Corvett formally severs him; a maritime trade agreement with House Valmere is bound to the Evermere Harbor Authority rather than to Dain personally, protecting the agreement from being used as leverage against him again.

World State After Campaign B6

Geography. The Lantern is established as a coastal tavern in Evermere — the first dedicated civic anchor point for what becomes a recurring cast across Campaigns B6–B9.

Magic. This campaign demonstrates that Timeline B's proto-Ley-Heir architecture (seeded in B5) can activate around any bonded pair under the right conditions, not just Caen and Vareth specifically — a worrying generalisation of the pattern that the Order will need to account for going forward.

The Durrel Line. Still absent — this campaign continues the trend of Timeline B developing its coercion/consent and identity-conditioning storylines entirely independent of any Durrel-descended mechanism.

Institutions. B6 resolves without conquest or cataclysm — autonomy over inheritance, rooted belonging over reaction, partnership over domination. Evermere gains strengthened maritime autonomy and The Lantern is established as a stable anchor node. For once in Timeline B, nothing is collapsing — a notable tonal break the site's Canon Ledger tracks as a deliberate structural choice.
