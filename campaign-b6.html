Timeline B · Campaign B5

The Living Reliquary

—

3–4 ARV Primary location: Bastion, Greymount, Brevair

Core Premise

Campaign B5 opens with catastrophe: the Six — future-born temporal anchors whose knowledge had guided early Timeline B stabilisation — are executed within Bastion under orders from the High Justicar, justified as preventing a projected metaphysical fracture.

Party & Key Figures

  • Lio Serrin
    Uncovers, with Caen, the Shah-aligned infiltration behind the Six's execution.
  • Caen Vance
    Investigates alongside Lio; receives a Veil imprint at Greymount that becomes the hidden turning point of the campaign.
  • Seraphine Vale
    Retains nominal authority over the Paladin order as strategic control passes increasingly to Caen.

Act-by-Act

The Assassination of the Six

Through interrogation and Veil resonance analysis, Lio and Caen uncover that the "fracture" narrative was manipulated — the deaths of the Six were exploitation, not prevention. The execution shatters internal trust within Bastion.

Greymount and the Conduit

Seeking the deeper source of manipulation, Lio and Caen find Veil-infused conduit architecture beneath Rillhaven. During its attempted destruction, Caen receives a Veil imprint — the conduit is damaged, but not fully eradicated.

The Living Reliquary

As Bastion stabilises politically, Lio is renamed Vareth. The relationship between Sovereign and Reliquary evolves into a sustained Veil-aligned resonance state; Bastion's clergy begin referring to Vareth as the "Living Reliquary."

The Grand Council Fracture

During a council meant to reassure foreign envoys, a ritual pause triggers Bastion's foundational sigils to activate simultaneously — proof the Veil has integrated the Sovereign-Reliquary structure into long-term recurrence architecture, seeding itself beyond Caen's intent.

Final World State

Bastion stands politically stable, but the Veil now exhibits autonomous behaviour and a proto-Ley-Heir resonance signature is embedded into Bastion's architecture. Caen authors the Sovereign Reliquary Codex, formalising what he believes is stabilisation through permanence — though the pattern now exists independent of his intent. The era of the Ley-Heirs has not yet begun, but the seed is planted.

Canon status: follows the source account directly; this is where Lio Serrin is renamed Vareth.
