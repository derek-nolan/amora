Timeline B · Campaign B11

The Fall of Silver Bastion

—

148–149 ARV Primary location: Evermere, Highever, Silver Bastion

Core Premise

After the Veil consolidation of Brevair in Campaign B10, the northern struggle shifts to Silver Bastion, Highever's sacred fortress-centre — the very location where Timeline B began in Campaign B1. The campaign begins not with armies at the gate but with a relationship — one built first in shared missions and attraction, then deepened into hierarchy, ritual, and willing descent.

Party & Key Figures

  • Gavren Fixed beneath Cameron as ward-knight
    Bastion paladin whose devotion to Cameron becomes the road of his willing descent.
  • Cameron Becomes ruler of the Veiled Citadel
    Veil operative who rises through Gavren's devotion to become the Shah-aligned conqueror and ruler of Bastion.
  • Alwen Stroud Killed by Gavren, at Cameron's direction
    One of Bastion's last true Light stabilisers — the point where corruption becomes irreversible.
  • Tavian Killed during the open seizure
    Practical Bastion witness-figure who notices too much.

Act-by-Act

Evermere Missions

Gavren and Cameron work together in Evermere; attraction and mutual liking become explicit. Gavren's descent doesn't begin in doctrine, but in wanting to stay close.

Return to the Regime

As the campaign moves into Highever and Bastion, Cameron's Veil influence stops functioning as hidden theft and becomes something Gavren knowingly returns to — gear, ritual, and control hardening into a regime.

Pell and Alwen Stroud

Cameron has Gavren kill Pell, severing the old superior line and beginning Cameron's rise. Then Gavren kills Alwen Stroud at Cameron's direction — Bastion's strategic and emotional spine, and the personal severance point in Gavren's own past.

The Veiled Citadel

With Alwen gone, Bastion's collapse accelerates. Cameron takes Gavren into the fortress's ward chamber and uses him as a living conduit to turn the ward itself. Silver Bastion is publicly renamed The Veiled Citadel, and Gavren is fixed beneath Cameron as his ward-knight.

World State After Campaign B11

Geography. Silver Bastion — the founding location of all Timeline B from Campaign B1 onward — falls for the first time in this timeline's history and is renamed the Veiled Citadel. This is the single largest geographic loss Timeline B has suffered in 148 years.

Magic. Cameron's use of Gavren as a "living conduit" to turn the ward is a direct escalation of the conditioning-into-control pattern seen in Campaigns B5, B6, and B10 — but this is the first time it's been weaponised against an entire fortress's foundational magic rather than an individual or a house.

The Durrel Line. Not directly involved — this campaign is centred on Bastion's own paladin institution rather than the Durrel Ridge storyline, though the loss of Bastion makes Durrel Ridge's importance as a remaining Light stronghold (see Campaign B9) much greater going forward.

Institutions. Silver Bastion no longer survives as a Light fortress — its command, chapel, and ward are all under Shah-aligned order. Cameron ends the campaign as the effective ruler of the Citadel and one of the major northern Shah-aligned powers of Timeline B. The Light loses its northern institutional seat and becomes, from here forward, rebellion — setting up Durrel Ridge as its most plausible surviving centre, and setting up Campaign B14's eventual reclamation attempt.
