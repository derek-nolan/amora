The Severing

Post-Veil Reformation — First Cyberpunk Era

c. 6450 ARV Climax of the "old" Durrel cycle

Opening World State

Republics and technocracies all sit atop Veil, Choir, and machine remnants — the world has layered republic, theocracy, machine faith, and Veil residue on top of one another. This is the climax of the old Durrel cycle.

Party & Key Figures

  • Serik Thorne
    Inquisitor turned reformer; breaks the last shard. Learned exactly how the system justified itself.
  • Lior
    Co-rebel with Serik; his moral equal.
  • Iria
    Personal leverage in Serik's bargain, and proof that no abstract victory is worth sacrificing the people caught beneath it.

Act-by-Act

From Inquisitor to Reformer

Serik Thorne begins as an inquisitor and becomes a reformer once he learns exactly how the system justifies itself. The struggle moves through records, courts, defections, street resistance, and finally open collapse.

Refusing the Compromise

Old authorities try to preserve the last Veil shard as a tool that can be made responsible under new ownership. Serik and Lior refuse — their revolution isn't an attempt to place a better ruler above the machine, but to remove the machine's right to choose rulers at all.

The Final Shard

The final shard is destroyed. Divine and dynastic inevitability ends, and magic becomes power without a script. The Severing doesn't create peace — it creates responsibility.

Final World State

The age of divine and dynastic inevitability ends. Magic, law, and history become contingent, opening space for later Echoes to be choices, not mandates. Timeline A's later campaigns still echo Taren and Zethyr — but as cycles people fall into, not hard destiny.
