Timeline A · Campaign III

The Durrel Legacy

Age of Reclamation — “Through Knowledge, Redemption”

c. 900 ARVPrimary theatres: the Hollow, Vault of Lineage, Mirror Bastion, Durrel Ridge/Tarenus

Opening World State

Roughly nine centuries after the first Dominion collapses, its empire has become ruin, taboo, and argument. Brevair has fragmented into semi-autonomous states governed by scholar-lords and merchant councils. Auran Gate survives as a fortified republic of ex-legion families. Silver Bastion has been reclaimed but is half ruin and half academy, where Light and Veil are studied uneasily beneath a doctrine of balance rather than certainty.

Greymount/Tarenus is now a ringed ruin surrounded by fertile land remembered as the Fields of Remission. Beneath the wider Durrel Ridge region lies the sealed legacy of the first age: records, fragments, and the last surviving pieces of the Heart Medallion. The people of this era know the Dominion mostly through ruins and inherited fear. Campaign III is the point where that history stops being legend and becomes personal again.

Party & Key Figures

  • Edrin Durrel PC
    Human sorcerer-scholar, descendant of the imperial line and the last known direct claimant of the Durrel bloodline in this era. Bears the Heart Medallion fragment and must decide whether inheritance is obligation, opportunity, or warning.
  • Cael
    Paladin of Luminara and Ley-Heir echo of Taren. Begins as a figure of faith and conviction, then increasingly reads his inheritance as authority. The Echo Chain with Edrin repeats the emotional geometry of the first Durrel bond without repeating the original people.
  • Merien Thalos
    Collegium archaeologist and institutional counterweight to the two heirs. Helps recover the old record, reseal the Vault, reform the Collegium, and found the New Heartwardens.
  • Dominion restorationists
    Cult remnants and political opportunists who interpret the recovered past as proof that Amora once possessed a stronger, more orderly civilisation and should possess one again.

Act-by-Act

The Hollow

Edrin meets Cael at the Hollow during a period when old Dominion sites are being reopened by scholars, scavengers, and believers. Their connection forms before either man understands it. The Echo Chain is not a conscious vow like the original marriage bond; it is a psychic re-enactment carried by Ley-Heir inheritance, proof that Zethyr and Taren’s architecture survived their deaths.

The Vault of Lineage

The expedition into the Vault of Lineage turns the first two campaigns into recoverable history. Records of Zethyr, Taren, the Heart Medallion, the Bond of Chains, and the creation of the Ley-Heirs are uncovered. The central question becomes immediately political: should the recovered power be restored to protect a fractured world, destroyed before it can repeat itself, or preserved under new custody?

Merien’s presence keeps the campaign from becoming solely an inherited romance. She represents the possibility that history can be studied without being obeyed, and that institutions can be rebuilt around preservation rather than worship.

Mirror Bastion and the Cost of the Old Peace

At the Mirror Bastion and other Dominion sites, the party encounters what imperial nostalgia omits: places and lives shaped around a system that called obedience peace. Restorationists see roads, law, and lost power. Edrin increasingly sees the human cost of a world organised around one will.

Taren Reborn

Cael’s echo does not awaken as instant villainy. It begins as certainty. He speaks again in the old grammar — order against chaos, control as protection, the belief that the past selected him because the present has failed. Eventually he begins claiming the identity of Taren Reborn and moves toward rebuilding the Dominion around the Chain.

The Severing

Edrin acts before the repetition can mature. Using the Heart Medallion, he deliberately severs the Echo Chain. Where Zethyr’s act in Campaign II was desperate mutual destruction, Edrin turns Severance into a conscious doctrine: memory may survive without retaining authority over the living.

Cael vanishes at the Northern Gate. The surviving sources do not agree whether he dies, disperses into the leys, or passes into another state. His disappearance is therefore retained as unresolved rather than converted into a definitive death.

The Final Chronicle

Edrin writes the Final Chronicle and seals the Vault of Tarenus under new terms, hiding the last Heart Medallion shard from those who would use it to restart the cycle. Merien reforms the Collegium Arcanum into a neutral school of equilibrium and helps establish the New Heartwardens — scholars, mages, and custodians bound by preservation rather than unquestioning divine oath.

The campaign ends with black auroras returning over Menge. They are not proof that the Dominion has returned; they are a warning that balance remains unstable and that the world still contains powers older than any single interpretation of Light or Veil.

World State After Campaign III

Geography. Brevair remains fragmented. Auran Gate is a secular fortified republic. Silver Bastion is reclaimed as a mixed academy. Tarenus/Greymount survives as ruin and research zone, while the Vault associated with the Durrel legacy is resealed beneath the wider Ridge tradition. Menge’s black auroras return as an unresolved northern phenomenon.

Magic. The Echo Chain is identified as a recurring Ley-Heir mechanism rather than proof that the original Taren and Zethyr physically reincarnate intact. Edrin’s deliberate Severance demonstrates that inherited magical patterns can be broken without erasing history itself.

The Durrel Line. Edrin is the last known direct claimant of the line in this era. His legacy is the opposite of restoration: he chooses to inherit responsibility for the past without inheriting its throne.

Institutions. Merien’s reformed Collegium studies Light and Veil as forces rather than moral absolutes. The New Heartwardens become guardians of knowledge and equilibrium. Late in Edrin’s lifetime, rumours also speak of an Order of the Echo — seekers who claim to hear old Veilbound voices — but their nature and connection to surviving Ley-Heirs remain unresolved.

Canon status: reconstructed from the early Campaigns I–VI compendium and later Timeline A summaries. Confirmed elements include Edrin, Cael, Merien Thalos, the Vault of Lineage, Echo Chain, Cael’s attempt to rebuild Dominion, Edrin’s Severance, the resealing of the Heart Medallion shard, Merien’s reformed institutions, and the return of black auroras over Menge. The exact relationship between the Vault of Lineage, the Vault of Tarenus, Greymount/Tarenus, and the later Durrel Ridge archive is preserved cautiously because the surviving sources use overlapping names for related but not always identical sites.
