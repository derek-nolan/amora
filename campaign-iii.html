Timeline A · Campaign III

The Durrel Legacy

Age of Reclamation

c. 900 ARV Primary location: The Hollow, Vault of Lineage, Mirror Bastion

Opening World State

Roughly nine centuries after the Dominion's fall: a time of ruins and fragile republics trying to rise in the shadow of Durrel legend. The fall was followed by roughly nine centuries of reconstruction and fragmentation — Brevair dissolved into city-states, the Collegium Arcanum was rebuilt to catalogue Veil remnants, the last Ley-Heir sightings faded, and Silver Bastion eventually returned as a mixed-faith academy. Evermere has been re-forged as a Collegium, the Vault of Tarenus lies sealed, and Brevair is fragmented into mercantile states. The people of this age inherit ruins whose names have become arguments about what the first age meant.

Party & Key Figures

  • Edrin Durrel PC
    Scholar-echo; the last known claimant of the Durrel line. Ultimately severs the Echo Chain rather than let Cael rebuild an empire around it.
  • Cael
    Paladin-echo of Taren, bonded to Edrin through the reformed Echo Chain. Begins to claim he is Taren reborn.
  • Merien Thalos
    Collegium archaeologist; aids the sealing of the Vault and later co-founds the New Heartwardens.

Act-by-Act

The Hollow

Edrin Durrel, a sorcerer-scholar, meets Cael, a paladin of Luminara carrying the echo-pattern of Taren, at the Hollow. Their encounter reforms the Echo Chain before either has chosen what it should mean — the first sign that the Durrel/Veyne bond pattern can recur even without the original people involved.

Vault of Lineage and Mirror Bastion

Edrin, Cael, and Collegium archaeologist Merien Thalos enter the Vault of Lineage and the Mirror Bastion. Records of Zethyr, Taren, the Heart Medallion, and the first bond stop being legend and become ancestral memory. Merien's role matters because the campaign isn't only a romance between echoes — it's also an archaeological and institutional struggle over who controls recovered history.

Taren Reborn

Cael begins to claim he is Taren reborn and moves toward reconstruction of the Dominion. The old rhetoric returns as conviction before it returns as open tyranny: order against chaos, protection through unified command, the belief that the past has selected him to finish its work.

The Severing

Edrin acts earlier than Zethyr had. He uses the Heart Medallion to cut the Echo Chain before Cael can build an empire around it. Cael vanishes at the Northern Gate; the records leave open whether he died, dissolved into the ley, or crossed into another state of existence. This is the first time the word Severance is used for what Zethyr did in Campaign II — Edrin's act names the pattern retroactively.

The Final Chronicle

Edrin writes the Final Chronicle and seals the Vault of Tarenus. Merien goes on to help found the New Heartwardens, an order based on preservation and study rather than divine certainty. Campaign III therefore ends with both a personal refusal and an institutional alternative: knowledge can preserve memory without obeying it.

World State After Campaign III

Geography. Brevair has dissolved into city-states. Evermere is rebuilt as a Collegium. The Vault of Tarenus beneath Durrel Ridge — not Greymount, despite the shared name — is discovered, explored, and resealed under Edrin's new terms. Silver Bastion returns as a mixed-faith academy.

Magic. The Echo Chain is identified and named — proof that the Durrel/Veyne bond pattern can recur across generations through echo-bonded strangers, not just direct descendants. Edrin's Severance becomes the second recorded instance of the act, establishing it as a deliberate, repeatable choice rather than a one-time tragedy.

The Durrel Line. Edrin Durrel is confirmed as "the last known claimant of the line" at this point in the record — meaning the direct line has thinned dramatically in the nine centuries since Zethyr and Taren. His choice to sever rather than restore becomes the founding precedent for every later Severance, including Zethyr's own act being retroactively understood through it.

Institutions. The New Heartwardens are founded by Merien Thalos — an order built on preservation and study rather than divine certainty, a direct answer to how the original Heartwardens calcified into the Dominion. The Vault of Tarenus is sealed under new terms: the world steps toward a future where law, not bloodline or bond, is supposed to rule.

Canon status: restores Merien Thalos and the archaeological/ institutional strand alongside Edrin and Cael.
