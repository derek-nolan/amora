Timeline A · Campaign II

The Veil Ascendant

Age of Dominion

28–55 ARV Primary location: Silver Bastion → Obsidian Bastion, Highever

Opening World State

Silver Bastion becomes Obsidian Bastion; Highever turns from a citadel of Light into a fortress of the Veil. Greymount/Tarenus becomes the imperial capital, and Evermere becomes an imperial pleasure and legal centre.

Party & Key Figures

  • Zethyr Durrel
    Consort and eventual savior of the age; ends the Dominion by sacrifice.
  • Taren Durrel
    (also recorded as Taren Veyne) — Veiled Emperor; Dominion apex. Lover and foe to Zethyr.
  • Seraphine Vale
    Representative of the old Paladin Council. Executed as the Heartwardens were replaced by the Veilwardens.
  • Aldric Fen
    Chaplain and religious opponent of the new order. Executed during Taren's turn.
  • Arienna Voss
    (also recorded as Arienna Lume) — Collegium Magister and Veil researcher. Petitioned for scholarly autonomy; later mind-stilled and sealed into Veil infrastructure.
  • Lyra & Thane
    Former initiates; survived by rising to Acolyte and then General of the Veil.
  • Kellan
    Casualty within the collapsing old order; killed by Zethyr. Full scene unresolved in the record.

Act-by-Act

The First Year of the Veil

Political councils gather in Highever while Taren begins private study of Veil energy — in secret from Seraphine, Aldric, Arienna, and Zethyr, as he starts deciding who can be trusted with the future he imagines.

Greymount and the Rite of Binding

The Rite of Binding beneath Greymount fuses the Veil Core with the Heart Medallion, creating the power the Dominion will rest on. Greymount becomes Greymount Sanctum, later Tarenus Keep. Arienna petitions for autonomy to study Core saturation; Taren treats the request as disloyalty.

The Old Bastion Is Broken

Seraphine and Aldric — institutional and spiritual resistance — are both executed. The Heartwardens are renamed the Veilwardens; Silver Bastion becomes the Obsidian Bastion. Kellan is killed by Zethyr, in a scene the record doesn't fully preserve.

Conquest and Coronation

Auran Gate is conquered and converted into a foundry for Veil paladins. Evermere is annexed and given to Zethyr, turning their old sanctuary into a court and legal centre of the regime. Taren is crowned Veiled Emperor.

The Dominion and the Fall

Taren creates the Ley-Heirs from fused Heart and Veil essence, annexes Brevair and Greymount, and renames the capital Tarenus. At the end of the cycle, Zethyr severs the Veil Core, killing himself and Taren and scattering the Ley-Heirs.

Final World State

The Dominion achieves a terrifying stability — one empire, one god-emperor, one consort. The Veil is entrenched as a tool for control, a power and a scar later ages inherit. The age ends with the seeds of collapse already present: overextension, spiritual exhaustion, and the first cracks in Zethyr's unquestioning devotion.

Canon status: carries the founding cast forward from Campaign I so executions, imprisonment, and betrayal aren't reduced to anonymous institutional change.
