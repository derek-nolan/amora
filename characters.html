Recurring Figures

Seraphine Vale

Grand Marshal of the Paladin Council · High Paladin of Silver Bastion

The clearest case of a single person's fate splitting completely between timelines. Both branches start from the same founding-era Seraphine — Timeline B diverges at 0 ARV, the exact moment of Campaign I/B1, so up to that instant she's literally the same woman in both histories. In Timeline A she's executed within decades; in Timeline B she survives far longer as the Order's ethical spine, but ultimately dies the same way — removing the strongest voice for restraint at a critical moment.

CampaignRole
IGrand Marshal of the Paladin Council; supervises the first Seal work.
IIRepresents the old Council against the Dominion; executed as the Veilwardens replace the Heartwardens.
B1High Paladin of Silver Bastion; founds the Order of Anchors after the rupture that unmakes Taren and Zethyr.
B2Oversees the Hollowrest Custodial Mandate.
B3–B4Guides the Order's political and doctrinal response to the Frostborn and Veil-echo discoveries.
B5Dies during the consolidation following the Six's execution — ending the Order's direct continuity with its founding.

Personality. Composed, principled, and deeply perceptive — she balances compassion with duty so cleanly it borders on artistry, reading people quickly and kindly and always leaving room for honesty. She leads by clarity rather than force, trusts competence over hierarchy, and is acutely aware of how power corrodes intention. Soft-spoken but commanding: she listens first, asks precise questions second, and speaks last.

Zethyr Durrel & Taren Durrel

the founding Durrel bond

The origin point every later "Durrel pattern" recurrence — commanding figure paired with devoted anchor — traces back to. Their story ends completely differently depending on the timeline: Timeline A lets it play out in full; Timeline B erases them before it can begin at all.

CampaignRole
IFound the Heartwardens together; their first ritual creates the psychic bond and unknowingly seeds the Dark Filament.
IITaren becomes Veiled Emperor; Zethyr ends the Dominion by severing the Veil Core, killing them both.
B1Unmade by a third-layer force in the opening rupture — erased, not killed, before their story can begin. Timeline B's entire divergence starts here.

The Six Travellers

Arden Vale & Rian Thale · Zeth Caden & Talren Vale · Solan & Marrek

Time-displaced from the very end of Timeline A into the very beginning of Timeline B by Campaign XXV's temporal collapse — the hinge point connecting both timelines. Arden and Zeth/Talren's stories begin in Timeline A proper before all six cross over together.

CampaignRole
XXII–XXIIIArden Vale (PC) and Caelen Marrith form the accidental shard-pattern imprint that reinstantiates a millennium later; Caelen dies at the Spirefall.
XXIVZeth Caden (PC) and Talren Vale become the era's first Living Dyad, mentored by Arden.
XXVAll six — Arden, Rian, Zeth, Talren, Solan (PC), Marrek — form the six-point resonance formation that ends the Caelen echo and triggers the Fracture.
B1Arrive in the Age of Founding; witness Lio and Kellan's bonding and the Order of Anchors' founding.
B2–B4Continue as stabilisers and witnesses through the Hollowrest, Frostborn, and Veil-echo campaigns.

Personality. Arden — quietly intense, hyper-observant, and now almost over-corrective about consent since his own history with Caelen; mentors by asking questions, goes flat and uncompromising the moment a line is crossed. Rian — warm and deliberately ordinary in a world full of living spells; makes the tea, notices the exits, and is Arden's anchor by choice rather than duty. Zeth — loud-feeling and reactive, reaches for people first and thinks second, allergic to any hidden structure after what Dominion logic did to him. Talren — controlled and quietly fearful of his own capacity to hurt people he loves; his arc is learning to share control rather than simply hold it. Solan — soft-hearted and anxious but with a spine of steel around his own choices; deeply wary of anything that smells like destiny. Marrek — steady and laconic, shows care through action rather than speech, and stays suspicious of any institution that wants to make him useful again.

Lio Serrin (later Vareth) & Kellan Vance (later Caen)

Timeline B's first Anchor and Thread

The founding dyad of Timeline B's entire magic system — every later Anchor/Thread pairing is measured against them. Lio (later Vareth) is the played character across B1–B5; both undergo major identity shifts, tracked in full on the Canon Ledger.

CampaignRole
B1Bond during the founding rupture; become the Heart Seal's first Anchor and Thread.
B2Lead the Hollowrest expedition; recognised by the proto-Anchor as successors to the Binders.
B3Kellan's Frostborn heritage is revealed; the source material's naming shifts to Caen from here.
B4Perform the first Deep-Drop Inversion Purge against a Veil-class echo.
B5Lio is renamed Vareth; he and Caen become the "Living Reliquary," an unintentional Ley-Heir-equivalent structure.

Personality. Lio is earnest and deeply relational — his first move in any crisis is to protect someone he loves, gentle until pushed and incandescent when defending others; naturally submissive-coded in intimate dynamics out of trust and admiration, never out of weakness, and won't tolerate coercion regardless. Hollowrest leaves him with a new quiet confidence — he still doesn't brag, he just stands a little firmer. Caen's own temperament shifts significantly once his Frostborn heritage surfaces in B3; see his campaign pages for that arc in full.

The Timeline B Durrel Line

Taren Durrel (Ridge) → Jack Durrel → Caelen Durrel

A later, separate branch of the Durrel family — not descendants of Zethyr and Taren, who were erased before they could have children. The Taren Durrel who appears here in B9 shares a name with the original Taren but is a distinct person, born generations later. This line carries Durrel Ridge's institutional weight through the second half of Timeline B's current record.

CampaignRole
B7Seren, Caelan, and Steward Alren Durrel introduce the Ridge as a standing ally.
B9Taren Durrel PC reclaims Tarenus's Vault and swears the Renewed Oath of the Ridge.
B12Jack Durrel PC is named Warden of Meaning; a cadet-branch Durrel child is brought to the Ridge.
B13That child, now Caelen Durrel PC, is targeted and reclaimed from Thorn House.
