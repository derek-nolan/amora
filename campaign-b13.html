Timeline B · Campaign B13

The Thorned Hound

—

c. 162 ARV Primary location: Durrel Ridge, Thorn House, Evermere

Core Premise

Twelve years after B12, the cadet-line Durrel child raised at the Ridge has grown into Caelen Durrel, a young paladin shaped by a house built from survival and chosen continuity. That makes him vulnerable in a specific way: he wants to be worthy of the home that saved him.

Party & Key Figures

  • Caelen Durrel
    Young Durrel paladin; manipulated, transformed into the hound-identity Veyr, taken by Thorn House, restored, and reborn an Oath of Vengeance paladin. The player character.
  • Rul Hask
    Caelen's initial lover and manipulator; later transformed by Thorn House into Ren, then cleansed and exiled by Caelen.
  • Corvin Hale
    Silver Dawn paladin who resists Sareth's seduction, shatters the Veil hold over Veyr, and restores Caelen — later his romantic partner.
  • Sareth
    Thorn House alpha and seducer-handler; transforms both Rul and Edran, killed during the Thorn House strike.
  • Maelor Venn
    Thorn House senior alpha and architect of the Thorn Reliquary Project; killed by Caelen.
  • Edran Valehart & Mara Vennic
    Silver Dawn paladin transformed into Tav and later rescued and restored; Mara, a caravan factor, joins the Ridge and becomes Edran's love interest.

Act-by-Act

The First Leash

Rul Hask becomes Caelen's lover and gradually isolates him through private dominance and Veil-influenced conditioning, renaming him Veyr — a hound-state identity. The campaign draws its central line early: consensual kink and chosen surrender aren't the problem; hidden control and magical rewriting are.

Thorn House and the Fall of Rul

Thorn House, a Brevair-adjacent alpha/hound cult, takes both Rul and Veyr. Sareth seduces and remakes Rul into Ren using the same logic Rul used on Caelen — proving Thorn House's doctrine that a hound cannot keep a hound.

The Thorn Reliquary Project

Thorn House identifies Veyr as proof of concept for converting high-value bloodlines into stable Veil-owned hounds. Sareth field-tests the method again, transforming Silver Dawn paladin Edran Valehart into Tav.

Corvin Resists, Caelen Returns

Sareth misreads Corvin Hale's concern for Edran as an opening — but Corvin resists, uses paladin revelation magic, and shatters the Veil illusion over Veyr, calling out the hidden name: Caelen Durrel. Mother Alayne performs the Rite of Sovereign Return at the Evermere shrine.

The Vault Cleansed

Caelen tells Jack and Taren that Rul accessed the Vault of Tarenus through him. The vault is blessed and cleansed of faint Veil residue, and access wards are strengthened so coerced Durrel blood no longer counts as valid entry.

Final World State

Thorn House is destroyed, Caelen restored with every memory intact — his victory is not that he becomes untouched, but that memory no longer commands him. He reclaims leather and kink as chosen desire with Corvin. A six-week epilogue shows Durrel Ridge changed but not broken: a protection circle of Caelen, Corvin, Jack, Dena, Taren, Mara, and Edran, properly guarded, properly fed, and properly home.

Canon status: follows the source account directly; this campaign already uses the settled Durrel spelling throughout.
