Timeline B · Campaign B13

The Thorned Hound

The difference between chosen surrender and stolen selfhood — and how a home learns to protect without becoming a cage

Age of Founding, Reforged

c. 162 ARV Primary location: Durrel Ridge, Thorn House, Evermere

Core Premise

Twelve years after Campaign B12, the cadet-line Durrel child raised at the Ridge has grown into Caelen Durrel, a young paladin shaped by a house built from survival, resistance, and chosen continuity — Taren as house-holder, Jack as Warden of Meaning, Dena as household anchor. That makes Caelen vulnerable in a specific way: he wants to be worthy of the home that saved him, and that desire to belong is exactly what an outside predator learns to exploit. What begins as a private relationship becomes, through isolation and hidden magical influence, a full identity-conquest — Caelen is reshaped into an entirely different persona and eventually taken into Thorn House, a Brevair-adjacent Veil cult built around dominance hierarchy, transformation, and the conversion of desire into obedience.

Party & Key Figures

  • Caelen Durrel PC Young Durrel paladin, raised at the Ridge after Campaign B12. Manipulated into an altered, obedient persona, taken by Thorn House, restored, and reborn an Oath of Vengeance paladin.
  • Rul Hask
    Caelen's initial lover and manipulator, gradually isolating him through private control and magically-reinforced conditioning. Later transformed by Thorn House into a broken identity of his own, then cleansed and exiled by Caelen.
  • Corvin Hale
    Silver Dawn paladin who resists Sareth's manipulation, breaks the illusion holding Caelen, and restores him — later his romantic partner.
  • Edran Valehart
    Silver Dawn paladin transformed the same way Caelen was; rescued and restored, though it takes a second restoration rite before the pull fully breaks.
  • Mara Vennic
    Caravan factor and route-broker who joins the Ridge's protection circle; becomes Edran's partner, helping him recover by treating him as capable rather than fragile.
  • Sareth
    Thorn House's manipulator-handler; transforms both Rul and Edran using the same methods used on Caelen. Killed during the Thorn House strike.
  • Maelor Venn
    Thorn House's senior figure and architect of the Reliquary Project. Killed by Caelen.
  • Mother Alayne
    Evermere shrine authority who performs Caelen's restoration rite, and later a scar-removal rite.
  • Jack Durrel & Taren Durrel
    Carried from Campaigns B9 and B12; lead the Ridge's response once Caelen is recovered, and help cleanse Tarenus's Vault of the residue Rul's access left behind.

Act-by-Act

Act I — The Ridge, Rul, and the First Leash

Caelen begins at Durrel Ridge as a young paladin raised in the household formed after Campaign B12. Rul Hask becomes his lover and gradually isolates him through private control, secrecy, and magically-reinforced conditioning that Rul frames the entire time as intimacy and play. Caelen is guided step by step into obedience cues, private rituals, and identity-shaping experiences; Rul begins naming and treating him as an entirely separate persona built around obedience and possession. Caelen initially understands what's happening as private, consensual dynamics between lovers, unaware how much magical influence is actively reinforcing the transformation underneath it. The campaign draws its central line here, early: consensual power exchange and chosen surrender were never the problem. Hidden control, isolation, and magical rewriting of someone's identity without their genuine consent are.

Act II — The Making of a New Self

Rul draws Caelen away from ordinary Ridge structures and deepens the conditioning in private, using magical influence to intensify the new persona and reshape Caelen's sense of self more thoroughly. Caelen is gradually made to understand the altered identity as his real self in private, while a public-facing mask stays intact around the Ridge. Rul gains access to Tarenus's Vault only through Caelen — a detail that matters later, since the Vault itself is never broadly compromised, only accessed through him. By the act's end, Rul is convinced he's won completely: Caelen is buried, the new persona dominant, and the Ridge entirely unaware of how far the transformation has actually gone.

Act III — Thorn House and the Fall of Rul

Thorn House enters the story through Varro Kain, revealing a Brevair-based cult where transformed followers and dominance hierarchy are fully systematised. Rul, intrigued by the promise of stronger structure and a wider hierarchy, brings Caelen's altered persona toward Thorn House — and is subdued alongside him and transported there. Maelor Venn, the House's senior figure, immediately identifies what Caelen has become as rare: a Durrel paladin fully remade. Sareth, a younger but highly capable manipulator within the House, seduces Rul using the exact same methods Rul once used on Caelen — proving Thorn House's own doctrine in the process: no one who conditions another is safe from being conditioned themselves. Rul is renamed and remade in turn, and Sareth takes control of Caelen's altered persona away from him structurally — the old master/subject structure Rul built is broken, and both men now answer to Sareth and the House instead.

Act IV — The Thorn Reliquary Project

Thorn House uses both Rul and Caelen's altered persona in ongoing hierarchy rites and conditioning cycles. Caelen's transformation is deepened further under Sareth, refined well beyond what Rul had originally built, until almost nothing recoverable of Caelen himself remains accessible on the surface. Thorn House identifies what they've built in him as proof of concept for something much larger — the Thorn Reliquary Project, aimed at converting high-value bloodlines (paladins, noble heirs, sacred-blood men) into permanently obedient assets while preserving useful traits: bloodline access, oath-discipline, identity traces, political value.

Act V — Sareth's Field Trial

Maelor sends Sareth out to prove the method works beyond Caelen specifically. Sareth travels to Evermere and targets Edran Valehart, a young Silver Dawn paladin, framing the entire process as romance and trust rather than transformation. Edran is drawn in gradually, step by step, with Caelen's own altered persona used throughout as living proof that the process is safe — a calm, obedient example showing the next target how it can feel like relief rather than loss. Edran is remade in turn, though less completely than Caelen — enough of the original Edran stays accessible that he can still function as a public face while the altered persona waits underneath.

Act VI — Corvin Resists, Caelen Returns

Sareth next targets Corvin Hale, another Silver Dawn paladin close to Edran, reading his concern for his friend as an opening. Corvin resists instead — he uses paladin revelation magic, breaks the first hold Sareth attempts to place, and shatters the magical illusion sustaining Caelen's altered persona entirely. Corvin recognises the truth underneath it and calls out Caelen's real name directly. The hold fractures. Caelen resurfaces — unstable, still marked by what happened to him, but human and himself again. Sareth flees but is captured alive in Evermere. Caelen is taken to the Evermere shrine, where Mother Alayne performs the Rite of Sovereign Return — restoring him fully and cleanly as himself while preserving every memory of what happened, rather than erasing them. The altered persona remains memory from here forward, not master. Caelen doesn't long to go back to it.

Act VII — Vengeance Oath and the Destruction of Thorn House

Restored but carrying the full memory of everything that happened to him, Caelen reaches a milestone and becomes an Oath of Vengeance paladin — an oath he frames not as blind rage, but as memory carrying a blade: prevent repetition, hold accountable those who would remake others, and refuse to let what happened to him become silence. He summons the Ridge and plans a surgical strike against Thorn House, split across three teams — one protecting Mother Alayne while she casts a mass rite to sever every active command-line Thorn House maintains over its victims, one dedicated purely to rescue, and one aimed squarely at the House's leadership. The severing rite breaks the entire network of conditioning triggers and identity-locks in one motion; Edran is freed and restored enough to know himself again; Jack personally destroys the chamber where Thorn House stripped its victims' names from them. Caelen confronts Maelor Venn directly in the House's inner chamber and kills him under his vengeance oath. Sareth dies during the strike attempting to reach Edran one last time. Thorn House's local leadership, its transformation infrastructure, and its command architecture are all destroyed or cleansed in the same operation.

Act VIII — Cleansing, Exile, and Return

Rul is cleansed once Thorn House falls. Caelen decides his fate personally: exile, not forgiveness — if Rul ever returns, joins another such group, or repeats what he did, Caelen will kill him himself. Caelen asks Mother Alayne to remove the physical scars left by his conditioning; the rite succeeds, memories staying fully intact throughout. He receives Oath of Vengeance armour from the Evermere paladins, bearing a hidden inscription: Returned. Remembering. Unowned. He returns quietly to Durrel Ridge through the side gate, and goes instinctively first to Dena's kitchen — confirming it, without needing to say so, as his safest place in the world. Edran receives a second restoration rite once Caelen notices he's still struggling more than he's letting on; this one succeeds fully, breaking the last pull of what was done to him.

Act IX — Reclaiming the Ridge, Reclaiming Desire

Caelen makes clear that the trusted group forming around him isn't meant to become a cult-hunting band — its purpose is simply to protect Durrel Ridge properly. Mara Vennic joins as a non-paladin member of that circle, a logistics-savvy outsider who understands exactly how cults, smugglers, and corrupt houses actually move people and goods, and begins a slow relationship with Edran. Caelen and Corvin's own relationship deepens into something warm and genuinely chosen — Caelen explicitly isn't scarred by consensual power-exchange dynamics themselves; what happened to him was the coercion and the magical rewriting, not the dynamic Rul originally claimed to offer. He reclaims that kind of chosen desire with Corvin on his own terms, and by the campaign's end the two of them are close to saying they love each other.

Act X — The Vault Cleansed

Caelen tells Jack and Taren that Rul accessed Tarenus's Vault through him while under Thorn House's influence, and that it may carry lingering traces. Neither judges him for it. The group confirms Rul only ever reached the Vault through Caelen specifically — it was never otherwise compromised — and together they bless and cleanse it, finding and removing faint residue. Access wards are strengthened afterward so coerced Durrel blood no longer counts as valid entry, closing the vulnerability this campaign exposed for good. A six-week epilogue shows Durrel Ridge changed but not broken: a protection circle of Caelen, Corvin, Jack, Dena, Taren, Mara, and Edran, properly guarded, properly fed, and properly home.

World State After Campaign B13

Geography. Thorn House, a Brevair-adjacent cult site, is introduced and destroyed in the same campaign. Evermere gains new significance as the site of Caelen's restoration, Sareth's capture, and the anti-conditioning rites central to the whole arc. The Vault of Tarenus beneath Durrel Ridge is cleansed and re-warded.

Magic. Tarenus's Vault access wards are permanently strengthened — coerced Durrel blood no longer counts as valid entry, closing a vulnerability this campaign exposed for the first time. This is a direct, lasting change to how the Vault's bloodline-based magic works, tracing all the way back to the access mechanism first established in Campaign B9.

The Durrel Line. Caelen Durrel's victory isn't that he becomes untouched by what happened to him — it's that memory no longer commands him. He reclaims his own desires as chosen, with Corvin, rather than something imposed on him. The line proves resilient here: exploited, but not permanently broken.

Institutions. A six-week epilogue shows Durrel Ridge changed but not broken: a protection circle of Caelen, Corvin, Jack, Dena, Taren, Mara, and Edran, properly guarded, properly fed, and properly home — continuing the household model Jack established back in Campaign B12, now expanded by an entire new generation of chosen family around it.

Canon status: follows the source account, with terminology adjusted substantially to keep the site's content at a general-audience level while preserving the coercion-vs-consent themes central to the story. The source material is significantly more explicit in its depiction of Thorn House's methods, imagery, and the private dynamics between characters than reflected here — see the Canon Ledger for the site's approach to this material.
