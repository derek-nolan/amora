Timeline B · Campaign B12

The Warden of Meaning

Durrel Ridge Era

150 ARV Primary location: Durrel Ridge, Tarenus's Vault

Core Premise

Jack Durrel returns to the Ridge in the aftermath of Marcus's death — no longer trapped beneath the threat that shaped his recent life, but not yet fully restored to ease. The campaign opens not with a fortress assault but with return: can Jack truly inhabit his own inheritance rather than merely stand near it?

Party & Key Figures

  • Jack Durrel
    Durrel heir, Ridge operative, and survivor of Marcus's shadow. The player character.
  • Taren Durrel
    Durrel Ridge leadership; his distance from Jack begins to close once Marcus is confirmed dead.
  • Jorah
    Arrives during the campaign and is gradually allowed into Jack's private orbit; confirmed as real and structurally important by the Vault revelation.
  • Marek & Dena
    Jack's core circle; Dena becomes a key emotional anchor and continuity figure for the household.
  • Marcus
    Dead before the campaign's present, but structurally important as the road Jack's life might have taken.

Act-by-Act

The House Reopens

Jack reopens his inherited house at the Ridge, offering it as a shared home to his team. What could have remained a private mausoleum becomes a communal house — the campaign's first answer to the Veil: home must be reclaimed in truth before it can be defended in war.

Scouting and Household Life

The Ridge functions as both resistance centre and lived household. Jorah becomes central not through spectacle, but through the ordinary: meals, rooms, jokes, trust, and the possibility of chosen intimacy that doesn't feel like manipulation.

Marcus's Road Closes

Marcus's death is confirmed in the true line, breaking the old route of manipulation and fear. Jorah can exist in Jack's life without being overshadowed, and Taren's distance from Jack begins to lose its old justification.

Tarenus's Vault

Jack descends into the Vault with Jorah and is shown a true possible future: Marcus surviving, taking Jorah's place, and corrupting Jack through home and false intimacy. Tarenus confirms Marcus's death and Jorah's reality, then names Jack a Warden of Meaning — heir to the Durrel line's power to name and defend truth inside bonds.

Taren and Jack Align

Jack tells Taren the truth of the Vault vision. Taren finally stops treating Jack as a lingering risk and starts treating him as vital to the mission. A surviving seven-year-old cadet-branch Durrel child is brought to the Ridge, and the household becomes visibly the place where the next generation will be raised.

Final World State

Durrel Ridge re-centres as command-centre, lived household, and the most plausible surviving Light stronghold after Bastion's fall. Taren inherits the Ridge and Warden-seat; Jack inherits the truth-knife inside corrupted bonds — their roles no longer compete. The arrival of the cadet-branch child locks in the next generational handoff: the Ridge is now raising what comes next, not merely defending what remains.
