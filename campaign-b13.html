Timeline B · Campaign B13

The Thorned Hound

—

c. 162 ARV Primary location: Durrel Ridge, Thorn House, Evermere

Core Premise

Twelve years after Campaign B12, the cadet-line Durrel child raised at the Ridge has grown into Caelen Durrel, a young paladin shaped by a house built from survival and chosen continuity. That makes him vulnerable in a specific way: he wants to be worthy of the home that saved him — and that desire to belong is exactly what an outside predator learns to exploit.

Party & Key Figures

  • Caelen Durrel PC Alive; restored with memory intact
    Young Durrel paladin; manipulated into an altered, obedient identity, taken by Thorn House, restored, and reborn an Oath of Vengeance paladin. The player character.
  • Rul Hask Alive; exiled
    Caelen's initial lover and manipulator; later transformed by Thorn House into a broken identity of his own, then cleansed and exiled by Caelen.

    Silver Dawn paladin who resists Sareth's manipulation, breaks the illusion holding Caelen, and restores him — later his romantic partner.

  • Maelor Venn Killed by Caelen
    Thorn House senior figure and architect of the Reliquary Project.

    Silver Dawn paladin transformed and later rescued; Mara, a caravan factor, joins the Ridge and becomes Edran's love interest.

Act-by-Act

The First Leash

Rul Hask becomes Caelen's lover and gradually isolates him through private control and magically-reinforced conditioning, renaming him and reshaping his sense of self. The campaign draws its central line early: consensual devotion and chosen surrender aren't the problem — hidden control and magical rewriting of someone's identity without their genuine consent are.

Thorn House and the Fall of Rul

Thorn House, a Brevair-adjacent cult built around control and devotion, takes both Rul and Caelen. Sareth manipulates and remakes Rul using the same logic Rul used on Caelen — proving Thorn House's doctrine that no one who conditions another is safe from being conditioned themselves.

The Thorn Reliquary Project

Thorn House identifies Caelen as proof of concept for converting high-value bloodlines into permanently obedient assets. Sareth field-tests the method again, transforming Silver Dawn paladin Edran Valehart the same way.

Corvin Resists, Caelen Returns

Sareth misreads Corvin Hale's concern for Edran as an opening — but Corvin resists, uses paladin revelation magic, and breaks the illusion holding Caelen, calling out his true name and identity. Mother Alayne performs a restoration rite at the Evermere shrine.

The Vault Cleansed

Caelen tells Jack and Taren that Rul accessed Tarenus's Vault through him while under Thorn House's influence. The Vault — the same one Taren reclaimed in Campaign B9 and Jack was named Warden of in Campaign B12 — is blessed and cleansed of the residue, and access wards are strengthened so coerced Durrel blood no longer counts as valid entry. This is a direct, concrete institutional response to what B13 reveals: bloodline-based magic can be exploited if the person carrying it is compromised.

World State After Campaign B13

Geography. Thorn House is destroyed as an institution. Durrel Ridge and Evermere both feature prominently, tying this campaign directly into the household and Vault continuity established across B9 and B12.

Magic. Tarenus's Vault access wards are permanently strengthened — coerced Durrel blood no longer counts as valid entry, closing a vulnerability this campaign exposed for the first time. This is a direct, lasting change to how the Vault's bloodline-based magic works.

The Durrel Line. Caelen Durrel's victory isn't that he becomes untouched by what happened — it's that memory no longer commands him. He reclaims his own desires as chosen, with Corvin, rather than something imposed on him. The line proves resilient: exploited, but not permanently broken.

Institutions. A six-week epilogue shows Durrel Ridge changed but not broken: a protection circle of Caelen, Corvin, Jack, Dena, Taren, Mara, and Edran, properly guarded, properly fed, and properly home — continuing the household model Jack established back in Campaign B12.
