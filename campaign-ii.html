Timeline A · Campaign I

The Heartwardens

Age of Founding

0–27 ARV Primary location: Silver Bastion, Highever Duration: seven months

Opening World State

The very beginning of recorded history. Highever's Silver Bastion holds the faith of Luminara and the first order of paladins, the Heartwardens. Evermere is a coastal sanctuary; Brevair a crown ally; Menge a frozen frontier of zeal. The world is small, pious, and still mostly whole — shaped already by the Mage Calamity, the Mage Wars, and the Oath Wars, which belong to the pre-game past.

Party & Key Figures

  • Zethyr Durrel
    Lightbound paladin; Heartwarden founder. Bonded to Taren.
  • Taren Durrel (also recorded as Taren Veyne)
    Veil champion; co-founder of the cycle. Lover of Zethyr.
  • Seraphine Vale
    Grand Marshal of the Paladin Council; supervised the first Seal work. Later executed during the rise of the Dominion.
  • Arienna Voss (also recorded as Arienna Lume)
    Magister of the Collegium Arcanum; Flamebinder. Linked the Bastion to the mage college and joined the founding ranks.
  • Aldric Fen
    Heartwarden chaplain and Bastion staff. Later executed by Taren.
  • Lyra & Thane
    Silver Bastion initiates; later rose through the Veil order and became Generals of the Veil.
  • Orell
    Antagonist whose plot closed the Age of Light. Exact office and motive unresolved.
  • Kellan
    Named member of the first-cycle cast. Later killed by Zethyr. Whether he was Zethyr's remembered best friend remains unresolved.

Act-by-Act

Months One & Two — Silver Bastion

Zethyr and Taren's shared beginning at Silver Bastion, beneath the temple of Luminara, where the unstable Heart Seal was discovered. Their first successful contact created the psychic bond that would define both campaigns.

Month Three — The First Balancing Ritual

The first balancing ritual succeeds. The Durrel bond manifests visibly and the Heart Medallion reawakens — and beneath that victory, the first trace of the Dark Filament is absorbed into the medallion before anyone understands what it will become.

Month Four — The Other Seals

The Aether, Flame, and Stone Seals are stabilised. Zethyr and Taren earn recognition as co-leaders of the Heartwardens; the order's oath becomes "In heart, there is light; in light, there is restraint."

Month Five — Evermere

Zethyr and Taren marry at Evermere and strengthen the bond. The danger isn't their love — it's the way the link begins turning private devotion into usable power. The Chains of the Veil begin forming unconsciously.

Months Six & Seven — Orell's Plot

The Age of Light ends through Orell's plot. Zethyr and Taren uncover and end it — requiring their first taking of life. Campaign I closes not at the height of triumph, but with faith fractured.

Final World State

The Heartwardens become both paladins and proto-bureaucracy: love as chain, obedience as miracle. The Durrel bond becomes a mythic constant that later ages re-enact in echo form. The Dominion is not yet born, but its seed — love as hierarchy — is planted.

Canon status: expanded from chat-mined chronicle reconstruction. Orell's exact office and motive, and the identity of Zethyr's separately-remembered best friend, are not present in the surviving record and have not been invented here.
