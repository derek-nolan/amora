Timeline A · Campaign II

The Veil Ascendant

Age of Dominion

28–55 ARV Primary location: Silver Bastion → Obsidian Bastion, Highever

Opening World State

Silver Bastion becomes Obsidian Bastion; Highever turns from a citadel of Light into a fortress of the Veil. Greymount/Tarenus becomes the imperial capital, and Evermere — the site of Zethyr and Taren's marriage in Campaign I — becomes an imperial pleasure and legal centre. The Dark Filament absorbed into the Heart Medallion during Campaign I's first ritual is about to become the foundation of an entire magical technology.

Party & Key Figures

  • Zethyr Durrel
    Consort and eventual savior of the age; ends the Dominion by sacrifice, severing the Veil Core.
  • Taren Durrel (also recorded as Taren Veyne)
    Veiled Emperor; Dominion apex. Lover and foe to Zethyr.
  • Seraphine Vale
    Representative of the old Paladin Council. Executed as the Heartwardens were replaced by the Veilwardens.
  • Aldric Fen
    Chaplain and religious opponent of the new order. Executed during Taren's turn.
  • Arienna Voss (also recorded as Arienna Lume)
    Collegium Magister and Veil researcher. Petitioned for scholarly autonomy to study Core saturation and cognitive looping; Taren treated the request as disloyalty. Later mind-stilled and sealed into Veil infrastructure.
  • Lyra & Thane
    Former initiates; survived by rising to Acolyte and then General of the Veil.
  • Kellan
    Casualty within the collapsing old order; killed by Zethyr. The full scene isn't preserved in the accessible indexed record.

Act-by-Act

The First Year of the Veil

Campaign II is longer than the seven-month founding campaign, following the conversion of a successful order into a state. In the first three months, political councils gather in Highever while Taren begins private study of Veil energy. The secrecy matters — he's no longer merely solving a magical danger alongside Seraphine, Aldric, Arienna, Zethyr, and the initiates; he's beginning to decide which of them can be trusted with the future he imagines.

Greymount and the Rite of Binding

The Rite of Binding beneath Greymount fuses the Veil Core with the Heart Medallion and creates the power the Dominion will rest on. Greymount becomes Greymount Sanctum and later Tarenus Keep. The Court of the Veil, founded by the Emperor of Brevair in an attempt to control or weaponise Taren, becomes an instrument Taren absorbs and ultimately rules himself. Arienna's path becomes especially important here: she petitions Taren and Zethyr for autonomy to study Core saturation and cognitive looping. Taren treats the request as disloyalty. Later records describe her as mind-stilled, imprisoned in harmony, or sealed alive within the Veil Core — the wording varies, but the loss of her autonomy doesn't.

The Old Bastion Is Broken

Seraphine Vale and Aldric Fen represent two different forms of resistance to the new order — institutional and spiritual. Both are executed. The Heartwardens are renamed the Veilwardens, and Silver Bastion becomes the Obsidian Bastion or Sanctum of the Veil. This isn't administrative reorganisation; it's the moment the staff and traditions that formed Zethyr and Taren are declared obsolete by one of their own founders. Lyra and Thane survive by moving with the new power, becoming Acolytes and later Generals of the Veil. Kellan doesn't survive — the record states Zethyr killed him, but the indexed extracts don't preserve enough of the original scene to say why, where, or whether Kellan was the best friend remembered elsewhere in the project. This edition doesn't merge those identities without evidence.

Conquest and Coronation

Auran Gate is conquered and converted into a foundry and training centre for Veil paladins. Evermere is annexed and presented to Zethyr, turning the former sanctuary of their marriage into a private court and legal centre of the regime. Taren is crowned Veiled Emperor. Zethyr yields command within the bond, hoping proximity and devotion might still restrain what public opposition can no longer stop.

The Dominion and the Fall

The following years complete the structure. Taren creates the Ley-Heirs from fused Heart and Veil essence — the first appearance of the mechanism that will echo through every later age — annexes Brevair and Greymount, and renames the capital Tarenus. The Emperor who had founded the Court of the Veil is executed. The Ritual of Unification deepens the bond while Zethyr foresees the imbalance and fails to turn Taren away from it. Menge's auroras become the sign of a Light resurgence outside the Dominion's control; the Veil reacts violently. At the end of the cycle, Zethyr severs the Veil Core, killing himself and Taren and scattering the Ley-Heirs.

World State After Campaign II

Geography. Highever's Silver Bastion is reforged as the Obsidian Bastion. Greymount is rebuilt as Greymount Sanctum, then renamed Tarenus Keep — the Dominion's capital. Evermere shifts from sanctuary to imperial pleasure-and-law centre. Auran Gate becomes a Veil-paladin foundry. Brevair is annexed and its capital renamed Durrelion, rebuilt in black stone.

Magic. The Veil is created — the Dark Filament from Campaign I fused with the Heart Medallion at the Rite of Binding. The Ley-Heirs are created from fused Heart and Veil essence, then scattered when Zethyr severs the Core at the campaign's end. This severing is the first Severance in Amora's history, though the term itself isn't coined until Campaign III.

The Durrel Line. Taren becomes Veiled Emperor; Zethyr becomes consort, then savior-by-sacrifice. Their deaths at the end of this campaign end the first generation of the Durrel bond, but the pattern — commanding figure, devoted anchor, power that turns love into hierarchy — survives them and recurs for the rest of Timeline A.

Institutions. The Heartwardens become the Veilwardens. The Dominion achieves a terrifying stability: one empire, one god-emperor, one consort. The Veil is entrenched as a tool for control that later ages inherit as both power and scar. The age ends with the seeds of its own collapse already present — overextension, spiritual exhaustion, and the first cracks in Zethyr's unquestioning devotion.

Canon status: carries the founding cast forward so executions, imprisonment, promotion, and betrayal aren't reduced to anonymous institutional change.
