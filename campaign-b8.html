Timeline B · Campaign B8

The Corvett Reckoning

The Audit War — institutions can be weapons, unless you force them to tell the truth

86 ARV Primary location: Evermere, Corvett Estate

Core Premise

Same Evermere era as B7 — The Lantern established, Mark now a trusted youth-squad lead, using Shadow magic as a discipline rather than a spectacle. A covert "stewardship audit" apparatus begins mapping the Lantern's physical layout and testing its thresholds. The crew recognises immediately this isn't a raid — it's classification: learn who reacts, then crush them through civic channels rather than open force.

Party & Key Figures

  • Mark "Mark Val" (Marcus Valmere) PC Now a trusted youth-squad lead, using Shadow magic as a discipline rather than a spectacle. Emotional stake remains Darian Corvett; his arc here is belonging without being owned.
  • Elowen Marr
    Mid-tier Relief Registry steward; the crucial "flip" who agrees to testify in exchange for a guaranteed Stability for Evermere.
  • Darius Corvett
    Patriarch and steering hand behind the civic pressure network — the campaign's primary antagonist, ultimately removed through proof rather than violence.
  • Daeron Vale
    Courier and logistics link between the audit network and the Corvett estate, captured alive during Act VI's intercept.
  • Darian Corvett
    Dain's younger brother and Mark's ongoing relationship; becomes a leverage target the crew has to actively protect through a rotating safehouse network.

Act-by-Act

Act I — The Measured Walls

The crew discovers evidence of covert surveying at the Lantern — chalk marks, waxed thread, a distinctive brass washer and hook-mark pattern — and traces it into Evermere's chapel-side civic lanes, identifying a "logging" ward culture built to record trespassers rather than stop them. It's the first confirmation that someone is constructing a slow-pressure machine meant to strangle the Lantern without ever resorting to open violence. The Lantern is confirmed as a political target, not a random tavern.

Act II — The Committee Room / the Hook-Mark Network

Mark penetrates the "stewardship" layer directly, tailing a coordinator to a locked-room committee node and overhearing its actual priorities: protect the public face first, then identify the hidden operational core — the Annex — underneath. No steel, no martyrs, only paperwork strangulation and quiet coercion. The word "patriarch" surfaces as a steering authority sitting above the local committee layer entirely.

Act III — Printshop Drop

The crew strikes a shuttered printshop acting as a covert node, seizing physical evidence — hook-mark tokens, schedule slips, committee writ patterns, direct logistics fragments — using civic mimicry and shadow mobility, with sleep and containment tactics keeping the operation bloodless. This is the first chain-of-custody evidence solid enough to flip a civic insider: the audit war stops being rumour and becomes a documented network.

Act IV — Darian Protection

The committee's pressure turns personal once Darian becomes a plausible leverage target. Mark moves him through a rotating safehouse network — a salt-store loft, a net-mender's nook, a string of secondary lofts — deliberately avoiding any threshold logging that could be traced. The crew holds its discipline throughout: no public panic, no militia posture, Maera keeping the Lantern's face entirely normal to the outside world. Through overheard fragments and captured slips, the steering hand behind everything is finally named: Darius Corvett.

Act V — Flip the Steward

Rather than fight the committee head-on, the Lantern flips the registry itself. Elowen Marr agrees to testify in exchange for a guaranteed Stability agreement Darian himself drafts and delivers: no refugee scapegoating, no street war, the relief mission preserved, a controlled transition narrative. A dual-chain testimony protocol is set up — one sealed memo dead-dropped for extraction, a backup hidden inside the registry itself. Evermere's civic backbone becomes an ally instead of a battleground, and the campaign's actual victory condition comes into focus: make the system convict the parasite from the inside.

Act VI — The Inspection Pressure

The network escalates to daytime "fire inspections," but its handlers grow careless doing it. Maera manages the inspection calmly while noting the inspectors focus suspiciously on the back halls rather than actual hearth safety. Cassian tails them to a handler node and visually confirms Daeron Vale as the active courier. Dain and Joryn approve an intercept — Cassian leads, Bren and Nyssa close off the lanes, and Daeron is captured alive with his papers intact. Darius loses both a mouth and a timetable in one move, and the Lantern gains the logistics chain tying committee pressure directly to Corvett estate operations.

Act VII — The Corvett Estate Confrontation

The crew refuses a conventional siege. Mark overchannels his Shadow magic for a one-time group transit, folding leadership and youth squad alike directly into Darius's own command interior — a sealed "shadow door" insertion that forces the truth to exist somewhere it can't be deferred any longer. Evidence is presented as a cascade: Elowen's memo, the inspection patterns, Daeron's logistics chain, the hook-mark schedules. Dain confronts Darius directly while Darian asserts, in front of everyone, what "house" means to him now — and refuses re-ownership outright. Darius is removed through proof and civic inevitability rather than open war: the committee's cover collapses, registry channels expose the coercion for what it is, and Corvett authority fractures in daylight.

Epilogue

The Lantern and Annex remain fully intact — Maera keeps the public face stable while the Annex stays the quiet engine underneath. Edrin and Tomas fight openly together as a couple within the crew, embodying the campaign's central promise that love doesn't have to hide to be protected. Dain and Joryn stabilise the city's narrative and keep the backlash from curdling into scapegoating or street war. Mark and Darian consolidate openly within the crew's reality — the relationship becomes a lived future rather than a stolen hour. With Darius removed, Corvett remnants, loyalists, and outside patrons remain a possible long-horizon threat, just without the committee's cover to hide behind anymore.

World State After Campaign B8

Geography. The Corvett Estate is confirmed as a location and site of confrontation; Evermere's civic institutions — registry, licensing, printshops — get significant new detail as the setting for this campaign's entirely paper-and-proof-based conflict.

Magic. Mark's Shadow Sorcery is used for a dramatic one-time group transit — the most significant demonstration yet of his training paying off since Campaign B7 sent him to the Umbral Collegium.

The Durrel Line. Not directly involved this campaign — the story stays centred on the Lantern/Corvett/Valmere conflict rather than Durrel Ridge, though the B7 pact presumably remains in effect in the background.

Institutions. Evermere's audit-war tactic is neutralised, at least locally, by making it public through legitimate channels rather than force. Corvett power itself is redefined by this campaign's ending — a future arc is opened where "House Corvett" becomes a portable political identity rather than a single fixed estate.
