Timeline B · Campaign B8

The Corvett Reckoning

Evermere Era

86 ARV Primary location: Evermere, Corvett Estate

Core Premise

Same Evermere era as Campaign B7 — The Lantern established, Mark now a trusted youth-squad lead. A covert "stewardship audit" apparatus begins mapping the Lantern's physical layout: not a raid, but classification — learn who reacts, then crush them through civic channels rather than open confrontation.

Party & Key Figures

  • Mark "Mark Val" (Marcus Valmere) Alive; consolidates relationship with Darian
    Now a trusted youth-squad lead, using Shadow magic as a discipline rather than a spectacle after his Umbral Collegium training.
  • Elowen Marr Alive; testifies against the network
    Relief Registry steward; the crucial "flip" who agrees to testify against the audit network.
  • Darius Corvett Removed via civic proof, not violence
    Patriarch and steering hand behind the civic pressure network — the campaign's primary antagonist.
  • Daeron Vale Exposed alongside Darius
    Courier and logistics link between the audit network and the Corvett estate.

Act-by-Act

The Measured Walls

The crew discovers evidence of covert surveying at the Lantern and traces it into Evermere's civic lanes — confirming someone is building a slow-pressure machine meant to strangle the Lantern without open violence, continuing the "paper kills more than unsanctioned murders" ethic established in Campaign B7.

The Hook-Mark Network

Mark penetrates the "stewardship" layer and identifies a covert network operating through civic language — audits, licensing delays, and reputational corrosion rather than steel.

Proof Acquisition and the Flip

The crew seizes physical evidence from a shuttered printshop, then flips registry steward Elowen Marr, who agrees to testify in exchange for guarantees protecting Evermere's stability.

The Corvett Estate Confrontation

Rather than a siege, Mark overchannels his Shadow magic for a one-time group transit directly into Darius's command interior — a dramatic use of the very training the Umbral Collegium gave him in Campaign B7. Evidence is presented as a cascade, and Darius is removed through proof and civic inevitability rather than open war.

World State After Campaign B8

Geography. The Corvett Estate is confirmed as a location and site of confrontation; Evermere's civic institutions (registry, licensing, printshops) get significant new detail as the setting for this campaign's entirely paper-and-proof-based conflict.

Magic. Mark's Shadow Sorcery is used for a dramatic one-time group transit — the most significant demonstration yet of his training paying off since Campaign B7 sent him to the Umbral Collegium.

The Durrel Line. Not directly involved this campaign — the story stays centred on the Lantern/Corvett/Valmere conflict rather than Durrel Ridge, though the B7 pact presumably remains in effect in the background.

Institutions. Evermere's audit-war tactic is neutralized by making it public through legitimate channels. The Lantern and Annex remain intact, Edrin and Tomas fight together openly as a couple, and Mark and Darian consolidate their relationship within the crew's reality. Corvett remnants and external patrons remain a long-horizon threat, now without the committee's cover.

Canon status: follows the source account directly.
