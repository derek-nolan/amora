Timeline B · Campaign B8

The Corvett Reckoning

Evermere Era

86 ARV Primary location: Evermere, Corvett Estate

Core Premise

Same Evermere era as B7 — the Lantern established, Mark now a trusted youth-squad lead. A covert "stewardship audit" apparatus begins mapping the Lantern's physical layout: not a raid, but classification — learn who reacts, then crush them through civic channels.

Party & Key Figures

  • Mark "Mark Val" (Marcus Valmere)
    Now a trusted youth-squad lead, using Shadow magic as a discipline rather than a spectacle. Emotional stake remains Darian Corvett.
  • Elowen Marr
    Relief Registry steward; the crucial "flip" who agrees to testify against the audit network.
  • Darius Corvett
    Patriarch and steering hand behind the civic pressure network — the campaign's primary antagonist.
  • Daeron Vale
    Courier and logistics link between the audit network and the Corvett estate.

Act-by-Act

The Measured Walls

The crew discovers evidence of covert surveying at the Lantern and traces it into Evermere's civic lanes — confirming someone is building a slow-pressure machine meant to strangle the Lantern without open violence.

The Hook-Mark Network

Mark penetrates the "stewardship" layer and identifies a covert network operating through civic language — audits, licensing delays, and reputational corrosion rather than steel.

Proof Acquisition and the Flip

The crew seizes physical evidence from a shuttered printshop, then flips registry steward Elowen Marr, who agrees to testify in exchange for guarantees protecting Evermere's stability.

The Corvett Estate Confrontation

Rather than a siege, Mark overchannels Shadow magic for a one-time group transit directly into Darius's command interior. Evidence is presented as a cascade, and Darius is removed through proof and civic inevitability rather than open war.

Final World State

Evermere's audit-war tactic is neutralized by making it public through legitimate channels. The Lantern and Annex remain intact, Edrin and Tomas fight together openly as a couple, and Mark and Darian consolidate their relationship within the crew's reality. Corvett remnants and external patrons remain a long-horizon threat, now without the committee's cover.
