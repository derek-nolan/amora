Timeline B · Campaign B7

The Lantern Crew

Evermere Era

85 ARV Primary location: Evermere, Durrel Ridge

Core Premise

Roughly 80 years after Caen, Lio, and the execution of the Six. A grounded, classic-adventuring campaign of civic cleanup and family drama, built around a new youth squad growing up in The Lantern's orbit.

Party & Key Figures

  • Mark "Mark Val" (Marcus Valmere)
    Human Shadow Sorcerer; exiled from a minor Valmere branch after a forbidden relationship with Darian Corvett, Dain's younger brother. The player character of this campaign.
  • Youth Squad
    Mark, Bren, Tomas, Edrin, and Nyssa — the Lantern's next generation, growing from rookies to a trusted, competent crew.
  • Lantern Leadership
    Dain ("Daeron") Corvett, Joryn, Maera, Cassian, and Rafe, running Evermere's quiet-authority civic operation.
  • Durrel Ridge
    Seren Durrel, Caelan Durrel, Steward Alren Durrel, Clerk Iven Marr, Captain Haleven, and Orren Vey — the Heartwarden seat that becomes a standing ally.

Act-by-Act

Mark Arrives

Mark reaches The Lantern under an alias, is tested and accepted by Maera, and begins ordinary jobs alongside Bren to earn the crew's trust — including learning not to panic-cast his Shadow magic.

Evermere Cleanup

The Lantern Crew commits to cutting off corrupt harbour chains and underworks smuggling. The approach becomes consistent: paper kills more than unsanctioned murders. The crew converts a warehouse into the Lantern Annex to grow without destabilising the public tavern.

Family Drama and Lucan's Return

Mark's exile story surfaces — he was to be married into Corvett politics while secretly loving Darian Corvett. Dain chooses to protect him rather than use him as leverage. Lucan resurfaces, targeting Mark with a conditioning "comfort ritual"; the crew intervenes with containment rather than violence.

Durrel Ridge Re-enters Amora

A Durrel cousin, Caelan, arrives to reclaim a stolen Heartwarden relic. The Lantern secures it, escorts it to Durrel Ridge, and forms a mutual-aid pact between the two houses. Mark's Shadow power later flares out of control, sending him to the Umbral Collegium in Gloamford for formal training.

The Ridge Leak (Finale)

Durrel Ridge calls in the pact when a route leak starts getting couriers taken alive. The Lantern closes the leak through a sting, exposing corrupted Ridge staff and proving the pact real through action.

Final World State

Evermere is clean, the Durrel Ridge pact is proven active and callable, and the youth squad has become competent and trusted. Mark's Shadow Sorcery is stabilised through training rather than suppressed, and his relationship with Darian Corvett continues as an ongoing choice under family pressure — set up as a live thread for future campaigns.

Canon status: the Durrel Ridge cast (Seren, Caelan, Alren, and the founding Tarenus) appear as "Durell" in the source material — normalized to Durrel here per the site's spelling decision.
