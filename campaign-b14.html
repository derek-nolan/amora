Timeline B · Campaign B14

The Bastion Reclaimed

Silver Bastion Reclamation Era

Age of Founding, Reforged

Late c. 162 ARV Primary locations: Silver Bastion, Highever Lower District, Durrel Ridge

Core Premise

Silver Bastion remains under Veil occupation, remade into the Veiled Citadel since Campaign B11's fall. Its sacred wards have been turned against visible oath-light, preventing any ordinary paladin-led infiltration — Cameron rules through the fortress's corrupted ward-anchor, and Gavren serves as the living proof that the Bastion has accepted its conquest. Durrel Ridge prepares a full reclamation, but the assault needs someone who can enter through the occupied lower district, move through pleasure-house and servant networks, survive without formal oath-light, and reopen the fortress from within. That person is Michael "Mike" Rookvale — and the very traits society might use to dismiss him become the means by which Silver Bastion is reclaimed.

Party & Key Figures

  • Michael "Mike" Rookvale PC Human rogue and swashbuckler, former full-time pleasure-house worker, fixer, thief, and information broker. Son of Tomas and Elian Rookvale, two Ridge clerics killed in Silver Bastion's earlier fall. Unsworn — never becomes a paladin or cleric, but ends the campaign a formally recognised field scout of Durrel Ridge.
  • Evan Marric
    Durrel Ridge scout; Mike's infiltration partner, immediate field superior, and eventual romantic partner.
  • Jack Durrel
    Warden of Meaning; plans the reclamation, restores Silver Bastion's ward-anchor, and formally names Mike a Ridge scout.
  • Taren Durrel
    Commands the main Ridge assault and holds the chapel approach.
  • Caelen Durrel & Corvin Hale
    Clear the lower cells and Veil remaking sites; help break Lucien's final trap; marry in the Ridge sun chapel at campaign's end.
  • Dena
    Protects survivors, organises evacuation, food, and survival; becomes one of Mike's closest connections at the Ridge.
  • Harlan
    Surviving ward-priest and keeper of the last clean Saint-Ward.
  • Lucien Varric
    Velvet Gate broker, Officer Chain operator, and Mike's primary personal antagonist. Killed by Mike in Act IV, but leaves a final trap behind.
  • Cameron & Gavren
    The Veiled Citadel's effective ruler and his ward-knight, carried from Campaign B11. Both killed during the reclamation.

Act-by-Act

Act I — The Velvet Gate and the Night Gate Key

Mike begins undercover at the Velvet Gate with Captain Orlan Vey, an occupation officer with access to Silver Bastion. Playing the expected role convincingly, he drugs Vey's wine and steals his Night Gate ward-key strip, black pass-token, and officer signet. Vey reveals that the Night Gate rotation can be exploited and that the Bastion's Saint-Wards have been altered to reject or burn visible oath-light — and that the Officer Chain operating through the occupied fortress connects back to Lucien Varric. Lucien confronts Mike after the operation and attempts to influence him through a conditioning working that amplifies attraction and the feeling of being understood; Mike recognises the influence and escapes, reaching a Ridge safehouse and reporting the full truth of what happened rather than concealing it.

Act II — Jack, Evan, and the Infiltration Team

At the safehouse, Mike meets Evan Marric, the Ridge scout assigned to accompany him inside. Mira Tallow arrives with word from Harlan, a surviving ward-priest imprisoned beneath the fortress — the last clean Saint-Ward survives, but it can't be carried through the Bastion by someone bearing ordinary oath-light. Mike reveals he's Light-aligned by choice but has never sworn a formal oath. Jack recognises the names of Mike's parents, Tomas and Elian Rookvale, confirms both died helping civilians during the Bastion's fall, and promises Mike work, training, pay, protection, and a genuine place to live if he survives and wants it. Mike, Evan, and Mira adopt pleasure-house staff cover and cross the lower district using Mike's professional confidence and Vey's stolen signet.

Act III — Cook Ansel, Harlan, and the Last Clean Saint-Ward

Inside the Wash Hall, the group meets Cook Ansel, who has quietly protected servants, prisoners, and internal loyalists throughout the occupation — and who remembers Elian Rookvale holding the chapel stairs while civilians escaped. In the Lower Refectory, Mike manipulates Quartermaster Pell through flirtation and Pell's own resentment of Lucien, opening the route beneath the wine store. Mike kills the reliquary guards, frees Harlan, and conceals the bodies to delay discovery. Harlan leads him to the last clean Saint-Ward, and Mike agrees to carry it only after Harlan promises it won't claim, bind, convert, or oath-bind him. The Saint-Ward recognises him anyway — Michael Rookvale: free man, unsworn bearer, Light-aligned by choice — and Silver Bastion's hidden servant-light paths reopen through him, establishing that Light allegiance never required social purity or formal status.

Act IV — The Servant Paths and the Death of Lucien

Mike uses the awakened servant paths to move through the occupied lower Bastion and discovers civilians trapped behind guarded dormitory barriers. Rather than taking the fastest route toward the breach, he stops to free them, killing the guards and sending Evan, Mira, Harlan, and the rescued civilians toward safety while he stays behind to prevent pursuit. Lucien confronts him in the servant passages and tries to frame Mike's own desires as proof that Mike belongs to him; Mike rejects the claim outright — being understood is not the same as being owned — wounds Lucien's ring hand, destroys the conditioning working binding it, and kills him. Among Lucien's effects: correspondence, a private-house key, officer debts, and a note reading "The Bastion must be opened by the unwanted, not the pure." Lucien dies before he can become Mike's long-term handler or antagonist.

Act V — The Eastern Breach and the Reclamation

Mike reaches the eastern ambulatory carrying the Saint-Ward's Light and opens the true breach from within. Durrel Ridge launches the main assault — Taren holds the chapel approach, Dena organises evacuation and survival, Caelen and Corvin clear the lower cells and Veil remaking sites, Mara controls routes and captured records, Edran supports the practical ward work, and Jack advances toward the central ward-anchor while Mike kills occupation soldiers from the shadows to let Ridge forces establish control. In the ward chamber, Mike kills Gavren, removing Cameron's living proof of conquest; Jack reaches the ward-anchor and identifies the Veiled Citadel's imposed identity as false, while Mike kills Cameron during the restoration. A white-gold eruption breaks the occupation wards and drives surviving Veil power back toward Brevair. The Veiled Citadel ends. Silver Bastion is reclaimed.

Act VI — Ridge Scout and the Fleeing Command

Jack formally names Mike a field scout of Durrel Ridge — a choice, not a religious conversion, bloodline obligation, or debt — with Evan as his immediate field superior. The pair pursue the fleeing occupation officers, ward-priests, and command carriers; Mike follows Evan's commands in the field, including taking an officer alive when intelligence proves more useful than immediate execution. The fleeing column is broken, enemy ward-priests are killed, the command case is captured, and no important carrier escapes the reclamation route toward Brevair.

Act VII — Lucien's Final Trap

After the victory, Mike and Evan enter an old private side room inside the Bastion, containing a single working Lucien had prepared specifically for Mike before his death — meant to let Lucien survive long enough to claim him afterward and occupy the handler role himself. With Lucien already dead, the working still activates, catching Evan on one side of the structure and Mike on the other. It uses real attraction, exhaustion, and genuine preferred dynamics between them as its entry point, magnifying Mike's own wish to submit into something imposed rather than chosen, and Evan's protective instincts into something possessive and commanding rather than freely offered — neither man is choosing this state freely. Jack and Caelen find them before it fully sets. Caelen recognises the working is using the appearance of consent to disguise compulsion on both sides; Jack anchors Mike through his name and his freedom, Caelen breaks the active structure, and Dena and Edran oversee the full cleansing. It's confirmed as the only working of its kind inside Silver Bastion, built specifically for Mike — and both men are treated as victims of it, not participants in it. Nothing of it remains after the cleansing.

Act VIII — Durrel Ridge, Home, and the Wedding

Mike chooses to see Evan again, refusing to let Lucien's trap define them — lightly calling the disastrous encounter an unexpected first date and asking how they'll possibly top it next time. He returns to the Ridge as a recognised scout and gradually becomes part of the household: Caelen teaches him to operate within a military unit without losing his own methods, Corvin helps him learn to fight around armoured formations, Mara values his knowledge of unofficial routes, Edran trades dry banter with him, and Jack gives him real work rather than treating him as a damaged dependent. Dena's kitchen becomes his favourite place — caught taking food, she simply invites him to sit and eat instead, and through her and Ansel he learns more about his mother. Evan assigns him his parents' former quarters — hearth, sleeping alcove, courtyard window, writing desk, a small sunburst scratched near the hearthstone — and reveals he's deliberately moved his own room next door, giving Mike a spare key. Mike suggests that one day they could knock through the shared wall. Caelen and Corvin marry in the Ridge sun chapel before the gathered household, scouts, paladins, and Bastion survivors; during the celebration, Mike and Evan kiss openly, and Evan realises Mike trusts him completely. The campaign closes with Silver Bastion reclaimed, Caelen and Corvin married, and Mike imagining a home and a future with Evan.

World State After Campaign B14

Geography. Silver Bastion is fully reclaimed from Veil occupation and is no longer the Veiled Citadel; the eastern ambulatory is established as the successful internal breach point, and the servant-light paths reopen and return to Bastion control. The central ward-anchor is restored to the Light. Mike's parents' former rooms at Durrel Ridge are occupied again. Brevair remains intact and becomes the principal territorial concentration of surviving Veil power, absorbing what the white-gold eruption drove out of the Bastion.

Magic. Chosen Light allegiance without a formal oath is confirmed as magically meaningful — the Saint-Ward recognises Mike through name, consent, and chosen alignment, proving sacred recognition doesn't require institutional purity. Jack's Warden of Meaning role is shown capable of restoring the true identity of a corrupted sacred site outright. The ruler/ward-knight/ ward-anchor structure Cameron built in Campaign B11 is successfully broken. Lucien's final working demonstrates directly that both the person commanding and the person obeying can be magically compelled at once — the same B13 principle applied again: desire does not make hidden magical control consensual. His working is confirmed unique, destroyed, and leaves no active structure behind.

The Durrel Line. Durrel Ridge successfully projects military power beyond its own walls for the first time, becoming the force responsible for reclaiming the Light's lost sacred stronghold rather than simply defending its own. Jack's role as Warden of Meaning proves capable of restoring a corrupted sacred site's true identity, extending what the title was first shown to mean back in Campaign B12.

Institutions. Mike joins the Ridge scouts without erasing his background or swearing a religious oath — the clearest statement yet that the Ridge's continuity runs on chosen belonging rather than bloodline or doctrine. Lucien's Officer Chain and personal influence network are broken outright. Silver Bastion requires rebuilding, staffing, and continued cleansing, but its reclamation is complete, and the lower district's pleasure-houses and informal networks are not collectively treated as collaborators. The campaign's thematic core: the traits used to dismiss Mike — sex work, deception, theft, criminal access — are exactly what makes him capable of saving the Bastion, and his final victory is gaining a home, a family history, and love without surrendering ownership of his own life.

Canon status: follows the source account, with Act VII's terminology adjusted to keep the site's content at a general-audience level while preserving the coercion-vs-consent themes central to the story — the source material is more explicit in its depiction of the working's specific mechanics than reflected here.
