Timeline B · Campaign B14

Reclaiming Silver Bastion

Provisional — chat-derived record

c. 165–170 ARV (provisional) Primary location: Durrel Ridge, former Silver Bastion

Core Premise

A Ridge-led effort to reclaim the former Silver Bastion from the Veiled Citadel established in Campaign B11. Michael "Mike" Rookvale, a human rogue orphaned during a Light-Veil battle near the Bastion, is recruited for skills that work as well from within the fortress as from outside it.

Party & Key Figures

  • Michael "Mike" Rookvale
    Human Rogue (Swashbuckler); formerly a full-time sex worker, orphaned near Silver Bastion. The player character.
  • Evan
    A Ridge soldier caught in the same coercive magical trap as Mike; develops a genuine romance with him after recovery.
  • Lucien Varric
    Intelligence broker and intended dark romantic trap, his influence linked to magical attraction and coercive conditioning.

Act-by-Act

Recruitment

Mike is drawn into the Ridge-led effort to reclaim the Veiled Citadel, his street experience and ability to operate outside paladin expectations making him valuable for understanding the fortress from within.

Lucien's Trap

Lucien Varric attempts to turn intimacy and magical influence into a route of control; Evan is caught by the same architecture. The campaign treats both men as victims of coercive systems rather than reducing their actions to moral failure.

Reclamation

The campaign's central military objective succeeds: the Veiled Citadel is liberated and cleansed, restoring the former Bastion as a site the Light and the Ridge can inhabit again.

Final World State

Former Silver Bastion is reclaimed and no longer functions as the Veiled Citadel — proof that Durrel Ridge can organise a major regional liberation without losing its identity as a lived household. Mike receives a permanent home in his parents' old quarters beside Evan. The epilogue includes Caelen and Corvin's wedding, carrying B13's relationship into a stable shared future.
