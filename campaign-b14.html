Timeline B · Campaign B14

Reclaiming Silver Bastion

Provisional — chat-derived record

c. 165–170 ARV (provisional) Primary location: Durrel Ridge, former Silver Bastion

Core Premise

A Ridge-led effort to reclaim the former Silver Bastion — lost to Cameron's Veiled Citadel back in Campaign B11 — using the household and continuity Durrel Ridge has been building since Campaign B12. Michael "Mike" Rookvale, a human rogue orphaned during a Light-Veil battle near the Bastion, is recruited for skills that work as well from within the fortress as from outside it.

Party & Key Figures

  • Michael "Mike" Rookvale PC Alive; receives a permanent home at the Ridge
    Human Rogue (Swashbuckler); orphaned near Silver Bastion. The player character.
  • Evan Alive; partner to Mike
    A Ridge soldier caught in the same coercive magical trap as Mike; develops a genuine romance with him after recovery.
  • Lucien Varric Status unresolved — see Canon Ledger
    Intelligence broker whose influence is linked to magical attraction and coercive conditioning.

Act-by-Act

Recruitment

Mike is drawn into the Ridge-led effort to reclaim the Veiled Citadel, his street experience and ability to operate outside paladin expectations making him valuable for understanding the fortress from within — a very different skillset from the paladins and Wardens the Ridge has relied on since Campaign B9.

Lucien's Trap

Lucien Varric attempts to turn intimacy and magical influence into a route of control; Evan is caught by the same architecture. The campaign treats both men as victims of coercive systems rather than reducing their actions to moral failure — a continuation of the same lesson Campaign B13 established about conditioning and consent.

Reclamation

The campaign's central military objective succeeds: the Veiled Citadel is liberated and cleansed, restoring the former Bastion as a site the Light and the Ridge can inhabit again — closing the loss that's defined Timeline B's northern geography since Campaign B11.

World State After Campaign B14

Geography. Former Silver Bastion is reclaimed and no longer functions as the Veiled Citadel — the site returns to Light control for the first time since Campaign B11, roughly two decades after it fell.

Magic. No new magic system, but the campaign continues the pattern established since B5 of treating coercive magical control as a recurring threat to be recognised and undone rather than a one-time crisis — Lucien Varric's methods echo Cameron's from B11 and Seran's from B10.

The Durrel Line. Not a Durrel protagonist this time, but the campaign is entirely enabled by Durrel Ridge's institutional strength — proof that the household Jack built in Campaign B12 and the Vault security Caelen's arc secured in Campaign B13 gave the Ridge the stability needed to go on the offensive rather than just defend itself.

Institutions. Proof that Durrel Ridge can organise a major regional liberation without losing its identity as a lived household. Mike receives a permanent home in his parents' old quarters beside Evan. The epilogue includes Caelen and Corvin's wedding — carrying Campaign B13's relationship into a stable shared future, and closing Timeline B's current record on a note of restoration rather than crisis.
