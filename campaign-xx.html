Timeline A · Campaign XVIII

The Mage of the Red Veil

Age of Red Renewal

8500–8505 ARV Primary location: Evermere, the Archive of Glass

Opening World State

Directly after the Widow's Oath. Evermere's institutions exist but are brittle, longing for a new unifying rhythm. The cyber-law charters of the Concord Grids are long gone; the republics now survive by candlelight statute.

Party & Key Figures

  • Auren Lysar
    Collegium scholar; obedient vessel and Red Veil synchronist. Voluntarily sealed within the Dream of Red Glass as co-guardian.
  • Kael Verris
    Veil-adept and architect of command; citywide cadence setter. Mentor/lover dynamic with Auren; designs the Mirror of Law regimen.
  • Sorell Marcone
    Senior magistrate and conflicted mentor; maintains the republic under ritual calm after the sealing.
  • Thalen
    Council technocrat and seizure proponent, outmaneuvered when the Mirror of Law takes hold.
  • Iree
    Councillor-agitator and counterfeit resonance tester; spared to preserve dissent within the new order.
  • The Red Veil
    A sentient ley current, awakening to dream and desire; learns via Auren and Kael's synchrony.

Act-by-Act

A Private Ley-Thread

Auren Lysar and Kael Verris uncover a private ley-thread beneath the Collegium Vaults. Their synchrony calls forth the Red Veil, a living current that learns through them.

The Mirror of Law

What begins as study becomes governance. Rewriting the city's Aegis Writ, they forge the Mirror of Law — a crimson lens that transforms obedience into civic order. Evermere's people fall into perfect rhythm.

The Oath in Red Light

As their influence spreads, devotion becomes dominion. The Council yields under an Oath in Red Light binding every ration and pardon to their cadence — until the Veil begins to dream through them, and they understand the price of control.

Sealing the Dream

To spare the waking world from perfection, Auren and Kael descend into the Dream of Red Glass, sealing themselves within it. Evermere wakes free — calm, imperfect, alive.

Final World State

The Mirror of Law remains, functioning as civic metronome rather than will. Evermere is unified under a single humane charter; dissent survives under ritual courtesy. Auren and Kael are remembered as the Red Glass Guardians — saints of balance through surrender, a new echo of Crown and Consort who voluntarily stepped offstage.
