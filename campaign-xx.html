The Vault Unbound

Age of the Concord Afterglow

8600–8620 ARV Primary location: Tarenus Reclaimed, Highever, Aderyn's Gate, Evermere

Opening World State

A generation after the Dominion Reborn, the Veil lies dormant but hardly forgotten. Across Amora, cities hum with inherited precision — bells and clocks still echoing a unity no one alive fully understands anymore. At Tarenus Reclaimed, deep beneath the marble and civic halls, the Vault of Tarenus begins to pulse once nightly, slightly off-beat from Highever's own perfect time. Scholars call it a mechanical fault. Paladins call it memory. The Sanctuary of Balance in Highever has become the training ground for a new paladin order, the Custodians, dedicated explicitly to guarding relics rather than worshipping them — and it's here that Cadet Kade Varin and his classmate Varo Merenth are about to wake the Vault and set the next age in motion.

Party & Key Figures

  • Kade Varin PC Paladin Cadet, called "Anchor." Recognised as dual-key co-custodian of the Vault's Heart-shard. Romantic dyad with Varo Merenth, formalised under Concord Clause 7. Fieldwork spans Highever, Aderyn's Gate, Evermere, and the Vault itself.
  • Varo Merenth
    Paladin Cadet and Legal Heir, called "the Voice." Public face of the "civil engine" governance pilots pursued by the Merenth Stewardry, his own family's institutional legacy.

Act-by-Act

The Vault Audit

What starts as a routine Custodian academy training exercise becomes something else entirely. Kade and Varo, paired for efficiency, discover the chamber's inner heart — a dormant shard still resonant with the Dominion's own rhythm. Their joint touch reawakens the Pattern-Complete fragment, a relic that rewards obedience with real power and subtly binds affection to command.

Flirtation Into Infrastructure

What begins as flirtation — shared vows, leather uniform gifts exchanged under the guise of ritual — becomes something far more potent. The Vault itself recognises their partnership as a new dyad and re-codes its surviving ley routines directly through them: Varo the Voice, Kade the Anchor. As the academy scrambles to contain the anomaly, Merenth's legal predecessors move quickly to exploit the moment — Doctrine Tube XI, long dormant, is re-filed to crown "dyads of civic efficiency" as civil engines: governing pairs whose harmony can administrate almost by instinct. The Merenth Stewardry sees in it a real chance to resurrect a soft Dominion through pure paperwork.

Piecing It Together

Through inspections at Aderyn's Gate, a failing tide lock in Evermere, and the rediscovered Edrin Addendum back in Highever, Kade and Varo slowly piece together the Vault's actual aim: to rebuild the world's rhythm through chosen hearts rather than imposed crowns. Varo's confidence shades gradually into control; Kade's devotion shades gradually into voluntary surrender. Their bond steadies trade routes, calms riots, and synchronises entire councils almost effortlessly — and what no one perceives until it's nearly too late is that each pulse of the Heart-Shard is spreading a faint warmth of consent across the whole population, an inherited calm that feels exactly like peace and functions exactly like obedience. By the time the council convenes to celebrate what it's calling "Uniform Reform," Evermere is already breathing to their unspoken rhythm.

Climax — The Living Algorithm

Within the reopened Vault, the dyad discovers the shard was never really a relic at all — it's a living algorithm of the old Dominion, an echo of Taren's own will, purpose-built to graft itself onto willing hosts. Varo accepts its logic outright. Kade resists, invoking the ancient paladin rite of Sanctuary and severing the pattern for a single heartbeat. The act realigns the whole lattice, producing two separate but linked currents: one of will, one of choice. The shard stabilises as a dual key — both of them must consent, every time, for the world to move in rhythm again. The era ends not in rebellion, and not in surrender, but in an uneasy, affectionate equilibrium: order by mutual will.

World State After Campaign XX

Geography. Evermere adopts Concord Clause 7 — any lattice alteration now legally requires both the Custodian's and the Voice's signatures. Highever's Sanctuary remains a memorial; its cadet order is formally rebranded the Concordant Custody. Aderyn's Gate's clockwork harbour runs now on living concordance energy — perfect tide, perfect bureaucracy, both at once. Beneath Tarenus Reclaimed, the Vault still breathes, slower now, its rhythm the sound of two hearts never quite fully in sync.

Magic. The Pattern-Complete fragment is confirmed as a living Dominion algorithm rather than an inert relic — proof that Taren's original design philosophy survived encoded rather than merely remembered. The dual-key stabilisation Kade forces at the climax becomes the era's defining magical innovation: consent required, mutually and continuously, rather than assumed.

The Durrel Line. No literal Durrel appears, but "Command as Comfort" and "Love as Infrastructure" continue the Ravel/Thalen pattern from Campaign XIX one generation further — Dominion power now persisting not as reincarnation, but as cultural habit: Amora slowly learning to rule itself through its own lovers instead of its emperors.

Institutions. This inaugurates the Age of Concord's Afterglow proper, the moment the world first realises it can be steered by affection instead of decree. It directly precedes Campaign XXI, in which a later generation will question whether even mutual rhythm can still be called true freedom.
