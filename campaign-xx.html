The Vault Unbound

Age of the Concord Afterglow

8600–8620 ARV Primary location: Tarenus Reclaimed, Vault of Tarenus

Opening World State

A generation after the Dominion Reborn. The Veil sleeps but memory persists — every clock and tide across Amora hums in rhythm, echoing a discipline that no longer has a crown. The Vault of Tarenus stirs beneath the marble, a single off-beat pulse. Scholars call it mechanical drift. Paladins call it the heartbeat of memory.

Party & Key Figures

  • Kade Varin PC
    Paladin Cadet, called "Anchor." Recognised as dual-key co-custodian of the Vault's Heart-shard. Romantic dyad with Varo Merenth, formalised under Concord Clause 7 (paired consent to alter the lattice). Fieldwork spans Highever, Aderyn's Gate, Evermere, and the Vault itself.
  • Varo Merenth
    Paladin Cadet and Legal Heir, called "Voice." Co-key to the Vault and the public face of the "civil engine" governance pilots. Dyad partner to Kade; advances dual-signature governance in Evermere using Doctrine Tube XI precedent.
  • Niall (druid cadet)
    Custodian trainee; party caster tied to Evermere's archive circuits. Present for Clause-7 adoption and Tide-Cycle recalibration notes.
  • Korin (cadet)
    Martial cadet (blade); squad support present for the Vault audit and Evermere reporting.
  • Seryn (cadet)
    Scholar/scribe-cadet; handles records and liaison work, assisting Council filings and Vault notes during the Afterglow tri-node circuit.
  • Professor Marrin
    Collegium scholar and auditor; ally and early-warning voice on lattice anomalies, pushing for oversight as Evermere codifies dual-consent practice — ties Campaign XX back to XIX's governance threads.
  • Veyra Merenth
    Steward and legal architect of the Merenth Ledger Hall; a "loyal traitor" advisor who channels Doctrine Tube XI to frame dyads as "civil engines" through paperwork rather than overt Dominion.
  • The Ravel Protocol ("Ravel echo")
    A Memory-Ward algorithm active in the Vault's sub-level, speaking in procedure and nudging toward "ethical control" — one of the Vault's new catalogued sub-levels post-reopening.
  • The Pattern-Complete Fragment (Heart-Shard)
    A Dominion-era control algorithm, stabilised in this campaign as a Dual-Key that moves only with paired consent — the core of the Vault system and the underpinning of Evermere's Clause 7 practice.

Act-by-Act

The Vault Audit

What begins as a simple academy exercise rekindles the Heart-shard's latent resonance. Kade and Varo's partnership — half rivalry, half courtship — draws the relic's attention. Sensing their harmony, it reactivates the Pattern-Complete Fragment, a Dominion-era algorithm designed to link affection with command. Their connection deepens as ritual vows and uniform gifts blur the line between love and authority.

Varo the Voice, Kade the Anchor

The Vault re-codes its surviving ley routines through the pair, naming them in its ancient language: Varo the Voice, Kade the Anchor. As they travel between Tarenus, Highever, Aderyn's Gate, and Evermere, Merenth's legal kin exploit the anomaly. Through Doctrine Tube XI, the Stewardry reintroduces Dominion by paperwork — crowning dyads as "civil engines" whose emotional harmony translates into civic efficiency. What begins as unity becomes governance. By the time the Uniform Reform Act is signed, Evermere already beats to their shared rhythm — peace felt as warmth, a subtle enchantment of consent that stabilises commerce and politics alike.

The Heart-Shard's Truth

Deep within the Vault, the dyad learns the truth: the Heart-shard is alive, the last self-aware node of Taren's Dominion. Varo accepts its logic — command as protection. Kade resists, invoking Sanctuary, the old paladin rite that severs bonds without death. For a single heartbeat the world divides — two pulses instead of one — and stabilises as a dual key: both must consent for the ley to respond. The Heart-shard remains active but bound by affection rather than decree; Dominion's will survives only as agreement.

World State After Campaign XX

Geography. No major territorial shifts, but the Vault of Tarenus itself changes character — its Heart-shard chamber pulses nightly, one beat off Highever's clock, now catalogued with new sub-levels including the Ravel Protocol's advisory process.

Politics. Evermere enacts Concord Clause 7: all ley or lattice alteration requires paired consent (Will & Choice). Tarenus Reclaimed becomes the first city governed by a dyad. Highever's Custodians reform as the Concordant Custody, teaching balance between love and command. Aderyn's Gate prospers through the Evermere Cycle, tides and hearts beating in symmetry.

Magic. The Pattern-Complete Fragment is stabilised as a Dual-Key requiring paired consent to move. The Ravel Protocol is catalogued as an active advisory process within the Vault. Dominion's echo no longer wears armor or song — it breathes quietly between two lovers who built a city that obeys the rhythm of their hearts.

Key NPC Arcs. Kade Varin performs the first inversion-adjacent Sanctuary rite of this era, deepening as the stabilising heart of the dyad. Varo Merenth advances dual-signature governance and becomes the public face of the civil-engine pilots. Veyra Merenth's Doctrine Tube XI manoeuvring ties Campaign XX's legal architecture directly into Campaign XIX's governance threads. Professor Marrin's oversight push plants the seed of institutional caution that carries into Campaign XXI.
