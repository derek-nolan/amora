Timeline A · Campaign XX

The Vault Unbound

Age of the Concord Afterglow

8600–8620 ARV Primary location: Tarenus Reclaimed, Vault of Tarenus

Opening World State

A generation after the Dominion Reborn. The Veil sleeps but memory persists — every clock and tide across Amora hums in rhythm, echoing a discipline that no longer has a crown. The Vault of Tarenus stirs beneath the marble, a single off-beat pulse.

Party & Key Figures

  • Kade Varin
    Paladin Cadet ("Anchor"); recognised as dual-key co-custodian of the Vault's Heart-shard. Romantic dyad with Varo Merenth.
  • Varo Merenth
    Paladin Cadet and Legal Heir ("Voice"); public face of the 'civil engine' governance pilots. Dyad partner to Kade.
  • Niall (druid cadet), Korin (cadet), Seryn (cadet)
    Custodian trainees supporting the Vault expedition and Evermere reporting.
  • Professor Marrin

  • Veyra Merenth

Act-by-Act

The Vault Audit

What begins as a simple academy exercise between Kade and Varo rekindles the Heart-shard's latent resonance. The relic reactivates the Pattern-Complete Fragment — a Dominion-era algorithm designed to link affection with command.

Varo the Voice, Kade the Anchor

Ritual vows and uniform gifts blur the line between love and authority as the Vault re-codes its surviving ley routines through them. Merenth's legal kin exploit the anomaly, reintroducing Dominion by paperwork through Doctrine Tube XI.

The Heart-Shard's Truth

Deep in the Vault, the dyad learns the Heart-shard is alive — the last self-aware node of Taren's Dominion. Varo accepts its logic of command as protection; Kade resists, invoking Sanctuary, the old rite that severs bonds without death.

A Dual Key

For a single heartbeat the world divides into two pulses instead of one, then stabilises as a dual key: both must consent for the ley to respond. The Heart-shard remains active, but bound by affection rather than decree.

Final World State

Evermere enacts Concord Clause 7: all ley or lattice alteration requires paired consent. Tarenus Reclaimed becomes the first city governed by a dyad. The Vault is unbound but re-chartered — stronger, more transparent rules and safeguards placed around Durrel relics and echo-engines. The world leaves the era with more knowledge and more risk.
