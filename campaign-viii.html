Timeline A · Campaign VIII

The Codifier of Order

Age of Theocracy II

c. 5900 ARV Primary location: The Gate

Opening World State

Still the Theocracy Age, but later — the Gate is now a city of laws layered upon laws. Sirian inherits a theocracy already convinced of its moral purpose, and sets out to complete the Gate Codex.

Party & Key Figures

  • Sirian
    Codifies the Gate Codes and hardens order, believing a complete Codex will make the system fair rather than arbitrary.
  • Alaric
    Paladin of Light, married to Lorian; lives inside the consequences of Sirian's ambition.
  • Lorian
    Scholar of Silence, married to Alaric.

Act-by-Act

A Codex for Fairness

Sirian doesn't see himself as a tyrant — he sees inconsistency and arbitrary judgement, and believes a complete Gate Codex will fix it. Alaric and Lorian live the consequences as faith, marriage, and scholarship are all forced into legal categories.

Drafting and Weaponisation

The campaign follows the drafting, interpretation, and weaponisation of the Gate Codes. Every clarification removes one official's discretion while giving the state another permanent precedent.

Clear Law, Not Merciful Law

Sirian hardens order in the name of purity; Alaric and Lorian confront how easily intimate loyalty can be made evidence, leverage, or heresy. The age proves that clear law is not automatically merciful law.

Final World State

The Codex outlives its authors and the theocracy itself — quoted for generations by judges, inquisitors, reformers, and tyrants alike. Later ages read this as the ultimate cautionary tale about "neutral" law: discipline can be a prettier word for control.
