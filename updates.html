Site Updates

A running record of what's been added, corrected, or rebuilt on this site, newest first. This tracks the site itself — new campaigns, new pages, structural fixes. For in-world naming variants and settled ambiguities, see the Canon Ledger instead.

9 September

Cartographic correction: Corvane and the Greywake Range

  • A duplicate-capital error surfaced during a continuity check against the corrected B22 Atlas material: earlier B22 map work treated "Imperial Capital" and Corvane as though they might be two separate Brevair settlements. Corvane is confirmed as Brevair's one established capital — older maps' generic "Imperial Capital" label refers to the same city — and the Greywake Range is confirmed to sit entirely within north-western Brevair.
  • Both B22 map files are replaced with corrected versions: the full survey used by the Atlas's interactive viewer and the simplified map on the homepage.
  • The Atlas's Greywake Range description and its B10 Brevair chronology row are reworded to remove the implied second capital; Campaign B10's equivalent note is corrected the same way.
  • The Canon Ledger's "Corvane" entry — previously filed as a purely site-invented placeholder name — is superseded by a new Confirmed Canon entry locking Corvane as Brevair's actual capital; the original entry is kept on record, marked superseded, rather than removed.
  • Timeline B, Chronology, Campaign B22, Characters, Lore, Magic Codex, Factions, Relics, and the search index all used Corvane and Greywake narratively without encoding the error, and needed no changes.
9 September

Campaign B22: The Measure Holds

  • Campaign B22 — The Measure Holds added: set later c. 343 ARV, after Campaign B20 in the modern Age of Rediscovery — not immediately after B21, which remains a separate deep-history prequel. Corvane-born paladin Elias "Eli" Renn is drawn into conflict with the Radiant Office, a covert network using collars and artificial Pale Light to suppress Shadow responses; the campaign confirms the long-unlocated Shadow Great Seal at Vesperhold, intact, before ending at Eli's death holding its emergency closure.
  • Timeline B's Great Seal Arc extended through B22 and its introduction updated for the Corvane disaster and the Shadow Seal's recovery; Chronology gained a B22 row at later c. 343 ARV and its campaign count advanced to 47.
  • Characters gained a new B22 campaign filter tile, seven full profiles — Eli Renn, Tavian Rell, Seris Vale, Lucan Veyr, Joss Arlen, Perrin Vos, and Caer Veylan — and eight supporting entries, bringing the jump-index to 120 profiles.
  • The Atlas gained the new Greywake Range region — Brannoch, Caereth Peak and the High Orrery, and Vesperhold — plus the Corvane Disaster's permanent scars (the Rupture Basin, a fracture corridor, and the Fallen Guardian) and six new Corvane sub-locations, all pending a dedicated post-B22 map redraw noted directly on the map caption.
  • Lore's Great Seals entry gained a B22 paragraph on an intact Seal in a failing world around it; the Magic Codex gained a rewritten Shadow Seal entry, an extended Light Seal note, and three new sections — Pale Light and the Radiant Office's collars, Corvane's Shadow and Light manifestations as pressure rather than the Seals themselves, and the unresolved amber Conduit phenomenon.
  • Factions gained two new B22 entries, the Radiant Office and Dane House; Relics & Artefacts gained one new relic-site entry, the Shadow Great Seal at Vesperhold.
  • The Canon Ledger gained a new B22 locked-canon entry (Shadow Seal intact at Vesperhold, Eli not a Shadow Warden, Pale Light separated from Luminara and the Light Seal, Corvane's manifestations as pressure not Seals, Veylan as senior architect with no hidden superior mastermind, Tavian's consent/conditioning split) and a new open-threads entry (surviving Office scale, Rowan Kess's condition, the amber Conduit's nature, black-gold, and the surviving party's post-B22 paths).
  • The homepage's stats updated for a 47th campaign, 36 factions, and 24 relics; its Timeline B stamp now reads through later c. 343 ARV and Campaign B22.
23 August

Post-B21 canon audit: reference additions

  • A second, deeper audit against the full Volume II source material found the B20/B21 pages fundamentally sound, but surfaced worthwhile material that was correctly absent rather than contradictory — now promoted into the reference pages.
  • The Atlas's 343 ARV map is now explicitly confirmed as remaining valid through Campaign B20; Nareth Isle's Receiving House IV entry now covers its two-stage quarantine-then-severance history, with a new Hall of Soundings entry alongside it; Evermere gains a new River Office sub-location for Sella Morren's disappearance; and Greymount gains a short note on the four confirmed Ancient Way relation points (Greymount, Highever, Evermere, Nareth).
  • Lore gains a new Whispering Frost entry, covering Cairn Morren's history with the pact and Alec Verran's current pact-holder status — while explicitly ruling out any identification with the Frost Great Seal, the Icewardens, the Ancient Ways, or Jarek Flint's black-gold entity.
  • The Magic Codex's Ancient Ways section now records Amora's two moons, adds Sella Morren and Derran Voel as concrete route-state examples alongside Mera Vann, and gains a new entry on Edmund Shaw's forced-selection apparatus as a coercive modification rather than original Way machinery.
  • Factions gains an optional Belisar Civic Authority card covering Letha Marr, Nera Voss, and the Hall of Soundings — kept distinct from the Circle and House Saye.
  • Relics & Artefacts required no changes; the audit found nothing missing there.
23 August

Post-B21 canon audit: cross-page corrections

  • The Magic Codex's B19 Storm Walker section no longer reads as a present-tense contradiction of Ben's B20 Storm Warden recognition — it now explicitly frames "not yet recognised" as B19's own endpoint and points forward to the B20 section.
  • Timeline B's Great Seal Arc heading updated from "Campaigns B16–B19" to "Campaigns B16–B21," with the arc's introduction extended to actually describe B20 and B21.
  • Silas Kern's label corrected on Characters and in the search index — he's a Warlock bonded to a separate amber-working patron, not the patron himself.
  • Chronology's "Two Threads" intro corrected from a stale 44 to the current 46 campaigns.
  • The Canon Ledger's B15 entry no longer names a specific stale endpoint campaign for "Timeline B has since continued" — reworded to stay accurate as the site keeps growing.
  • The Factions Seal Wardens entry now distinguishes the still-unrecovered ancient Storm Warden lineage from Benjamin Strake's current, directly-recognised office after B20.
23 August

Campaign B21: Those Left Beyond

  • Campaign B21 — Those Left Beyond added: a deep-history prequel in the Great Seal Arc, c. -928 ARV, roughly ten years after B18 and played after B20 without touching the modern present. Circle runner Alec Verran pacts with the unresolved Whispering Frost, uncovers the ancient Ways — a mortal-built transit network wholly separate from the Great Seals — and, with Finn Carrow, ends First Crown founder Edmund Shaw's coercive Way experiments at Receiving House IV on Nareth Isle.
  • Timeline B gained a B21 tile, styled and dated as a deep-history prequel matching B18's treatment, placed after B20 in production order; Chronology's Ancient Amora table and visual timeline gained a B21 entry at its correct c. -928 ARV position, between the War of Ash and Glass's onset and the Seven Refusal.
  • Characters gained a new B21 campaign filter tile and eight new profiles — Alec Verran, Finn Carrow, the Whispering Frost, Mara Vey, Silas Kern, Rhydian Saye, Cairn Morren, and Mera Vann — plus seven supporting entries and an updated Edmund Shaw card covering his death.
  • The Atlas's Nareth Isle entry gained a B21 layer: Black Bell Ridge and Receiving House IV, without altering Nareth's modern, post-B16 state.
  • Lore's Oath Wars and War of Ash and Glass entries gained B21 material — First Crown's fracture after Shaw's death, and the ancient Ways as bounded, mortal-built infrastructure distinct from the Great Seals.
  • The Magic Codex gained a new Ancient Ways section covering the eight positional components, the Observation Anchor, and damaged-Way route-states — explicitly separated from both the Great Seals and B20's Inner Way.
  • Factions gained two new entries, the Circle and House Saye, and First Crown's entry updated with Shaw's death and the movement's fracture; Relics & Artefacts gained one new entry, Alec's retained eighth Way component.
  • The Canon Ledger gained a new B21 locked-canon entry (Ways≠Seals≠Inner Way, Nareth's severance, Shaw's execution, First Crown's fracture, Cairn Morren's consent scope) and a new open-threads entry (the Whisper's nature, the missing House Saye expedition, Finn's relationship to House Saye).
  • The homepage's stats updated for a 46th campaign, 33 factions, and 23 relics; its Timeline B stamp and map caption now note both B18's and B21's deep-history prequels.
19 August

Campaign B20: The Way Home

  • Campaign B20 — The Way Home added: a few weeks after B19, Ben returns to the Storm Great Seal and is directly recognised as its Warden — by demonstrated action, not bloodline — while the Hall of the Open Sky's Inner Way becomes physically traversable for the first time and Ben, Pella, and Rian raid North Fountain and Deep Market, reinvesting most of the haul in Farbound.
  • Nine character cards on Characters updated for B20: Benjamin Strake, Galen Vardane, Pella Ossel, Rian Vesker, Maud Halcro, Tessa Draith, Nicholas Soren, Ser Nora Durrel, and Professor Halden.
  • The Atlas's Alderwick and Badlands entries gained a B20 layer — a ruined merchant site, the Inner Way's ridge waystation and eastern Badlands pool, North Fountain, and Deep Market — with the Inner Way explicitly capped at the three traversals actually verified in play.
  • The Magic Codex gained a new Storm Warden section covering Ben's recognition, Warden's Passage, the first direct witness of the eight-around-empty-centre Great Seal geometry, and the verified Inner Way.
  • Lore updated with the office-vs-lineage distinction behind Warden recognition and the eightfold geometry's first in-play manifestation; Factions's Farbound Guild entry updated for the guild's post-B20 equipment and funding.
  • The Canon Ledger gained seven new locks: the corrected B19-vs-B20 Warden boundary, the office/lineage distinction, the centre-not-a-ninth-Seal rule, the three-route Inner Way cap, the confirmation that no "Blackglass" faction exists, the unidentified (not kobold) Deep Market creatures, and the Tempest Divide cross-timeline divergence.
  • Timeline B and the Chronology page's Two Threads visual updated for a 45th campaign.
16 August

Campaign B19: Storm Walker

  • Campaign B19 — Storm Walker added: Benjamin Strake's Farbound expedition rediscovers ruined Alderwick, locates the Storm Great Seal, and eases a major accumulation of pressure — set mid-343 ARV, a direct continuation of the modern Age of Rediscovery following B17, not a sequel to B18's c. -938 ARV prequel.
  • Eight new character cards on Characters: Benjamin "Ben" Strake, Galen Vardane, Maud Halcro, Tessa Draith, Pella Ossel, Rian Vesker, Jude Scarrow, and Veya.
  • The Atlas's Alderwick and Badlands entries gained a mid-343 ARV modern-ruin layer alongside the existing c. -938 ARV living city — Eastern Ward civic case, the Processional Way, and the Hall of the Open Sky among the new finds — plus a new map-viewer era button and a corrected Storm Seal note at Outpost Nine. Greymount's entry updated for its new role as the Farbound Guild's home city.
  • The Magic Codex's Storm Seal entry rewritten with its corrected location and a new section distinguishing the Storm Great Seal, the Western Pressure Station at Marker Sixteen, and Ben's Storm Walker adaptation from one another.
  • Factions gained the Farbound Guild — Greymount's professional exploration and rescue institution, not a continent-wide government or owner of the Badlands.
  • Relics & Artefacts gained three entries: the Storm Great Seal, the Sixteen Focus & Field Measure, and the Eastern Ward Survey Case.
  • Lore and the Canon Ledger updated with B19's confirmation that Storm contained the Shattering's wound rather than causing it, the resolved post-Shattering "report incomplete" note, and the permanent-but-bounded Badlands weather change.
  • Timeline B and the Chronology page's Two Threads visual updated for a 44th campaign.
