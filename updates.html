Site Updates

A running record of what's been added, corrected, or rebuilt on this site, newest first. This tracks the site itself — new campaigns, new pages, structural fixes. For in-world naming variants and settled ambiguities, see the Canon Ledger instead.

16 August

Campaign B19: Storm Walker

  • Campaign B19 — Storm Walker added: Benjamin Strake's Farbound expedition rediscovers ruined Alderwick, locates the Storm Great Seal, and eases a major accumulation of pressure — set mid-343 ARV, a direct continuation of the modern Age of Rediscovery following B17, not a sequel to B18's c. -938 ARV prequel.
  • Eight new character cards on Characters: Benjamin "Ben" Strake, Galen Vardane, Maud Halcro, Tessa Draith, Pella Ossel, Rian Vesker, Jude Scarrow, and Veya.
  • The Atlas's Alderwick and Badlands entries gained a mid-343 ARV modern-ruin layer alongside the existing c. -938 ARV living city — Eastern Ward civic case, the Processional Way, and the Hall of the Open Sky among the new finds — plus a new map-viewer era button and a corrected Storm Seal note at Outpost Nine. Greymount's entry updated for its new role as the Farbound Guild's home city.
  • The Magic Codex's Storm Seal entry rewritten with its corrected location and a new section distinguishing the Storm Great Seal, the Western Pressure Station at Marker Sixteen, and Ben's Storm Walker adaptation from one another.
  • Factions gained the Farbound Guild — Greymount's professional exploration and rescue institution, not a continent-wide government or owner of the Badlands.
  • Relics & Artefacts gained three entries: the Storm Great Seal, the Sixteen Focus & Field Measure, and the Eastern Ward Survey Case.
  • Lore and the Canon Ledger updated with B19's confirmation that Storm contained the Shattering's wound rather than causing it, the resolved post-Shattering "report incomplete" note, and the permanent-but-bounded Badlands weather change.
  • Timeline B and the Chronology page's Two Threads visual updated for a 44th campaign.
15 August

Campaign B18, Ancient Amora, and search filters

  • Campaign B18 — The Crown Within added: a deep-history prequel set c. -938 ARV, during the Oath Wars, played after B17 despite predating it by centuries.
  • The Chronology page's Ancient Amora section rebuilt as a proper table matching the Timeline A/B format, with unplayed historical entries styled as interludes.
  • The Atlas's interactive map viewer gained a third era group, Ancient Amora, with a new c. -938 ARV Vanmere map — shared pre-Fracture history, not exclusive to either timeline.
  • Site search can now be filtered by category (Campaigns, Characters, Locations, Factions, Relics, Lore, Pages), with live counts.
  • The home page's "Where to start" section expanded into proper reading paths for new visitors, with rough reading times.
13 August

Campaign B17: The Second Measure

  • B17 added: Nick and Halden recover the first coherent history of how the Great Seals were actually set, dating Tarenus Durrel and the Shattering — closing Nick's major inherited questions as he and Jarek get engaged.

  • A favicon rolled out across every page on the site.

Campaign B16: Echoes Beneath Stone
