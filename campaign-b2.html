Timeline B · Campaign B2 / XXVII

The Hollowrest Awakening

Age of Founding, Reforged

1 ARV Primary location: Hollowrest Citadel, Menge

Core Premise

In the weeks following the Mind's destruction, unnatural froststorms strike the border of Highever and Menge. Frostfall Outpost sends a fragmented distress signal, then falls silent. The Order of Anchors deploys its first full expedition — a test of whether the institution founded weeks earlier in Campaign B1 can function as more than a reactive emergency measure.

Act-by-Act

Frostfall's Silence

Distorted resonance signatures lead the Order to Frostfall Outpost. The outpost is found intact — survivors sheltered after a frost-surge, not abducted. Lio and Kellan detect false resonance reflections, a property of frost-magic mimicking Anchor pulses — the first sign that Menge's magic operates on principles the Order doesn't yet understand.

The Breach at Hollowrest

Frost patterns point to Hollowrest Citadel, long rumoured but never reached in Timeline A. Entry triggers ancient wards; the structure identifies the party as "successors" to lost Binders — a category of magic-user predating even the Heart, Flame, and Stone Seals from Timeline A's founding era. Lio and Kellan channel a controlled dyad-alignment that proves stabilising rather than coercive, satisfying whatever test the wards represent.

The Proto-Anchor's Recognition

The proto-Anchor recognises Lio and Kellan as stabilisers, Eryndrel as rightful steward, and the six travellers as external resonance-bearers. The Anchor Web expands to integrate Hollowrest's output, nearly overwhelming Lio before Kellan grounds the surge — an early demonstration that even the founding Anchor/Thread pair has limits that need active management, not just raw devotion.

World State After Campaign B2

Geography. Hollowrest Citadel is confirmed standing in Menge — notably, a location Timeline A's records only ever rumoured and never actually reached. Highever formally claims it under the new Hollowrest Custodial Mandate.

Magic. Seal theory itself is rewritten this campaign: the proto-Anchor is recognised as older than the Heart, Flame, and Stone Seals from Timeline A's Campaign I, meaning Binder-lineage magic predates the Seal system Amora has organised itself around since the very beginning. The "Icebinder cult" — treated as dangerous heretics back in Timeline A's Campaign I — is reclassified here as an ancient Binder diaspora, their downfall due to collapse, not heresy.

The Durrel Line. No Durrel figures appear — this remains true for the earliest Timeline B campaigns, consistent with Taren and Zethyr's erasure in B1.

Institutions. The Order of Anchors expands its jurisdiction and its theoretical framework in the same campaign — proof the institution can grow through genuine discovery, not just crisis response. This directly sets up B3's revelation that Kellan himself carries Frostborn — Binder-adjacent — heritage.

Canon status: follows the character register directly; "Kellan Vance" here predates his later renaming to Caen.
