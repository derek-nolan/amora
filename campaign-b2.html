Timeline B · Campaign B2 / XXVII

The Hollowrest Awakening

Age of Founding, Reforged

0 ARV (Weeks 2–5 after the Order's founding) Primary location: Frostfall Outpost, Hollowrest Citadel, Menge

Core Premise

In the weeks following the Mind's destruction, unnatural froststorms strike the border of Highever and Menge. Frostfall Outpost sends a fragmented distress signal, then falls silent. The newly operational Order of Anchors deploys its first full expedition — Lio, Kellan, and all six travellers — expecting a missing-persons rescue. What they find instead reshapes Timeline B's entire magical history: the Icebinders were never a cult in this timeline at all. They're the shattered remnants of an ancient Binder line, guardians of a proto-Anchor that predates the Heart Seal itself. Beneath Hollowrest Citadel, the party finds a living metaphysical engine older than any known Seal, and its steward — a construct called Eryndrel — who recognises the Anchor-Thread dyad on sight and yields control to the Order.

Party & Key Figures

  • Kellan Vance Alive; Thread

Act-by-Act

Act I — Frostfall's Silence

Distorted resonance signatures lead the Order to Frostfall Outpost, the expected site of Icebinder activity. What they find instead is intact and unkidnapped — survivors sheltered together after a frost-surge, not abducted by anyone. Lio and Kellan detect false resonance reflections there, a property of frost-magic that mimics genuine Anchor pulses closely enough to have triggered the original distress call. Scouts track the real anomaly further north, with Zeth, Talren, Rian, Arden, Solan, and Marrek splitting off to help stabilise and evacuate the remaining civilians.

Act II — The Breach at Hollowrest

Frost patterns lead the party to Hollowrest Citadel — long rumoured in old records, never actually reached in Timeline A. Entry triggers ancient wards that identify the party specifically as "successors" to lost Binders, a category of magic-user predating even the Heart, Flame, and Stone Seals from Campaign I's founding era. At its core they find a proto-Anchor: dormant, powerful, built on architecture that predates the Seal system entirely. The Citadel wakes its steward, Eryndrel, who initially treats the party as intruders and tests their intent through rhythmic Anchor-pressure rather than force. Lio and Kellan answer with a controlled dyad-alignment that proves stabilising rather than coercive, satisfying whatever test the wards represent. Eryndrel yields control, recognising them as rightful inheritors of the Binder mantle.

Act III — The Proto-Anchor's Recognition

The proto-Anchor fully engages, scanning the party's emotional and metaphysical alignment directly — evaluating intent, not bloodline or duty, which is itself a significant departure from how the Heart Seal has always worked. It recognises Lio and Kellan as stabilisers, Eryndrel as rightful steward, and the six travellers as external resonance-bearers in their own right. The Anchor Web expands temporarily to integrate Hollowrest's output, nearly overwhelming Lio before Kellan grounds the surge — an early demonstration that even the founding Anchor/Thread pair has real limits that need active management. Frostfall's storms cease as the Citadel settles into lockdown stabilisation mode under joint stewardship, Eryndrel internally and the Order externally. The Icebinder "cult" is formally reclassified as an ancient Binder diaspora, their downfall the result of collapse, not heresy. Back at Silver Bastion, Lio and Kellan's dyad becomes publicly recognised as the core stabilising force of the entire Reforged Age.

World State After Campaign B2

Geography. Hollowrest Citadel is added to the Atlas as a Critical Resonance Site and sealed fortress of pre-Seal architecture. Frostfall Outpost is reinforced with Anchor wards. The Highever Highlands, previously a rupture-risk Zone of Watching after Campaign B1, are downgraded to a Low-Risk Observation Area — and Silver Bastion itself gains a purpose-built Dyad Practice Wing, complete with early glyph wards and resonance-safe architecture.

Magic. Seal theory itself is rewritten this campaign: the proto-Anchor is recognised as older than the Heart, Flame, and Stone Seals, meaning Binder-lineage magic — Heart, Flame, and Ice branches alike — are revealed as descendants of one single ancient discipline rather than separate systems. Two new mechanics emerge directly from the mission: Proto-Anchor Alignment (requiring dyad emotional clarity and stabiliser consent, capable of reinforcing environmental stability across multiple chambers at once) and Resonance Frost-Reflection Theory, the discovery that frost constructs can mimic resonance signatures closely enough to fool detection — the Anchor Web has to be recalibrated afterward just to tell reflected resonance apart from the real thing.

The Durrel Line. No Durrel figures appear — this remains true for the earliest Timeline B campaigns, consistent with Taren and Zethyr's erasure in B1.

Institutions. The Order of Anchors formally expands its mandate to include long-range Seal-risk assessment and managing proto-Anchor sites and Binder ruins, and begins its first real training curriculum for stabiliser and dyad calibration. A new Hollowrest Custodial Mandate is established alongside it — Eryndrel listed formally as Fixed Custodian, with the Order holding external oversight only and no internal command over the Citadel itself. Highever also opens its first cross-border diplomatic contact with Menge in years, non-hostile, directly as a result of this campaign. This directly sets up B3's revelation that Kellan himself carries Frostborn — Binder-adjacent — heritage.

Canon status: follows the source account directly; "Kellan Vance" here predates his later renaming to Caen.
