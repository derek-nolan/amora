The Hollowrest Awakening

Core Premise

In the weeks following the Mind's destruction, unnatural froststorms strike the border of Highever and Menge. Frostfall Outpost sends a fragmented distress signal, then falls silent. The newly operational Order of Anchors deploys its first full expedition — Lio, Kellan, and all six travellers — expecting a missing-persons rescue. What they find genuinely changes what this generation knows: Hollowrest preserves an ancient custodial line and a proto-Anchor whose architecture is older than the Great Seal institutions familiar to them. The party concludes that the people they have found were not the fallen Icebinder cult the Order expected. That conclusion is correct about Hollowrest's legitimate custodians; their broader claim that the Icebinders as a whole were "never truly a cult" is later shown to have overgeneralised from this discovery.

Act-by-Act

Act I — Frostfall's Silence

Distorted resonance signatures lead the Order to Frostfall Outpost, the expected site of Icebinder activity. What they find instead is intact and unkidnapped — survivors sheltered together after a frost-surge, not abducted by anyone. Lio and Kellan detect false resonance reflections there, a property of frost-magic that mimics genuine Anchor pulses closely enough to have triggered the original distress call. Scouts track the real anomaly further north while the Six help stabilise and evacuate civilians.

Act II — The Breach at Hollowrest

Frost patterns lead the party to Hollowrest Citadel. Entry triggers old wards that identify the party as successors to lost custodians. At its core they find a proto-Anchor: dormant, powerful, and built in an architectural tradition the Order cannot yet place confidently in the later Great Seal chronology. Eryndrel wakes and tests intent through rhythmic pressure rather than force. Lio and Kellan answer with controlled dyad alignment that proves stabilising rather than coercive, satisfying the test. Eryndrel yields stewardship cooperation without becoming subordinate to the Order.

Act III — The Proto-Anchor's Recognition

The proto-Anchor scans alignment directly, evaluating intent rather than rank. It recognises Lio and Kellan as stabilisers, Eryndrel as rightful steward, and the six travellers as external resonance-bearers. The Anchor Web expands temporarily to integrate Hollowrest's output, nearly overwhelming Lio before Kellan grounds the surge. Frostfall's storms cease as the Citadel settles into stabilisation mode. The Order formally concludes the expected "Icebinder cult" was instead an ancient custodial diaspora — a conclusion later narrowed once B16–B17 distinguish the unfallen Icewarden branches from the genuinely fallen Icebinders.

World State After Campaign B2

Geography. Hollowrest Citadel is added to the Atlas as a critical resonance/custodial site. Frostfall Outpost is reinforced and Silver Bastion adds purpose-built dyad practice space.

Magic. Hollowrest proves that custodial/Binder practice and proto-Anchor architecture predate the institutions the Order currently associates with Great Seals. This is an important discovery, but B17 later provides the stronger historical model: individual pressure Houses and Binders existed before the coordinated Setting of the eight Great Seals, after which permanent Warden watches emerged. B2 therefore should not be read as proving every Binder tradition was one single bloodline or discipline.

The Durrel Line. No Durrel figures appear, consistent with Taren and Zethyr's erasure in B1.

Institutions. The Order expands its mandate to ancient custodial sites and Binder ruins while recognising Eryndrel as Hollowrest's internal Fixed Custodian. Highever also opens renewed cross-border contact with Menge.

Canon status: follows the campaign events. Later understanding: Hollowrest and Eryndrel are legitimate ancient custodial survivals, but the Order's B2 conclusion that the Icebinders were "never a cult" is a documented contemporary overcorrection. Later evidence distinguishes unfallen Icewarden/Frostborn branches from the fallen Icebinder faction encountered in Timeline A. B17 also replaces B2's broad "one ancient discipline" assumption with the better-attested model of multiple pressure Houses and Binder traditions preceding the eight Great Seals.
