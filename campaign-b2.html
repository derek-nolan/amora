Timeline B · Campaign B2 / XXVII

The Hollowrest Awakening

Age of Founding, Reforged

1 ARV Primary location: Hollowrest Citadel, Menge

Core Premise

In the weeks following the Mind's destruction, unnatural froststorms strike the border of Highever and Menge. Frostfall Outpost sends a fragmented distress signal, then falls silent. The Order of Anchors deploys its first full expedition.

Party & Key Figures

  • Lio Serrin
    First Anchor of Timeline B.
  • Kellan Vance
    First Stabiliser Thread.
  • Arden Vale & Rian Thale, Zeth Caden & Talren Vale, Solan & Marrek
    The Six Travellers, serving as stabilisers and witnesses throughout the expedition.
  • Seraphine Vale
    High Paladin; architect of the Order of Anchors.
  • Eryndrel
    Steward construct of Hollowrest's proto-Anchor, introduced this campaign.

Act-by-Act

Frostfall's Silence

Distorted resonance signatures lead the Order to Frostfall Outpost. The outpost is found intact — survivors sheltered after a frost-surge, not abducted. Lio and Kellan detect false resonance reflections, a property of frost-magic mimicking Anchor pulses.

The Breach at Hollowrest

Frost patterns point to Hollowrest Citadel, long rumoured but never reached in Timeline A. Entry triggers ancient wards; the structure identifies the party as "successors" to lost Binders. Lio and Kellan channel a controlled dyad-alignment that proves stabilising rather than coercive.

The Proto-Anchor's Recognition

The proto-Anchor recognises Lio and Kellan as stabilisers, Eryndrel as rightful steward, and the six travellers as external resonance-bearers. The Anchor Web expands to integrate Hollowrest's output, nearly overwhelming Lio before Kellan grounds the surge.

Final World State

The Icebinder "cult" is reclassified as an ancient Binder diaspora, their downfall due to collapse, not heresy. Highever formally claims Hollowrest under the Hollowrest Custodial Mandate, and the proto-Anchor is recognised as older than the Heart, Flame, and Stone Seals — Seal theory itself is rewritten as branches of one ancient discipline.

Canon status: follows the character register directly; "Kellan Vance" here predates his later renaming to Caen.
