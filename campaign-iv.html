Timeline A · Campaign III

The Durrel Legacy

Age of Reclamation

c. 900 ARV Primary location: The Hollow, Vault of Lineage, Mirror Bastion

Opening World State

Roughly nine centuries after the Dominion's fall: a time of ruins and fragile republics trying to rise in the shadow of Durrel legend. Evermere has been re-forged as a Collegium, the Vault of Tarenus lies sealed, and Brevair has fragmented into mercantile city-states.

Party & Key Figures

  • Edrin Durrel
    Scholar-echo; the last known claimant of the Durrel line. Ultimately severs the Echo Chain.
  • Cael Veyne
    Paladin-echo of Taren, bonded to Edrin through the reformed Echo Chain.
  • Merien Thalos
    Collegium archaeologist; aids the sealing of the Vault and later co-founds the New Heartwardens.

Act-by-Act

The Hollow

Edrin Durrel meets Cael Veyne, a paladin of Luminara carrying the echo-pattern of Taren. Their encounter reforms the Echo Chain before either has chosen what it should mean.

Vault of Lineage and Mirror Bastion

Edrin, Cael, and Collegium archaeologist Merien Thalos enter the Vault of Lineage and the Mirror Bastion. Records of Zethyr, Taren, the Heart Medallion, and the first bond stop being legend and become ancestral memory.

Taren Reborn

Cael begins to claim he is Taren reborn and moves toward reconstructing the Dominion — the old rhetoric returning as conviction before it returns as open tyranny.

The Severing

Edrin acts earlier than Zethyr had: he uses the Heart Medallion to cut the Echo Chain before Cael can build an empire around it. Cael vanishes at the Northern Gate; whether he died, dissolved into the ley, or crossed into another state of existence is left open.

The Final Chronicle

Edrin writes the Final Chronicle and seals the Vault of Tarenus. Merien goes on to help found the New Heartwardens — an order built on preservation and study rather than divine certainty.

Final World State

Edrin chooses Severance: the first deliberate breaking of the Chain. The Vault of Tarenus is sealed under new terms, and the world steps toward a future where law — not bloodline or bond — is supposed to rule. This establishes the pattern later Severings and reforms echo: better an imperfect world with choice than a perfect one without it.

Canon status: restores Merien Thalos and the archaeological/institutional strand alongside Edrin and Cael.
