Timeline A · Campaign IV

The Emperor Reforged

Age of Renewal — “Through Power, Eternity”

c. 1900–2000 ARVPrimary theatres: Silver Bastion, Evermere, Tarenus, the Confluence

Opening World State

A millennium after Edrin refuses the old throne, Amora has had time to forget the immediacy of the first Dominion while preserving its symbols. Republics and scholarly institutions exist, old ruins have become expedition sites, and the idea that the past can be handled safely has become increasingly attractive.

Campaign IV begins with exploration rather than coronation. Feran Durrel, an explorer-scholar descended from Edrin, leads work to raise and study old Silver Bastion structures and the surviving Seal traditions. In that recovered world he encounters Coren Veyne, an echo-pattern of Taren whose first appeal is not cruelty but competence. Coren believes the failure of the first Dominion lay in excess, not in the concentration of power itself.

Party & Key Figures

  • Feran Durrel PC
    Explorer-scholar, descendant of Edrin, and Heartbearer figure of the Confluence generation. Leads the expedition to raise Silver Bastion and study the old Seals. His relationship with Coren becomes both the emotional centre of the campaign and the mechanism through which the old pattern returns.
  • Coren Veyne
    Echo of Taren and later Emperor Reforged. Begins with a promise of order without cruelty and genuinely improves stability and infrastructure before the Confluence turns benevolent centralisation into renewed Dominion.
  • Renewal-era scholars and expedition allies
    The surviving sources preserve their institutional role more clearly than a stable roster of names: they help recover Silver Bastion, study old Seal architecture, and become witnesses to the reappearance of Heart–Veil harmonics.

Act-by-Act

Raising Silver Bastion

Feran’s expedition recovers Silver Bastion from the landscape of the Mired Veil and treats it first as an archaeological and magical site. The campaign returns physically to one of Timeline A’s oldest locations, but with a different question: can ancient power be studied without being inherited?

Recovered Seal structures and old Dominion remains place Feran in direct contact with the unresolved legacy Edrin tried to contain. His approach is scholarly and cautious. Coren’s is more ambitious: he sees in the recovered systems the possibility of restoring public order without reproducing Taren’s overt brutality.

Feran and Coren

The bond between Feran and Coren develops through shared work, attraction, ambition, and trust. Surviving records summarise Feran’s position with the phrase “Through Love, Surrender.” That line is important precisely because the surrender begins as chosen intimacy and political faith, not forced enthrallment.

Coren searches for a counterpart who can occupy the old Zethyr-shaped place without disappearing inside it. Feran believes he may be able to stand close enough to influence the structure while retaining himself. The campaign deliberately echoes Campaigns I–III while changing the personalities and circumstances.

The Confluence Chain

The central magical development is the Confluence Chain, a fusion of Heart and Veil harmonics used to stabilise ley energy. Unlike Taren’s Veil Core, the Confluence is initially presented as a balanced synthesis. It works. Infrastructure improves, dangerous currents settle, and a world tired of fragmentation experiences genuine material benefits.

That success is what makes the recurrence dangerous. Coren’s creed — “Through Power, Eternity” — becomes inscribed into the Confluence system. Practical authority, magical stabilisation, and personal devotion begin reinforcing one another until the distinction between a successful partnership and a governing hierarchy becomes increasingly difficult to maintain.

The Emperor Reforged

Coren is crowned during the Confluence period. The Dominion returns beneath a gentler vocabulary: not conquest for its own sake, but safety, coordination, permanence, and the promise that this time the person at the centre will be better.

The surviving early source is clear about the larger outcome even where scene-level detail has been lost: Coren moves from lawful restraint toward renewed authoritarianism, Feran remains bound to him through love and the Confluence, and the world once again finds itself organised around a paired Heart/Veil axis.

Mercy, Betrayal, and the Long Silence

The cycle does not simply repeat the first Dominion beat for beat. Later summaries call Campaign IV a half-success because Coren’s rule produces real peace and functioning infrastructure. The warning is subtler: a benevolent centre is still a centre, and systems designed around the virtue of one ruler remain vulnerable to that ruler’s drift.

The campaign closes with the balance teetering and Silver Bastion’s renewed importance tied to the Confluence. Later source layers connect this age to a catastrophic Tempest and the creation of the Mired Veil, but they disagree on exact timing and mechanism. Those events are therefore treated as a later interlude rather than silently folded into the campaign’s finale.

World State After Campaign IV

Geography. Silver Bastion has physically re-emerged as a major recovered site. Evermere and Tarenus again become political centres around the revived Heart–Veil axis. Later catastrophes reshape this geography further, but the sources do not agree precisely where the boundary between Campaign IV and the following interlude lies.

Magic. The Confluence Chain proves Heart and Veil harmonics can be fused without immediately reproducing the original Veil Core. It also proves that technical balance does not guarantee political or emotional balance.

The Durrel Line. Feran continues Edrin’s bloodline and becomes the Heartbearer counterpart of the age. His story demonstrates that awareness of history does not automatically prevent repetition when love, public success, and power all reward the same structure.

Institutions. Coren’s renewed Dominion becomes a model later ages will repeatedly reinterpret: centralised power justified not by divine right but by competence, safety, and measurable benefit. The Confluence itself survives in later archives as raw material for experiments in law and magic.

Canon status: this page follows the surviving early lineage record where it is strongest: Feran Durrel is the explorer-scholar/Heartbearer PC, Coren Veyne the Taren-echo counterpart, Silver Bastion is raised and studied, the Confluence Chain fuses Heart and Veil harmonics, and Coren is crowned as the Dominion returns. Some later compact summaries use variant names such as “Coren Vael” and add supporting figures not consistently preserved across sources; those have not been silently promoted to confirmed campaign canon. The later Tempest/Sundering chronology remains source-conflicted and is not invented here.
