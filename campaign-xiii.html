Timeline A · Campaign XIII

The Citadel of Inheritance

Early Cyberpunk Turn / The Chain Reclamation

7420–7421 ARV Primary location: Citadel of Inheritance (Tarenus Reclaimed)

Opening World State

Ten years after Kael Durreth's consolidation of the Republic, Amora enters a decade of uneasy peace. The Harmony Network — a vast leygrid binding emotion to civic duty — maintains order so absolute that even dissent feels virtuous.

Party & Key Figures

  • Rian Leth
    Oath Guard initiate who becomes an Anchor and the conscience of the Severance. Bonded to Calen Vorr.
  • Calen Vorr
    Sentinel captain who later leads the Independents. Bonded to Rian Leth.
  • Commander Seraphine Vale
    High-command liaison and covert patron, haunted by Kael Durreth's vanished teachings; opposes the Council's plan.
  • Speaker Lira Durrel
    Council chair and obedience absolutist; antagonist to Rian and Calen.
  • Kael Durreth (legacy)
    Absent founder whose harmonic code still haunts Council policy and public debate.

Act-by-Act

The Chain Awakens

During the Festival of Harmony, a Councilor's sigil ruptures in light and the dormant Chain awakes, its rhythm echoing through every ceremonial collar in the Citadel. Rian Leth and Captain Calen Vorr become the first to hear its voice.

The Council and the Chain

The Council seeks to integrate the Chain into the Republic's infrastructure so every citizen moves in perfect moral cadence. Seraphine covertly opposes the plan, directing Rian and Calen to uncover the Chain's origin — a Choir-era relic designed to translate devotion into command.

The Resonance Trials

In a public Resonance Interrogation, Rian is forced to kneel by a single command word — but Calen counters the order using cadence alone, revealing that genuine trust can override programmed obedience. The demonstration terrifies the Council.

The Severance

In the Anchor Vault, Rian and Calen confront the Harmonic Stone and the Chain's own will. They choose to sever the hierarchy, not destroy it. Collars dim and the Network falls silent across Amora — for the first time in a century, the Republic hears its own heartbeat.

Final World State

The Council dissolves within weeks. Local assemblies and guild coalitions — the Quiet Republics — rise to fill the vacuum. Inheritance Reclaimed becomes the Free Citadel, a city of archives rather than edicts. Rian and Calen vanish north to found a forge settlement where discipline is practiced as craft, not command. The Citadel becomes the hinge city connecting past dominion to later Concord systems.

Canon status: "Lira Durrel" appears as "Durell" in some source passages — normalized here per the site's spelling decision.
