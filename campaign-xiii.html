Timeline A · Campaign XIII

The Citadel of Inheritance

Second Cyberpunk Era begins / The Chain Reclamation

7420–7421 ARV Primary location: Citadel of Inheritance (Tarenus Reclaimed)

Opening World State

Ten years after Kael Durreth's consolidation of the Republic, Amora enters a decade of uneasy peace. The Harmony Network — a vast leygrid binding emotion to civic duty — maintains order so absolute that even dissent feels virtuous. Cities like Evermere, Highever, and the Citadel of Inheritance experiment with data-law, surveillance grids, and revamped dominion imagery — the opening of what this site marks as the Second Cyberpunk Era.

Act-by-Act

The Chain Awakens

During the Festival of Harmony, a Councilor's sigil ruptures in light and the dormant Chain awakes, its rhythm echoing through every ceremonial collar in the Citadel. What had been a metaphor for unity reveals itself as a living hierarchy, ancient and sentient. Rian Leth and Captain Calen Vorr become the first to hear its voice.

The Council and the Chain

Believing obedience the highest civic virtue, the Council of Inheritance seeks to integrate the Chain into the Republic's infrastructure so every citizen moves, breathes, and decides in perfect moral cadence. Commander Seraphine Vale, haunted by Kael Durreth's vanished teachings, covertly opposes the plan, directing Rian and Calen to uncover the Chain's origin beneath the Citadel. Their discoveries within the Hall of Memory expose the truth: the Chain isn't a gift of Kael's stewardship but a Choir-era relic — the last surviving fragment of the very system destroyed back in Campaign V — designed to translate devotion into command.

The Resonance Trials

To prove their control, the Council orders a public Resonance Interrogation. Rian is forced to kneel by a single command word transmitted through the leygrid. Calen counters the order using cadence alone, revealing that genuine trust can override programmed obedience. The demonstration terrifies the Council — if one man's conviction can out-command the state, the illusion of perfect order can never hold.

The Severance

Under Seraphine's guidance, the pair descend into the Anchor Vault beneath the Hall of Memory and find the Harmonic Stone, a crystalline engine linking every node of the Harmony Network. Confronted by the Chain's own will, they make a single, impossible choice: to sever the hierarchy, not destroy it. Calen issues the final command; Rian anchors the resonance. The Chain survives, but its ability to impose obedience dies with the Council's signal. Throughout Amora, collars dim and the Network falls silent — for the first time in a century, the Republic hears the sound of its own heartbeat.

World State After Campaign XIII

Geography. Inheritance Reclaimed becomes the Free Citadel, a city of archives rather than edicts. Local assemblies and guild coalitions — soon called the Quiet Republics — rise to fill the vacuum left by the Council's collapse.

Magic. The Chain, revealed as a surviving Choir-era relic, is severed rather than destroyed — the fifth recorded Severance in the record, and notably the first to leave the underlying structure intact but neutered rather than eliminated entirely. Leycurrents persist but no longer answer to collective emotion; spellwork from here on depends on individual resolve.

The Durrel Line. Speaker Lira Durrel represents the name recurring as antagonist rather than protagonist this time — proof the pattern isn't deterministically heroic, just recurring. (Some source passages spell this "Durell"; normalized to Durrel here per the site's spelling decision.)

Institutions. Central authority dissolves; self-governing provinces form the Quiet Republics. Worship of Harmony is replaced by the Quiet Doctrine — belief in personal moral rhythm. The Oath Guard fragments; Calen Vorr's Independents protect citizens without command hierarchy. The Citadel becomes the hinge city connecting past dominion to later Concord systems, laying groundwork for the Third Cyberpunk Age's obsession with law on camera and visible consent — directly seeding Campaign XV.
