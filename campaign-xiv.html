Timeline A · Campaign XIV

The Dominion Cycle

Second Cyberpunk Era — ends in the Cataclysm

7920–8040 ARV Primary location: The Citadel of the Vale

Opening World State

Still pre-Concord but firmly cyberpunk: neon, nodes, and civic cults of order. Roughly five centuries after the Quiet Republics of Campaign XIII, Rhett of the Vale rises among fractured city-states — Veylaris still trades, Evermere wanes, the Citadel of Inheritance stands half empty — preaching that motion breeds decay.

Party & Key Figures

  • Rhett of the Vale — Disappeared in the Northern Wilds; presumed dead
    Emperor; Architect of Stillness; founder of the Dominion. Consort to Niall Argent, creator of the Twin Chains.
  • Niall Argent — Alive by tradition; mythic figure in Renewal Age lore
    Witness and Consort; bearer of the Pattern Complete; first Warden of Motion. Founded the Sanctum of Renewal after Rhett's disappearance.
  • The Veil (entity) — Active elemental presence
    Sentient force of balance, reclaimed from Dominion captivity through Niall's mercy and Rhett's collapse; manifests as auroras and storms in the Age of Renewal.
  • Artisans of the Pattern (collective) — Survived the fall
    Guild of crafters who expanded the Pattern Complete and forged the Twin Chains; reformed as caretakers of relic fragments under the Collegium of Renewal.

Act-by-Act

The Accord of Stillness

Rhett's central philosophy declares that peace can be manufactured through alignment of spirit and law. His creed — "Stillness is mercy" — promises peace without struggle. With Niall Argent, paladin-scholar and his lover, he remakes the world into the Dominion, an empire where order itself becomes religion. Every citizen wears sigils echoing the Pattern Complete, the living armour crafted for Niall, turning the populace into a visible map of obedience.

The Pattern and the Twin Chains

The Pattern Complete — forged from black leather and living silver, inscribed with all provinces' sigils — binds Niall's body to Rhett's command lattice, fusing love with governance. When Rhett discovers Niall's gentle falsehoods meant to preserve faith, he forges two chains: Silver, representing the Veil's mercy, and Iron, representing mankind's will. To the empire they symbolize harmony; to Rhett, they prove dominion. The proverb "Iron outlasts silver" becomes the imperial motto. The Witness Court echoes the old Heartwardens but is bound to Rhett alone; the Chainguard enforces the Two Chains' symbolism across the provinces.

The Fall

During his final decades, the Veil itself begins to weaken under Rhett's rigid harmonics — Dominion Era Magic has codified leycraft into Architectural Resonance, magic expressed through geometry and alignment, requiring perfect stillness even in emotion. Rhett disappears into the Northern Wilds; auroras of pale iron light mark his last pilgrimage. When the Veil reclaims the world, magic reverts to natural flow — unpredictable but alive. This collapse is "the Cataclysm" that Campaign XV's Third Cyberpunk Age will begin decades after.

World State After Campaign XIV

Geography. The Citadel of the Vale stands as Rhett's seat, built atop the ruins of Inheritance Proper. Evermere Citadel converts into the Collegium of Doctrine. The Southern Marches become home to the Sanctuary of Renewal, founded by Niall after Rhett's fall. Menge's thawed lands again become the empire's forge and frontier, its auroras glowing steady silver at the Dominion's height.

Magic. Architectural Resonance — magic through geometry and alignment — is this era's dominant system, requiring perfect stillness. After the fall, magic reverts to natural, unpredictable flow; the last fragments of the Pattern dissolve into ley-light that seeds the next age's renewal. Theological shift: the Dominion Creed "To move is to err" inverts into the Renewal Creed "To err is to move."

The Durrel Line. No Durrel figures directly this campaign — Rhett and Niall represent a fresh, unrelated dyad pattern rather than a lineage continuation, showing the commanding/anchoring structure can recur without any blood connection to the founding line at all.

Institutions. The Sanctum of Renewal becomes the philosophical heart of the Age of Renewal and eventual prelude to Campaign XV's Third Cyberpunk Age. XIV becomes the cautionary prequel to the Neon/Concord work — a demonstration of how even consent-coded bonds can slide into empire when unbounded, providing the case law and trauma that later makes Concord so wary of "thrones."
