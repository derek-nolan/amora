Geography across both timelines

Atlas

Amora's map doesn't stay still — most major locations are rebuilt, renamed, or repurposed multiple times across each timeline's ages. Each entry below tracks a location's arc rather than a fixed description.

Highever

Realm and major city — crowned by Silver Bastion

Timeline A

Begins as a diplomatic and scholarly centre under a paladin council. Becomes the Dominion's capital of control after Campaign II, then cycles through republic, surveillance hub, neon civic centre, and paladin academy city across the later ages.

Timeline B

A wealthy, orderly city-state before 0 ARV. Becomes the political centre of rupture management and the Order of Anchors from B1. Loses its uncontested Light fortress when Silver Bastion falls in B11.

Silver Bastion

also Obsidian Bastion (Dominion era) · The Veiled Citadel (post-B11)

Timeline A

Founded as a fortress-temple and Heartwarden centre. Reforged as the Obsidian Bastion under the Dominion; later shells and restorations continue as academy, reliquary, and civic symbol through the ages.

Timeline B

The Heart Seal awakens early, making it the Order of Anchors' headquarters and proto-anchor citadel through B1–B5. Falls from within in B11 and is renamed the Veiled Citadel. B14's explicit objective is reclaiming it, planning from Durrel Ridge.

Evermere

Repeated rebuild site — village, city, and capital by turns

Timeline A

A coastal sanctuary of gardens and Heartwarden archives in Campaign I, later a Dominion pleasure-and-law centre. Destroyed and rebuilt more than once — as a glass-and-neon megacity, then a candle-lit low-tech city-state atop drowned datacores after Concord's grids fail, then Archive Capital of the dyad-law era.

Timeline B

A modest fishing village before 0 ARV. Becomes a coastal port and refuge from B6 onward, home to The Lantern and its youth squad, and later a major anti-conditioning and identity-restoration site in B13.

Greymount / Tarenus

also Tarenus Keep · Citadel of Inheritance · Inheritance Reclaimed · Free Citadel

Timeline A

A fortified hill-city rebuilt as Tarenus Keep, the Dominion's southern capital, after Campaign II's Rite of Binding. Left in ruin after the Severing, later rebranded Citadel of Inheritance and then the Free Citadel once the Harmony Network falls. The Vault of Tarenus lies beneath it through most of Timeline A's later ages.

Timeline B

An independent fortified hill-city before 0 ARV. The B5 investigation into Rillhaven's Veil conduit runs through it, though the city itself never becomes a Dominion capital in this timeline.

Brevair

Timeline A capital renamed Durrelion · Timeline B capital named Corvane

Timeline A

Crown ally and dynastic heartland in Campaign I. Its capital is renamed Durrelion and rebuilt in black stone under the Dominion, then splinters into scholar-states and later the industrial Brevair League.

Timeline B

An isolationist centralised empire before 0 ARV. Its Emperor's assassination in B5 shifts regional power toward Bastion; by B10, Rhalen Valecour becomes its quiet, uncrowned ruler. The capital is named Corvane on this site.

Durrel Ridge

Ancestral estate, Warden seat, and continuity house

Both timelines

A rugged highland ancestral seat of the Durrel line, holding the family crypt, Heart Vault, and Tarenus Durrel's tomb.

Timeline B

Controls the passes between Highever and the northern routes before 0 ARV. Re-founded as a publicly Light-aligned Warden holdfast in B9, becomes a resistance centre and house of future continuity in B12, and gains stronger domestic security in B13. By B14 it's the planning and home base for reclaiming Silver Bastion.

Veylaris / Veylaris Rest

Brevan Coast metropolis sequence

Timeline A

Minor fishing colonies in the early era, transformed into an AI metropolis of glass towers during the Choir age. Restored as a cyber-religious metropolis in Campaign IX, then rebuilt on Choir foundations as Veylaris Rest — a free republic of merchants and relic brokers — through Campaigns X–XI.

The Gate

Theocratic capital, later archive ruin

Timeline A

Capital of the Wardens' Theocracy and city of layered law during Campaigns VII–VIII, home to the Gate Codex. The Gate Empire collapses in fire and rebellion at the start of Campaign X, leaving a half-deserted archive city mined for relics by Campaign XI.

Menge / Northern Expanse

Frozen frontier region

Timeline A

A frozen frontier of tribes and Binder conflict in Campaign I, declared beyond redemption after Campaign II. Coastlines fracture into the Verge Archipelago during the Tempest, and the region thaws into new flora and auroras by Campaign XI, with Bastion Saren sleeping beneath the ice.

Timeline B

A harsh snowbound frontier with unconfirmed Icebinder enclaves before 0 ARV. B2 reveals Frostfall and Hollowrest, reclassifying the Icebinders as a fractured Binder lineage rather than a hostile cult; B3 confirms a surviving Frostborn culture at Vyrn's Spring.
