Highever

Realm and capital region

Highever is the political territory surrounding its central fortress, Silver Bastion — see that entry below for the fortress's own detailed history. This entry covers the wider realm: its status, government, and role in the world at large, independent of who currently holds the Bastion itself.

Chronology — Timeline A

I0–27 ARVHome realm of the new Heartwarden order; diplomatic and scholarly ties to Brevair and Menge.
II28–55 ARVBecomes the political heart of the Dominion; Auran Gate, on Highever's approach, is conquered and converted into a foundry and training centre for Veil paladins.
XV–XVI8210–8245 ARVBecomes the Ley-Crown / civic optics capital — nerve-centre for the Concord Charter and the Five Optics (Witness Hand, Line-of-Sight, Three Counts, Exit Shown, Auric Anoint). Anchors one end of the Concord corridor, linked by Corridor Cadence to Tarenus Reclaimed and the Port Annex.
XIX8550–8555 ARVLinks administratively to Aderyn's Gate and Tarenus Reclaimed as part of a three-city harmonised network.

B11148–149 ARVLoses its uncontested Light fortress when Silver Bastion falls — the realm itself remains, but its symbolic centre is now hostile territory.

  • Crown District ("the Crown")

  • Plaza Spine
    Broad ceremonial concourse where Echo Burn crowd tests ran and daylight Regency optics were staged (Campaign XV).
  • Underharbor
    Service quays, floodgates, and hearing halls beneath the canal grid — venue of the Underharbor Tribunal, where paper beats spin (Campaign XV).
  • Hall Quarter

    The old Vigil Bastion training yards, refitted as Aegis muster pads under Badge Unification (Campaign XV).

  • Port Annex (Harbor Satellites)

Silver Bastion

also Obsidian Bastion (Dominion era) · The Veiled Citadel (post-B11) — located within Highever, above

The single most contested structure in the entire chronicle — the same physical fortress changes hands, names, and purpose more than anywhere else in Amora. It's also where both timelines begin: Campaign I and Campaign B1 open in the same chamber, on the same night, with radically different outcomes.

Chronology — Timeline A

CampaignDateState
I0–27 ARVFortress-temple of Luminara; seat of the Paladin Council and the Heartwardens. The Heart Seal is discovered here.
II28–55 ARVReforged as the Obsidian Bastion / Sanctum of the Veil; the Heartwardens are renamed the Veilwardens.
IIIc. 900 ARVReturns as a mixed-faith academy under the New Heartwardens.
IVc. 1900–2000 ARVRises again as the Confluence's seat under Coren Veyne; later becomes a pilgrimage ruin rather than a functioning centre of power.
XIX8550–8555 ARVHouses the Sanctuary of Balance, an academy annex; site of the Purity network's rise and Severance.

Chronology — Timeline B

CampaignDateState
B10 ARVBecomes the founding seat of the Order of Anchors, in the same chamber where Taren and Zethyr Durrel are unmade before their story can begin.
B3–B41–2 ARVThe Frostborn Vault is discovered beneath the Anchor Quarters; the quarters are later classified Resonance Zone 14 after the first Veil-echo purge.
B9136–137 ARVIts Tribunal Undercroft is liberated from a lingering Veil/Shah civic anchor by Taren Durrel.
B11148–149 ARVFalls to Cameron: the internal Light-aligned command structure is dismantled through infiltration and staged collapse, the central chapel is seized and publicly reconsecrated to Shah rather than merely profaned, and the fortress-scale ward-anchor is corrupted through a living conduit rather than brute force. Formally renamed the Veiled Citadel; "Silver Bastion" is killed as a living institutional identity and survives only as historical reference.
B14c. 165–170 ARVReclaimed by a Durrel Ridge-led campaign; returns to Light control.

Notable sub-locations

  • Sanctuary of Balance
    Academy annex where the Purity network's Heart Crown is rekindled and severed (Campaign XIX).
  • Main Chapel
    Former Light ritual chamber, stripped and reconsecrated rather than destroyed when Cameron takes the fortress — Light altars overwritten with black-cloth, iron-lattice, basin-based Shah observance. Site of the first public Shah rite of the new regime and Gavren's public fixing beneath Cameron (Campaign B11).
  • Bastion Ward Chamber (Fortress Ward-Anchor)
    Not the original Heart Ward of Durrel Ridge, but Bastion's closest functional equivalent — a fortress-soul mechanism. Corrupted through a living conduit (Gavren) rather than destroyed; now answers Shah-aligned warding logic instead of Light discipline. Cleansing this chamber is expected to be a central requirement for any future Light retaking (Campaign B11).
  • Tribunal Annex Corridors & the Archivum
    Civic corridors and restricted record stacks distinct from the Tribunal Undercroft itself — used as public-facing power spaces during Taren Durrel's campaign against coercive "hold" architecture, before the finale shifted to cleansing the Undercroft directly (Campaign B9).
  • Anchor Quarters
    Lio and Caen's private rooms; formally designated "R.Z. 14 — Emotional/Resonance Imprint Zone" after the Veil-echo incursion, and fitted with Triad-Echo Barriers, a Stabiliser-Anchor Containment Mesh, and a full Seal-Weave on the doorframe (Campaign B4).
  • Frostborn Vault
    Discovered beneath the Anchor Quarters (Campaign B3).
  • Elder Circle Hall (Council Chamber)
    Site of Caen and Lio's joint report on the first documented inversion purge; formally logged in the Atlas as the "Recorded Site of Triad Echo Purge Debrief" (Campaign B4).
  • South Practice Hall (Training Quarters)
    Referenced but not physically altered; its training implements were briefly misidentified as resonance conduits during echo analysis (Campaign B4).

Evermere

Repeated rebuild site — sanctuary, capital, and port by turns

Evermere is destroyed and rebuilt more times than anywhere else in Timeline A, and separately becomes Timeline B's most detailed civic setting from Campaign B6 onward.

II28–55 ARVAnnexed and given to Zethyr; becomes an imperial pleasure and legal centre.
IIIc. 900 ARVRebuilt as a Collegium.
IVc. 1900–2000 ARVProspers as a rebuilt kingdom.
XIc. 6550 ARVLatest incarnation as a citadel of contracts, home to the Lyricum.
XVII8410–8415 ARVRebuilt atop drowned Concord-era datacores as a candlelit, low-tech city-state; the Red Lattice Quarter becomes its defining district.
XVIII8500–8505 ARVGains the Collegium Vaults, the Archive of Glass, Pilgrims' Square, the Lower Wards, and the Ruined Spires; unified under the Mirror of Law.

CampaignDateState

Notable sub-locations

  • The Red Lattice Quarter
    Evermere's most infamous sanctuary district — information, refuge, and confession (Campaign XVII, echoed in Campaign XVI's "Red Lattice D-3").
  • The Clerk's Hall
    Once an administrative monolith of Concord glass, refitted as a stone-and-timber citadel of record — candles burn where holo-projectors once glowed (Campaign XVII).
  • The Cage
    Reclaimed from an old transport dock, now a marketplace and tribunal arena; its rusted elevators are sealed, and no one remembers how to make them rise (Campaign XVII).
  • The Charity House
    Formerly a relief agency, secretly the Widow's laundering hub — burned and repurposed as an orphanage after the tribunal (Campaign XVII).
  • The Island Refuge
    A two-day sail from the coast; Sorell Marcone's temporary residence and later administrative retreat, outside the city's direct jurisdiction — neutral ground for treaties and quiet exile (Campaign XVII).
  • The Lantern
    Dain Corvett's tavern and safehouse hub; treated informally as neutral ground — dock captains and local operators recognise its presence as deterrence on its own (Campaign B6 onward).
  • Lantern Annex
    A converted dockside warehouse serving as bunkhouse, training space, and evidence storage, kept separate from the tavern to protect civilian crowd safety (Campaign B7).
  • Evermere Underworks & Salt Cellars
    Older cellar networks beneath the dock sheds, historically used by smugglers as movement corridors; cleared of infestations and now actively monitored (Campaign B7).
  • Gloamford
    An inland river-town two days from Evermere, crossed by a black-stone bridge with strict local oversight and marshward approaches that punish careless travel. Functions as a quiet "pressure release" point for dangerous talents and discreet transit, and the practical midpoint between Evermere and the highlands.
  • The Umbral Collegium
    A discreet, non-factional training school for unstable sorcerous manifestations, concealed at Quarry Hollow outside Gloamford. Built around a Null Hall containment chamber for safe de-escalation, plus drill circles for flare control under stress. Where Mark Val trains his Shadow Sorcery (Campaign B7).
  • The Archive of Glass
    A shattered observatory turned mirror-library, its lenses repurposed as focusing mirrors; seat of the Mirror of Law, treated by later scholars as both warning and shrine (Campaign XVIII).
  • Collegium Spire (Ruined Tower)
    A fractured pinnacle overlooking the bay; site of the final convergence and gateway into the Dream of Red Glass. Its upper chambers shimmer translucent at dusk, marking the boundary between mortal Evermere and the dream where Auren and Kael remain (Campaign XVIII).
  • Sable Gate Cisterns
    Ritual floor of the Null council (Campaign XXI).
  • Corvett Estate
    Seat of House Corvett's covert civic-pressure network (Campaign B8).

also Tarenus Keep · Citadel of Inheritance · Inheritance Reclaimed · Free Citadel

This city changes its own name more often than any other location in Amora — five distinct identities across Timeline A alone, each marking a different political regime built on the same ground.

IIIc. 900 ARVRuins explored and the Vault of Tarenus resealed under Edrin's new terms.
XII7400–7410 ARVRebuilt as Inheritance-Reclaimed, a durable republican centre under Kael Durreth's Harmony Network.
XIII7420–7421 ARVBecomes the Citadel of Inheritance; falls into the Free Citadel, a city of archives rather than edicts, after the Chain's Severance.
XV–XVI8210–8245 ARVFunctions as Tarenus Reclaimed, the administrative/commerce core of the Concord corridor.
XIX–XX8550–8620 ARVCompletes a three-node network with Highever and Aderyn's Gate; the Vault of Tarenus stirs nightly, leading to Campaign XX's Dual-Key stabilisation.

Chronology — Timeline B

CampaignDateState
B53–4 ARVA Bastion-aligned inland city with residual buried vault structures (referenced back in Campaign V); site of Lio and Caen's investigation into Shah-aligned ritual influence, and where Caen receives his Veil imprint at Greymount's conduit beneath Rillhaven.

Notable sub-locations

  • The Vault of Tarenus
    Sealed by Edrin Durrel (Campaign III); reopened and stabilised as the Dual-Key Heart-shard chamber (Campaign XX); catalogued with new sub-levels including the Ravel Protocol.
  • Hall of Memory / Anchor Vault
    Where the Chain's origin is uncovered and severed; part of the Upper City alongside the Council Spires and Collegium Archives. The Anchor Vault itself sits sealed in the substructure beneath the Hall (Campaign XIII).
  • Forge District (Lower City)
    The former industrial quarter, converted to civilian housing after the Severance (Campaign XIII).
  • Western Surge
    A storm-basin plateau roughly 110 leagues north via the Spine Highway; site of the Chain's physical resurgence as a ley-storm (Campaign XIII).
  • Anchor Vale (Silent Pass)
    Northern valleys where Rian Leth and Calen Vorr settle after the campaign — the "Quiet Years" region, forge by a river (Campaign XIII epilogue).

Brevair

Timeline A capital renamed Durrelion · Timeline B capital named Corvane

Brevair's starting relationship with Highever is genuinely different between the two timelines, not the same premise playing out two ways. Timeline A begins diplomatically engaged and becomes a crown ally through the Heartwardens' work; Timeline B begins as an isolationist, militarised rival under Emperor Trainor Fell.

Chronology — Timeline A

CampaignDateState
I0–27 ARVBecomes a crown ally to the new Heartwarden order.
II28–55 ARVAnnexed; capital renamed Durrelion, rebuilt in black stone under the Dominion.
IIIc. 900 ARVDissolved into independent city-states.

Chronology — Timeline B

CampaignDateState
Pre-0 ARV—Isolationist, militarised empire under Emperor Trainor Fell — palace, barracks, drill-yards, ministerial halls, coastal forts, and inland garrisons. Not a crown ally at this point.
B53–4 ARVEmperor Trainor Fell is assassinated, producing internal instability and shifting regional power toward Bastion.
B10146–147 ARVHouse Valecour's engineered collapse and rebirth leaves Rhalen Valecour as Brevair's uncrowned ruler — capital named Corvane on this site (see the Canon Ledger). His rule spreads through dependency rather than conquest: widow houses become a principal channel for quiet consolidation, chapel and charity funds are administratively redirected rather than openly captured, and merchant/magistrate authority increasingly answers to Rhalen's systems while misreading Delryn as the "acceptable" public face of a reformed house.

Notable sub-locations

  • House Valecour
    Noble house at the centre of Campaign B10's political engineering; becomes a political instrument disguised as a restored noble house. Key rooms: the West Salon/West Wing (private governance and disclosure site, treated as part of the house's inner ruling architecture, not ordinary quarters); the Old Music Room's hidden piano cache (where records proving Rhalen and Seran's long collaboration are found); and the former Chapter Council Chamber, where Rhalen poisons the surviving inner circle — the regime-turning room that ends chapter pluralism for good.

Durrel Ridge

Ancestral estate, Warden seat, and continuity house

The ancestral home of the Durrel line, predating Campaign I itself. Its family crypt houses statues of the first Heartwardens and the vault where the Heart Seal was originally found — founded generations before Campaign I by Tarenus Durrel, Taren's ancestor. By the time Zethyr and Taren arrive at Silver Bastion, the order Tarenus founded had lapsed; they revive it rather than start it fresh. Appears only as background lineage estate in Timeline A, but becomes one of Timeline B's most important recurring settings from Campaign B7 onward — the site that carries the Durrel line's institutional weight for the second half of that timeline's current record.

Chronology — Timeline B

CampaignDateState
B785 ARVFirst appearance in Timeline B; a mutual-aid pact is formed with The Lantern in Evermere.
B9136–137 ARVReclaimed and cleansed by Taren Durrel; rebuilt as a Warden institution anchored in witness law.
B12150 ARVRe-centres as command-centre and lived household under Jack Durrel; a cadet-branch Durrel child is brought here.
B13c. 162 ARVThe cadet-branch child, now Caelen Durrel, is targeted and reclaimed by Thorn House; Vault access wards are strengthened afterward.
B14c. 165–170 ARVServes as the planning and staging base for reclaiming Silver Bastion.

Notable sub-locations

  • Tarenus's Vault (Ridge)
    Not the same chamber as Greymount's Vault of Tarenus — a separate truth-mechanism beneath the Ridge itself, responding to bloodline, words, and witness (Campaigns B9, B12, B13).
  • Family Crypt & Heart Vault
    Beneath the Ridge; houses a statue hall of early Heartwarden figures and Tarenus Durrel's own entombment site. Restricted access, used for stewardship rites and controlled legacy custody (Campaign B7).
  • Element Hall
    Seal-linked stewardship plaques and a training circle, functioning as both memorial and practical discipline space. Became central to the "Ridge Leak" sting operation — its dead-drop niches are now monitored (Campaign B7).
  • Ridge Gatehouse
    Operational security node controlling passes and patrol tags; patrol-tag manipulation was the key mechanism behind the B7 leak. Procedures tightened afterward beyond single-clerk dependence.
  • Cindergate Ford
    A river-crossing choke point on the route between the Ridge and Evermere, introduced during escort operations; remains a future hotspot for bandit pressure (Campaign B7).
  • Jack's House at Durrel Ridge
    Jack Durrel's inherited residence, associated at first with absence and grief; reopened, repopulated, and established as a lived domestic centre for his household. One of the campaign's most important spatial shifts — a dead inheritance-site becoming a living resistance-household (Campaign B12).

Menge / Northern Expanse

Frozen frontier region

Menge is the clearest example of a region whose entire reputation changes between timelines — treated as a hostile threat in Timeline A, reclassified as a wronged people in Timeline B.

Notable sub-locations

  • Bastion Saren
    Frozen pre-Dominion relay fortress; site of Clause XI's completion (Campaign XI).
  • Hollowrest Citadel
    Ancient structure predating the Heart, Flame, and Stone Seals (Campaign B2).
  • Vyrn's Spring
    A hidden Frostbound enclave carved into the Northern Ridges above Silver Bastion, warmed by a geothermal Fissure. Notable features include the Watcher Tunnels (training and travel, kin-only access), the Training Ring (its sigils respond to Caen's bloodline), the Resonance Wells where Frostbound craft ice-signature tools, and the Marker of Caelen — a stele marking where Caen's mother fell during a storm, inscribed "she carried her son into the storm." Holds one half of a watcher-bridge Sending Stone paired with Caen's own, giving Bastion and the Spring a permanent communication link (Campaign B3).

4CE Territories

Caelwick, East Vanmere, Academy of Resonant Studies — new geography introduced after the 1,000-year jump

These locations only exist in the final stretch of Timeline A (Campaigns XXII–XXV) — entirely new geography introduced after the thousand-year jump, with no equivalent earlier in either timeline.

Chronology — Timeline A

CampaignDateState
XXII9650–9660 ARVCaelwick Frontier Post: a rainswept charter outpost (population ~2,400) built atop a collapsed Dominion scribe-vault, where the Arden/Caelen shard-pattern imprint first forms. Designated Fault-01: the Caelwick Node — the first identified Quiet Fault on the continent. The Under-Pump Galleries nearby are separately classed a Tier-2 Quiet Zone: no active arcane power, but architecture that still listens for command.
XXIII9670–9680 ARVEast Vanmere / Vanmere city: the shard reinstantiates Arden and Caelen a millennium later; the Rainshelf district becomes Arden's community after Caelen's death.
XXIV–XXV9700–9800 ARVAcademy of Resonant Studies: built to train stabilisers and anchors for the post-Dominion age; site of the world's first Living Dyad and, ultimately, the Fracture that ends Timeline A.

Regions flagged but unaffected

  • The Vanmere Heartlands
    Stable, no recorded faults — but folklore after Campaign XXII calls it "the land that sleeps with one eye open."
  • The Sunrise Barrows
    Unrelated to the shard event, but flagged for stone that carries a faint post-Severance resonance of its own.

Notable sub-locations

  • The Courtyard
    An open-air quadrangle between the Academy's towers, originally a grid-stabilised training yard; becomes the first recorded Resonance Burst Zone when Zeth's uncontrolled aura, amplified by the shard-presence, causes a radial shockwave (Campaign XXIV).
  • The Containment Chamber
    A cylindrical deep-structure vault beneath the Academy, hybridising inert Concord shielding with 4CE gridcode dampeners. Site of Zeth's early second-layer activation, with Arden providing Safe Anchor oversight (Campaign XXIV).
  • East Archive Wing
    A library-laboratory hybrid where Zeth studies second-layer lattices and Talren repeatedly grounds him — the Academy's first recorded instance of a dyadic lattice filament forming between two people (Campaign XXIV).
  • Dyadic Lattice Threshold
    Not a physical location — a conceptual magical zone reached only through fully merged resonance. Zeth and Talren reach its threshold without full entry, the first empirical proof of a third magical layer existing anywhere post-Severance (Campaign XXIV).
