Timeline A · Campaign XII

Age of Inheritance

Post-First-Cyberpunk Medieval Interlude

7400–7410 ARV Primary location: Inheritance-Reclaimed

Opening World State

The world rebuilds in a quasi-medieval frame after the machine and cyber ages of Campaigns IX–XI. The First Cyberpunk Era's technology-driven republics have receded, Highever and Evermere have shifted roles again, and civic authority is being rebuilt around memory, stewardship, and institutions that promise continuity without restoring the old Dominion outright. Inheritance-Reclaimed becomes the centre of that experiment.

Party & Key Figures

  • Evren Thalos PC Hidden Ledger scholar and co-architect; supplies historical suspicion and repeatedly asks who would be harmed when temporary civic solutions are made permanent. Partner to Kael Durreth.
  • Kael Durreth Antagonist Prime Steward and Oath of Inheritance paladin. Founder of Inheritance-Reclaimed and principal architect of the Harmony Network — brilliant, effective, and increasingly willing to treat centralisation itself as proof of civic virtue. (Spelled Durreth per the site's Dur-name convention; see the Canon Ledger.)
  • Seraphine Vale
    Commander overseeing the Sentinel build-out that protects the new institutions.
  • Jalen / Joren
    Tavern-keeper and personal tie to Kael; keeps the human world around the central pair alive beyond councils and civic architecture.
  • Cassius
    Envoy and trader; opens trade and diplomatic routes to Harn.

Act-by-Act

Building the Republic

Evren works beside Kael to recover old civic knowledge and give it usable public form. Their partnership is intimate as well as architectural: Kael supplies authority, confidence, and the ability to make a new institution function; Evren supplies the Hidden Ledger's long memory and the habit of asking what a successful institution can become once nobody remembers why its limits were there. Inheritance-Reclaimed grows around that tension rather than around one uncontested founder.

The Harmony Network

The Republic consolidates around the Harmony Network, a leygrid intended to bind civic memory to duty without restoring the old Dominion. It succeeds spectacularly: administration becomes coherent, public services stabilise, and the new state can coordinate across distance with a reliability earlier governments could only imitate. Evren's concern is not that the system fails. It is that it works well enough for obedience to begin feeling indistinguishable from participation.

Inheritance and Warning

Kael increasingly treats the Network's success as evidence that tighter integration is justified, while Evren keeps measuring that achievement against the histories the Hidden Ledger exists to preserve. The campaign therefore closes with a functioning republic and a genuine moral fracture inside its foundation: nothing proves Kael set out to build the compulsory order that follows, but the architecture he champions is exactly what the next Council will try to make permanent. Seraphine oversees the Sentinel build-out protecting the new institutions; Cassius opens trade and diplomatic routes to Harn so Inheritance-Reclaimed does not become an isolated civic shrine; and Jalen — also recorded as Joren — keeps the private world around Kael and Evren visible as the public one hardens.

World State After Campaign XII

Geography. Highever is rebuilt; Tarenus Reclaimed and Evermere shift roles during this quasi-medieval transition. Inheritance-Reclaimed becomes a durable republican centre, protected by Seraphine and connected outward through Cassius and the Harn trade frameworks.

Magic. The Harmony Network is a leygrid, not a Choir engine or the Veil — a genuinely new attempt to bind civic memory to duty using lessons inherited from Clause XI's Breathing Law. Its danger is institutional rather than secretly predetermined: a system intended to support participation can still make obedience feel virtuous when authority decides harmony matters more than refusal.

The Durrel Line. The surviving Campaign XII material does not securely establish Kael Durreth as a blood descendant of the Durrels. His surname and the recurrence of familiar authority patterns make the comparison historically tempting, but resemblance is not genealogy. Until a lineage source proves otherwise, Kael remains a consequential Inheritance-era figure rather than a confirmed continuation of the Durrel bloodline.

Institutions. Kael Durreth and Evren Thalos establish the civic architecture inherited by the next generation. Kael is remembered as the Republic's principal public architect; Evren is the internal critical voice who refuses to let success erase the question of consent. The campaign ends with the Age of Inheritance secure on the surface and the unresolved intent behind its strongest systems leading directly into Campaign XIII.

Canon status: corrected to the fuller campaign record. Evren Thalos is Campaign XII's player character and Kael Durreth occupies the antagonist / centralising counterpart role.
