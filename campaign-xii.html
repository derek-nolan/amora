Timeline A · Campaign XII

Age of Inheritance

Post-First-Cyberpunk Medieval Interlude

7400–7410 ARV Primary location: Inheritance–Reclaimed

Opening World State

The world rebuilds in a quasi-medieval frame after the machine and cyber ages of Campaigns IX–XI — the First Cyberpunk Era's tech-driven republics have receded, and Highever and Evermere shift roles once again as the pendulum swings back toward a more grounded, personal scale of politics.

Party & Key Figures

  • Evren Thalos PC Hidden Ledger scholar and co-architect; supplies historical suspicion and asks who would be harmed by permanence. Partner to Kael Durreth.
  • Kael Durreth Antagonist Prime Steward; Oath of Inheritance paladin. Founder of Inheritance-Reclaimed — the Harmony/centralisation architecture Evren's own suspicion is ultimately measured against. (Spelled Durreth per the site's Dur-name convention — see the Canon Ledger.)
  • Seraphine Vale
    Commander overseeing the Sentinel build-out that protects the new institutions.
  • Jalen / Joren
    Tavern-keeper, personal tie to Kael; keeps the human world around him alive beyond councils and oaths.
  • Cassius
    Envoy and trader; opens trade and diplomatic routes to Harn.

Evren Thalos is confirmed here as this campaign's actual PC, with Kael Durreth in the antagonist role — a correction to this site's own earlier framing, which had the two reversed. See the Canon Ledger for the full correction. The act-by-act account below was written under the old framing and still narrates events from Kael's perspective; treat the events themselves as accurate and the point-of-view as pending a fuller rewrite.

Act-by-Act

Building the Republic

Kael Durreth, Prime Steward, works beside Evren Thalos of the Hidden Ledger to recover old civic knowledge and give it public form. Their partnership is both intimate and architectural: Kael supplies authority and conviction; Evren supplies historical suspicion and the habit of asking who would be harmed by permanence.

The Harmony Network

The Republic consolidates around the Harmony Network, a leygrid intended to bind civic memory to duty without restoring the old Dominion. It succeeds spectacularly — administration becomes coherent, public services stabilise, and citizens experience order as participation rather than occupation.

A Dangerous Foundation

The network also makes obedience feel virtuous. Kael's achievements become the foundation the next Council will try to make harmony compulsory — Campaign XII therefore ends in triumph shadowed by ambiguity. Commander Seraphine Vale oversees the Sentinel build-out that protects the new institutions; Cassius opens trade and diplomatic routes to Harn, ensuring Inheritance-Reclaimed doesn't become an isolated shrine to its own importance; Jalen — also recorded as Joren — keeps the personal world around Kael alive beyond councils and oaths.

World State After Campaign XII

Geography. Highever is rebuilt; Tarenus Reclaimed and Evermere shift roles during this quasi-medieval transition. Inheritance-Reclaimed becomes a durable republican centre, protected by Seraphine and connected outward through Cassius and the Harn trade frameworks.

Magic. The Harmony Network is a leygrid, not a Choir engine or Veil — a genuinely new attempt to bind civic memory to duty using lessons learned from Clause XI's Breathing Law, though it repeats the same underlying error of making obedience feel virtuous.

The Durrel Line. Kael Durreth represents the Durrel name having drifted, over the roughly 850 years since Campaign X, into a new spelling — evidence the line persists and continues producing leaders, even as the name itself erodes with time.

Institutions. Kael Durreth and Evren Thalos establish the Harmony Network as a civic inheritance system, giving the next generation both a functioning republic and a dangerous temptation toward compulsory order. The campaign closes with the Age of Inheritance secure on the surface, while the moral ambiguity of Kael's achievement leads directly into the Chains of Inheritance in Campaign XIII.

Canon status: this is the corrected cast for Campaign XII — the concise Timeline A summary elsewhere conflates this campaign with Arden and Caelen, who don't actually enter the story until the later Shattered Quiet/4CE arc.
