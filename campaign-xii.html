Timeline A · Campaign XII

Age of Inheritance

Pre-4CE Pivot

7400–7410 ARV Primary location: Inheritance–Reclaimed

Opening World State

A transitional era: the world rebuilds in a quasi-medieval frame after the machine and cyber ages, before it eventually leaps forward to 4CE. Highever is rebuilt; Tarenus Reclaimed and Evermere shift roles.

Party & Key Figures

  • Kael Durreth
    Prime Steward; Oath of Inheritance paladin. Founder of Inheritance-Reclaimed, partner to Evren Thalos.
  • Evren Thalos
    Hidden Ledger scholar and co-architect; supplies historical suspicion and asks who would be harmed by permanence.
  • Seraphine Vale
    Commander overseeing the Sentinel build-out that protects the new institutions.
  • Jalen / Joren
    Tavern-keeper, personal tie to Kael; keeps the human world around him alive beyond councils and oaths.
  • Cassius
    Envoy and trader; opens trade and diplomatic routes to Harn.

Act-by-Act

Building the Republic

Kael Durreth, Prime Steward, works beside Evren Thalos of the Hidden Ledger to recover old civic knowledge and give it public form — Kael supplying authority and conviction, Evren supplying historical suspicion.

The Harmony Network

The Republic consolidates around the Harmony Network, a leygrid intended to bind civic memory to duty without restoring the old Dominion. It succeeds spectacularly — administration becomes coherent, and citizens experience order as participation.

A Dangerous Foundation

The network also makes obedience feel virtuous. Kael's achievements become the foundation the next Council will try to make harmony compulsory — Campaign XII ends in triumph shadowed by ambiguity.

Final World State

Inheritance-Reclaimed becomes a durable republican centre, protected by Seraphine and connected outward through Cassius and the Harn trade frameworks. The campaign closes with the Age of Inheritance secure on the surface, while the moral ambiguity of Kael's achievement leads directly into the Chains of Inheritance.

Canon status: this is the corrected cast for Campaign XII — the concise Timeline A summary elsewhere conflates this campaign with Arden and Caelen, who don't actually enter the story until the later Shattered Quiet/4CE arc.
