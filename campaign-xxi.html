Timeline A · Campaign XXI

The Last Key

Late Concord Afterglow

8650–8660 ARV Primary location: Evermere, Sable Gate Cisterns

Opening World State

A generation after the Vault Unbound; the world edges toward a partial medievalisation again — tech receding, institutions ossifying, frontier conflicts brewing. The Collegium's Unbirth project aims to print a final chant to end recurrence.

Party & Key Figures

  • Talen Marric
    Custodian and bearer of a live Veil shard; co-steward of the Null Compact in Evermere. Speaks the Severance that ends ley-heir recursion. Partner to Seren Valor.
  • Seren Valor
    Former Warden Captain; guardian and partner to Talen. Stabilizes the ritual circle and advocates "no martyrs."
  • Rook Halvern
    Null ritual technician and ex-Custodian vows-master; inverts rune-logic during the Red Choir gambit.
  • Councilor Avar
    Null Council leader and scholar-priest; convenes the quiet ring-rite for the Severance.

Act-by-Act

The Unbirth Plates

Rumors of sanctioned erasures draw Talen and Seren to the Nulls below Sable Gate, where they learn the Unbirth plates target the Heart-Veil itself.

The Red Choir Gambit

At Annex V, Talen speaks a counter-verse, Rook inverts the runes, and Seren pins the fighting core — dusting the plate and turning chant into static. In the ash, the ley traces an open-ring: recognition of intent, not obedience.

Severance Spoken

At the pedestal rite, the shard shows that the world mirrors the actor's intent. In council, Talen chooses: "No chains, no heirs, only choice." The Heart-Veil unwinds; the metronome stops; the future returns to mortals.

Final World State

No new ley-heirs are made; the Taren/Zethyr recursion recedes to legend, not machinery. Annex V is re-tooled as the Hall of Paper, issuing law as revocable charters that sunset unless renewed. Historians mark the incoming epoch as the Age of Forgotten Kings — a deliberate reversion toward a truer medieval world where borders are human, not hummed.

Canon status: follows the character register directly; this campaign closes the Concord Afterglow era and sets up the eventual 1,000-year jump into 4CE.
