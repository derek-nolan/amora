Timeline B · Campaign B9

The Renewed Oath of the Ridge

—

136–137 ARV Primary location: Durrel Ridge, Silver Bastion

Core Premise

Roughly 50 years after Campaign B8, and the first Timeline B campaign to put a Durrel front and centre as protagonist. Taren Durrel begins at Durrel Ridge, where family legacy and hidden rites intensify. He undergoes Warden induction and is drawn into deeper Durrel history and secret architecture beneath the estate — where structures respond to bloodline, words, and witness, not mere ceremony.

Party & Key Figures

  • Taren Durrel PC Alive; player character, 20 years old
    Paladin on the Oath of the Ridge, later Heartwarded and publicly aligned to Luminara. (Source spells this "Taren Durell"; normalized to Durrel here.)
  • Mara Alive; appointed Captain of the Wardens
    Archer and Taren's closest confidant.
  • Cassien Alive; appointed Arcane Warden
    Arcane specialist, appointed under witness rules.
  • Arden (Ridge-era) Killed by Mara
    Initially a close companion, later revealed as the campaign's tyrant, using coercive systems and Veil methods to control the Ridge. A different figure from Arden Vale of the Six Travellers — same name, unrelated character.

Act-by-Act

The Vault Opens

Taren descends repeatedly into Tarenus's Vault beneath the Ridge, confronting an ancient truth-mechanism — script chambers and seal logic that demand witness and reject coercive authority, responding directly to the Warden bloodline. This is the first Durrel-specific vault architecture seen in Timeline B, distinct from anything in Campaigns B1–B8.

Arden's Regime

Arden's influence becomes systemic: a constructed "hold" architecture designed to make obedience feel like safety, spreading control rituals beyond the Ridge into Bastion's civic machinery — a Timeline B echo of the same institutional-control pattern that recurred throughout Timeline A (the Gate Codex, the Harmony Network, the Chain).

The Breach and Arden's Death

A breach reveals two bodies outside Taren's door. Mara kills Arden with a single arrow. His death doesn't instantly free Taren — the hold architecture still searches for a handler — but it breaks the central authority.

The Kill-Switch

Taren, Mara, and Cassien void Arden's hold under true witness and destroy the Root Engine chamber's central structure. The Ridge is cleansed of collaborators, and Taren formally reclaims Tarenus's Vault, turning decisively toward the Light.

Liberating Bastion

Taren leads a unified force to Bastion's Tribunal Undercroft, invoking Heart and Luminara under true witness to break the lingering Veil/Shah civic anchor — returning the stone to neutral legitimacy rather than conquering it. Silver Bastion, established as the Order of Anchors' seat back in Campaign B1, is confirmed still standing and politically contested 136 years later.

World State After Campaign B9

Geography. Durrel Ridge is reclaimed, cleansed, and publicly Light-aligned, rebuilt as a Warden institution anchored in witness law. Tarenus's Vault beneath it is formally reclaimed by Taren.

Magic. The Vault's truth-mechanism responds specifically to Warden bloodline, words, and witness — the first Durrel-specific magic system seen in Timeline B, distinct from the Heartborn/Thread/Frostborn/Veil-Echo categories established in the earlier campaigns.

The Durrel Line. Taren Durrel becomes Timeline B's first major Durrel protagonist, swearing the Renewed Oath of the Ridge — sanctuary, witness, consent, and "Light with teeth" — with Mara as Captain of the Wardens and Cassien as Arcane Warden.

Institutions. Bastion is liberated at its civic anchor point, though the chain is confirmed as a broader phenomenon that could resurface elsewhere. This establishes Durrel Ridge as a major Light-aligned power centre for the rest of Timeline B, directly setting up its role as a resistance and continuity centre in Campaigns B12–B14.

Canon status: "Taren Durell" in the source is normalized to Taren Durrel here. Note the Arden of this campaign is a distinct figure from Arden Vale of the Six Travellers — same name, unrelated character.
