Core Premise

Roughly 50 years after Campaign B8, and the first Timeline B campaign to put a Durrel front and centre as protagonist. Taren Durrel, 20, begins at Durrel Ridge, where family legacy and hidden rites intensify. He undergoes Warden induction and is drawn into deeper Durrel history and secret architecture beneath the estate — structures that respond to bloodline, words, and witness, not mere ceremony. His authority is framed at first as simply ceremonial; the campaign quickly reveals it's anything but.

Party & Key Figures

  • Taren Durrel PC Paladin on the Oath of the Ridge, later Heartwarded and publicly aligned to Luminara. (Source spells this "Taren Durell"; normalized to Durrel here.)
  • Mara
    Archer and Taren's closest confidant; her banter and loyalty ground him even as the legacy deepens into something darker. Later appointed Captain of the Wardens.
  • Cassien
    Arcane specialist, wizard front with darker power implied; later appointed Arcane Warden, constrained by witness rules and explicit prohibitions against coercive rites.
  • Arden (Ridge-era)
    Initially positioned as a close companion and operative; later revealed as the campaign's tyrant, using coercive systems and Veil methods to control the Ridge. A different figure from Arden Vale of the Six Travellers — same name, unrelated character. Killed by Mara.
  • Luminara Delegation
    A paladin-captain and a high cleric arrive post-liberation to formally bless the Ridge and establish its public Light alignment.
  • Silas & Rook
    Brief "spares" who appear early; confirmed dead at the breach moment outside Taren's door.

Act-by-Act

Act I — The Warden Made

Taren begins at Durrel Ridge, undergoing Warden induction as family legacy and hidden rites intensify around him. Mara and Cassien remain his emotional centre throughout, even as what "Warden" actually means starts to reveal itself as far more than ceremony — a responsibility, and just as much a vulnerability.

Act II — The Vault Opens and the Chain Appears

Taren descends repeatedly into Tarenus's Vault beneath the Ridge, and what starts as exploring his own legacy becomes confrontation with an ancient truth-mechanism: script chambers, seal logic, and "hearing" structures that demand witness, reject coercive authority outright, and respond directly to the Warden bloodline. The campaign layers in a dangerous understructure alongside it — annexed corridor systems that shift and "learn," a scribe-stone system that cross-examines power itself, and a deeper Root Engine chamber that asserts principle over procedure. Through it all, Taren keeps reaching back toward Tarenus and toward the Light — not naive purity, but a genuine return to truth, consent, and witness.

Act III — Arden's Regime

Arden's influence turns overt and systemic. Taren's Warden authority gets shaped, leveraged, and at times outright coerced through a constructed "hold" architecture — Seat/Return/clearance logic designed to make obedience feel like safety. This isn't simple cruelty; it's infrastructure, control embedded into ritual, language, law-adjacent spaces, and the body itself, spreading beyond the Ridge into Bastion's civic machinery and public-adjacent environments. Multiple hearings play out in the script system; the stone keeps demanding true witness and eventually forces the existence of a neutral witness chamber where consent simply can't be faked. Arden tries to counterfeit witness and reroute the remedies — the stone rejects every proxy and escalates, until it collides directly with the Root Engine.

Act IV — The Breach and Arden's Death

At the height of Arden's control, a breach occurs: two bodies slump outside Taren's door, later confirmed as Silas and Rook. The door is blasted off its hinges. Mara and Cassien enter calm and decisive, and Mara looses a single arrow through Arden's head, killing him instantly. His death doesn't instantly free Taren — the hold architecture keeps searching for a handler endpoint — but it breaks the central authority holding it together, and the party rushes for the Vault to end the system at its roots.

Act V — The Kill-Switch

Taren, Mara, and Cassien move through the understructure, enter the neutral witness space, and formally void Arden's hold under true witness, then destroy the Root Engine chamber's central structure outright — shattering the mechanism that could have kept perpetuating coercive "holding" long after Arden himself was gone. With Arden's followers still embedded at the Ridge, the campaign pivots to cleansing the estate: loyal staff and Wardens rise up, a small but highly placed collaborator core is identified and purged, and the murder of Seren Durrel is confirmed as ordered under Arden's regime, its collaborators publicly executed under Warden authority. Taren then formally reclaims Tarenus's Vault, channelling Heart and turning decisively toward the Light.

Act VI — The Lighted Ridge

A Luminara delegation arrives in response to the crisis. Taren requests formal blessing: the Ridge is publicly aligned to Luminara, though the Vault's blessing stays chapter-secret, never made public. A Sun Chapel is established at the Ridge, and Taren swears the Renewed Oath of the Ridge — sanctuary, witness, consent, and "Light with teeth." Leadership is formalised alongside it: Mara becomes Captain of the Wardens, Cassien becomes Arcane Warden under explicit witness-rule constraints.

Act VII — Liberating Bastion

Rather than wait for politics to come to the Ridge, Taren leads a unified force to Bastion itself, descending with the Light delegation into the Tribunal Undercroft — the civic stone-space where deep influence had embedded itself since the shifts of Campaign B5. Taren invokes Heart and Luminara under true witness, and the cleansing breaks Bastion's lingering Veil/Shah civic anchor, returning the stone to neutral legitimacy and establishing a covenant mark that actively resists coercive holding going forward. Bastion isn't conquered or annexed. It's returned.

World State After Campaign B9

Geography. Durrel Ridge is reclaimed, cleansed, and publicly Light-aligned, rebuilt as a Warden institution anchored in witness law, complete with its own Sun Chapel. Tarenus's Vault is reclaimed under Heart and Light and kept as chapter-secret. Bastion is liberated at its civic anchor point, stripping away long-standing Veil/Shah influence.

Magic. The Vault's truth-mechanism responds specifically to Warden bloodline, words, and witness — the first Durrel-specific magic system seen in Timeline B, distinct from the Heartborn/Thread/Frostborn/Veil-Echo categories established in the earlier campaigns. Arden is dead and burned, his personal regime collapsed — but the campaign is explicit that the "chain" architecture he used is a broader phenomenon, not unique to him.

The Durrel Line. Taren Durrel becomes Timeline B's first major Durrel protagonist, swearing the Renewed Oath of the Ridge with Mara as Captain of the Wardens and Cassien as Arcane Warden. Seren Durrel's murder under Arden's regime is formally confirmed and avenged.

Institutions. This establishes Durrel Ridge as a major Light-aligned power centre for the rest of Timeline B, directly setting up its role as a resistance and continuity centre in Campaigns B12–B14. Threads remain open for future campaigns: external actors including a hinted "Ash" network, lingering scars in the world's deeper systems, and the ongoing risk of coercive structures re-emerging somewhere else entirely. Taren, Mara, and Cassien all reach a milestone Level 4 at the campaign's close.

Canon status: "Taren Durell" in the source is normalized to Taren Durrel here. Note the Arden of this campaign is a distinct figure from Arden Vale of the Six Travellers — same name, unrelated character.
