Timeline B · Campaign B1 / XXVI

The Anchor at Silver Bastion

Age of Founding, Reforged

0 ARV Primary location: Silver Bastion, Highever

Core Premise

A rupture of violet light drags six travellers from a distant future into Silver Bastion at the moment the Heart Seal would have awakened. Their arrival triggers a lethal predation: Taren Durrel and Zethyr Durrel are unmade before their story can begin. The world diverges immediately — this is the moment Timeline B becomes its own history.

Party & Key Figures

  • Lio Serrin
    Squire of the Silver Dawn; becomes the first native Anchor of Timeline B. (Later renamed Vareth during B5.)
  • Kellan Vance
    Senior paladin; becomes Lio's Thread and stabiliser. (Later recorded consistently as Caen.)
  • The Six Travellers
    From a sealed future: Arden Vale, Rian Thale, Zeth Caden, Talren Vale, Solan, Marrek.
  • Seraphine Vale
    High Paladin of Silver Bastion; founder of the Order of Anchors.

Act-by-Act

Act I — The Unmaking and the First Bond

A catastrophic violet rupture tears through the Heart Seal chamber. Taren and Zethyr Durrel are erased by a third-layer force — not killed, not corrupted, but unmade, preventing all future cycles known from Timeline A. Six travellers are thrown into the chamber. Lio leaps to protect Kellan, taking the brunt of the rupture's backlash; this act of devotion imprints him as Anchor. Kellan stabilises him, and the Heart Seal chooses the two as its new dyadic core.

Act II — The Highland Hunt & The First Mind-Spawn

Tracks in the frost lead the party into the highlands, where a Mind-spawn attempts to seize Solan's unstable resonance. Lio and Kellan fight as one — Anchor and Thread working instinctively in tandem. Resonance techniques from the travellers are introduced into the timeline centuries early. The Mind-spawn is destroyed; the entity behind it retreats.

Act III — Founding of the Order of Anchors & Destruction of the Mind

Seraphine convenes the Council to hear the travellers' testimony. The Order of Anchors is sanctioned. A final incursion of the Mind erupts beneath the Bastion; Lio and Kellan together obliterate it, removing the third-layer predator before it can shape the early world.

Final World State

The Order of Anchors is formally founded at Silver Bastion. Highever becomes the continental centre for rupture management. With Taren and Zethyr unmade, the Dominion can never form in this timeline — but resonance, dyadic structures, and the Anchor Web now exist far earlier than they ever did in Timeline A.

Canon status: confirmed. Name variants in this record: Lio Serrin → Vareth (from B5 onward); Kellan Vance → Caen (later chapters).
