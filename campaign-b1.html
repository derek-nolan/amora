Core Premise

Silver Bastion begins this campaign as a stable diplomatic power, mid-preparation for early Seal rites — Taren and Zethyr Durrel are still alive as paladin candidates when it opens. A rupture of violet light tears through the Heart Seal chamber during early-morning rites and drags six travellers from a sealed future into that same chamber, at the exact moment the Heart Seal would have awakened for Taren and Zethyr. Their arrival triggers a lethal predation — a third-layer force later named simply "the Mind" unmakes Taren and Zethyr before their story can begin, not killing them so much as erasing them, preventing every future cycle known from Timeline A. The world diverges immediately. The Heart Seal, its intended anchors gone, seeks new ones — and chooses Lio Serrin and Kellan Vance instead.

Act-by-Act

Act I — The Unmaking and the First Bond

The rupture tears through the Heart Seal chamber during early-morning rites, and Taren and Zethyr Durrel are erased by the third-layer force behind it — not killed, not corrupted, unmade, in a way that prevents every future cycle Timeline A ever recorded. Six travellers are thrown into the same chamber, displaced from the far end of Timeline A's history. Lio leaps to protect Kellan from the rupture's backlash, taking the brunt of it himself; that single act of devotion is what imprints him as Anchor. Kellan stabilises him in turn, and the Heart Seal responds by choosing the two of them as its new dyadic core. Their combined resonance flares outward and forms what will come to be called the Anchor Web, binding all six travellers to the Seal as stabilisers in the same instant. See the Magic Codex for why this same moment destroys Taren and Zethyr while completing Lio and Kellan's bond instead.

Act II — The Highland Hunt & the First Mind-Spawn

Frost tracks outside Silver Bastion lead Lio, Kellan, and the six travellers into the Highever Highlands, where a creature of violet distortion — a Mind-spawn — attempts to seize Solan's still-unstable resonance. Lio and Kellan fight it as one, Anchor and Thread working in instinctive tandem for the first time. In the process, resonance techniques the travellers carry from their own era — pulse, fold, drop — enter Timeline B centuries before this world would otherwise have discovered them on its own. The Mind-spawn itself is destroyed; the entity behind it, wounded, retreats deeper into what the travellers call the third layer. Kellan and Lio's bond crystallises into a permanent dyad-thread in the fighting — the first of its kind this era has ever seen.

Act III — Founding of the Order of Anchors & Destruction of the Mind

Seraphine convenes the Highever Council to hear the travellers' testimony in full — the sealed future, the lost cycle, the danger inherent in Dominion-style logic, and the plain truth of what happened to Taren and Zethyr. The Council recognises the rupture unanimously as a world-scale threat and sanctions a standing order to answer it: the Order of Anchors, charged with stabilising seals, containing ruptures, and safeguarding the metaphysics of an age that no longer has any Dominion to fall back on. In Lio and Kellan's presence, the Heart Seal awakens further still, revealing sigils in the stonework that were never present in Timeline A's version of this chamber. A final incursion of the Mind erupts beneath the Bastion; Lio channels the Heart Seal directly while Kellan channels his oath-light, and together they obliterate it, removing the third-layer predator before it can shape the early world the way the Choir once shaped Timeline A's Campaign V. The six travellers take formal oaths as advisors to the new order — custodians, in effect, of a world that no longer exists anywhere but in their own memories.

World State After Campaign B1

Geography. No continental changes — but the Highever Highlands are formally classified as a rupture-risk Zone of Watching following the Mind-spawn incident. Silver Bastion itself shifts in institutional character from paladin fortress to proto-anchor citadel.

Magic. The Heart Seal partially awakens for the first time in Timeline B, manifesting new sigils never seen in Timeline A. The Anchor-Thread Bond emerges as a wholly new phenomenon, with Lio and Kellan as its first native dyad; the resulting Anchor Web links all eight of them — Lio, Kellan, Arden, Rian, Zeth, Talren, Solan, and Marrek — to the Seal as stabilisers. Resonance itself (dyadic structures, thread-share, controlled pulse, resonance fold) enters the First Age imported wholesale from Timeline A, centuries ahead of when this world would have developed it independently. Mind-Layer Interference is confirmed as a wholly new threat category: violet rupture behaviour, pattern-harvesting, anchor attraction, and — uniquely — the ability to erase individuals outright, as it did to Taren and Zethyr. It cannot be bound, only destroyed, which is exactly what Lio and Kellan do to it.

The Durrel Line. Ends before it begins. Taren and Zethyr Durrel are erased in the opening rupture, meaning the entire Timeline A history of Dominion, Choir, Chain, and Ley-Heirs traced across Campaigns I–XXV has no equivalent starting point here. Any future Durrel figures in Timeline B (Taren Durrel of Campaign B9, Jack Durrel of B12, Caelen Durrel of B13) descend from a different, later branch of the family, not from Zethyr and Taren directly. No Dominion ever forms in this timeline — the founding myth itself is broken.

Institutions. The Order of Anchors is formally founded at Silver Bastion by Seraphine Vale, with a clear mandate: stabilise ruptures, study the Mind, record the resonance knowledge the travellers brought with them, train future dyads responsibly, and — explicitly — prevent Dominion-like structures from ever forming again. Its founding composition is Lio and Kellan as the primary dyad, the six travellers serving as advisers and mentors, and the Silver Dawn providing oversight. The travellers themselves are granted a quiet legal status as "off-era scholars," letting them operate inside Highever's institutions without having to explain where — or when — they actually came from.

Canon status: confirmed. Name variants in this record: Lio Serrin → Vareth (from B5 onward); Kellan Vance → Caen (later chapters).
