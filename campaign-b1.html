Timeline B · Campaign B1 / XXVI

The Anchor at Silver Bastion

Age of Founding, Reforged

0 ARV Primary location: Silver Bastion, Highever

Core Premise

A rupture of violet light drags six travellers from the end of Timeline A — Arden Vale, Rian Thale, Zeth Caden, Talren Vale, Solan, and Marrek — into Silver Bastion at the exact moment the Heart Seal would have awakened for Taren and Zethyr Durrel. Their arrival triggers a lethal predation: Taren and Zethyr are unmade before their story can begin. The world diverges immediately — this is the moment Timeline B becomes its own history, and the ARV count restarts at 0.

Act-by-Act

Act I — The Unmaking and the First Bond

A catastrophic violet rupture tears through the Heart Seal chamber. Taren and Zethyr Durrel are erased by a third-layer force — not killed, not corrupted, but unmade, preventing all future cycles known from Timeline A. Six travellers are thrown into the chamber. Lio leaps to protect Kellan, taking the brunt of the rupture's backlash; this act of devotion imprints him as Anchor. Kellan stabilises him, and the Heart Seal chooses the two as its new dyadic core — the first Anchor and Thread pairing in Timeline B's history.

Act II — The Highland Hunt & The First Mind-Spawn

Tracks in the frost lead the party into the highlands, where a Mind-spawn attempts to seize Solan's unstable resonance. Lio and Kellan fight as one — Anchor and Thread working instinctively in tandem, even without formal training. Resonance techniques from the travellers — folds, drops, dyadic stabilisation — are introduced into the timeline centuries earlier than they were ever discovered in Timeline A. The Mind-spawn is destroyed; the entity behind it retreats.

Act III — Founding of the Order of Anchors & Destruction of the Mind

Seraphine convenes the Council to hear the travellers' testimony. The Order of Anchors is sanctioned as Silver Bastion's new institutional structure — replacing the Heartwardens that never got the chance to form. A final incursion of the Mind erupts beneath the Bastion; Lio and Kellan together obliterate it, removing the third-layer predator before it can shape the early world the way the Choir once did in Timeline A's Campaign V.

World State After Campaign B1

Geography. Silver Bastion, Highever remains the founding seat of power, but its purpose changes fundamentally: rather than the Heartwardens' paladin order, it becomes the Order of Anchors' headquarters from the very first day of this timeline.

Magic. Resonance — folds, drops, dyadic stabilisation — enters Timeline B at 0 ARV instead of being independently discovered thousands of years later (as it was in Timeline A's Campaign XXIV). This is the single biggest divergence point for how magic develops across the two timelines: Timeline B starts with knowledge Timeline A took nine millennia to rediscover.

The Durrel Line. Ends before it begins. Taren and Zethyr Durrel are erased in the opening rupture — meaning the entire Timeline A history of Dominion, Choir, Chain, and Ley-Heirs traced across Campaigns I–XXV has no equivalent starting point here. Any future Durrel figures in Timeline B (Taren Durrel of Campaign B9, Jack Durrel of B12, Caelen Durrel of B13) descend from a different, later branch of the family, not from Zethyr and Taren directly.

Institutions. The Order of Anchors is formally founded at Silver Bastion. Highever becomes the continental centre for rupture management. Lio and Kellan become the first Anchor/Thread pair — the founding template every later Timeline B dyad (Caen/Lio themselves through B5, and eventually Zeth/Talren-style pairings) will be measured against.

Canon status: confirmed. Name variants in this record: Lio Serrin → Vareth (from B5 onward); Kellan Vance → Caen (later chapters).
