Core Premise

Silver Bastion begins this campaign as a stable diplomatic power, mid-preparation for early Seal rites — Taren and Zethyr Durrel are still alive as paladin candidates when it opens. A rupture of violet light tears through the Heart Seal chamber during early-morning rites and drags six travellers from a sealed future into that same chamber. The campaign generation understands the Heart as entering a new awakening/recognition phase around Taren and Zethyr; later B17 evidence establishes that the Great Seal itself is ancient and had been permanently set centuries earlier. Their arrival triggers a lethal predation — a third-layer force later named simply "the Mind" unmakes Taren and Zethyr before their story can begin, not killing them so much as erasing them, preventing every future cycle known from Timeline A. The world diverges immediately. The Heart Seal's surviving recognition architecture then answers to Lio Serrin and Kellan Vance instead.

Act-by-Act

Act I — The Unmaking and the First Bond

The rupture tears through the Heart Seal chamber during early-morning rites, and Taren and Zethyr Durrel are erased by the third-layer force behind it — not killed, not corrupted, unmade, in a way that prevents every future cycle Timeline A ever recorded. Six travellers are thrown into the same chamber, displaced from the far end of Timeline A's history. Lio leaps to protect Kellan from the rupture's backlash, taking the brunt of it himself; that single act of devotion is what imprints him as Anchor. Kellan stabilises him in turn, and the Heart's recognition architecture answers to the two of them as a new dyadic core. Their combined resonance flares outward and forms what will come to be called the Anchor Web, binding all six travellers to the event as stabilisers in the same instant. See the Magic Codex for the later distinction between Great Seal history and Timeline B's Anchor/Thread system.

Act II — The Highland Hunt & the First Mind-Spawn

Frost tracks outside Silver Bastion lead Lio, Kellan, and the six travellers into the Highever Highlands, where a creature of violet distortion — a Mind-spawn — attempts to seize Solan's still-unstable resonance. Lio and Kellan fight it as one, Anchor and Thread working in instinctive tandem for the first time. In the process, resonance techniques the travellers carry from their own era — pulse, fold, drop — enter Timeline B centuries before this world would otherwise have discovered them on its own. The Mind-spawn itself is destroyed; the entity behind it, wounded, retreats deeper into what the travellers call the third layer. Kellan and Lio's bond crystallises into a permanent dyad-thread in the fighting — the first of its kind this era has ever seen.

Act III — Founding of the Order of Anchors & Destruction of the Mind

Seraphine convenes the Highever Council to hear the travellers' testimony in full — the sealed future, the lost cycle, the danger inherent in Dominion-style logic, and the plain truth of what happened to Taren and Zethyr. The Council recognises the rupture unanimously as a world-scale threat and sanctions a standing order to answer it: the Order of Anchors, charged with containing ruptures, safeguarding the displaced travellers, and studying the unfamiliar resonance system now active around Silver Bastion. In Lio and Kellan's presence, the Heart chamber responds further, revealing sigils this generation had not previously recorded. A final incursion of the Mind erupts beneath the Bastion; Lio channels the Heart while Kellan channels his oath-light, and together they destroy the predator. The six travellers take formal oaths as advisers to the new order — custodians, in effect, of a world that no longer exists anywhere but in their own memories.

World State After Campaign B1

Geography. No continental changes — but the Highever Highlands are formally classified as a rupture-risk Zone of Watching following the Mind-spawn incident. Silver Bastion itself shifts in institutional character toward the new Anchor programme.

Magic. The Heart Seal reactivates / answers in a new way for this generation; later B17 evidence proves this is not the first creation or permanent setting of Heart. The Anchor-Thread Bond emerges as a separate Timeline B resonance phenomenon, with Lio and Kellan as its first native dyad; the resulting Anchor Web links Lio, Kellan, Arden, Rian, Zeth, Talren, Solan, and Marrek as stabilisers. Mind-Layer Interference is confirmed as a distinct threat category.

The Durrel Line. Taren and Zethyr are erased in the opening rupture, meaning the Timeline A history of Dominion, Choir, Chain, and Ley-Heirs has no equivalent starting point here. Later Timeline B Durrels descend from other surviving branches, not from this pair.

Institutions. The Order of Anchors is formally founded at Silver Bastion by Seraphine Vale, with Lio and Kellan as the primary dyad, the six travellers serving as advisers and mentors, and the Silver Dawn providing oversight. Its founding purpose belongs to rupture response and resonance safety; it does not replace the much older Great Seal Warden histories that B16–B17 later recover.

Canon status: confirmed. Name variants: Lio Serrin → Vareth (from B5 onward); Kellan Vance → Caen (later chapters). Later understanding: B17 dates the coordinated permanent setting of all eight Great Seals to c. -914 to -913 ARV. B1's language of the Heart "awakening" therefore describes a campaign-era reactivation/recognition event and the birth of the Anchor/Thread system, not the historical creation of the Heart Great Seal.
