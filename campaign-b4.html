Timeline B · Campaign B4 / XXIX

The Veil That Never Was

Age of Founding, Reforged

2 ARV Primary location: Silver Bastion, Anchor Quarters

Core Premise

In the quiet weeks following the Frostborn revelations of B3, a new anomaly takes shape inside Silver Bastion — not in the Heart Seal, not in Hollowrest, but in the private quarters of Anchor and Thread. A Veil-class echo, unknown in Timeline B until now, manifests inside Lio and Caen's room. This is the first appearance in Timeline B of the magic Taren founded the Dominion on, back in Timeline A's Campaign II — except here, there was never a Dominion for it to serve.

Act-by-Act

The Quiet Stirring

Lio and Caen wake to subtle resonance disturbances over several nights until a full echo-shadow forms in their room. The echo studies them rather than attacking — it seems to be attempting to "complete" an imagined triadic structure, behaving like a pre- Dominion entity drawn to resonance itself rather than to any particular ideology or command structure.

Corruption of the Stabiliser-Thread

The echo misreads Caen's stabiliser role as an invitation to bind, partially overlaying his connection to Lio with a corrupting influence. Lio descends into a deep, focused resonance state — a controlled emotional plunge — to reach beneath the corruption and understand what the echo actually wants.

The First Inversion Purge

Lio overloads the echo with chosen resonance and deliberate emotional alignment; Caen uses the stabiliser-thread to anchor the backlash. The echo collapses and dissolves into inert resonance dust — the first dyad-led echo purge in Timeline B's history, and a technique the Order will refine and formalise afterward.

World State After Campaign B4

Geography. Silver Bastion's architects classify Lio and Caen's quarters as Resonance Zone 14 — safe but intensely imprinted — and install new warding protocols for all Anchor quarters going forward.

Magic. Veil Echoes are officially recognised as an independent metaphysical phenomenon in Timeline B — not Dominion-related, and not paralleling Timeline A's Veil directly, but predating or paralleling it as something seeking triadic resonance rather than control. This is a genuinely new magical category for Timeline B, distinct from Heartborn, Stabiliser Thread, and Frostborn lineages established in B1–B3.

The Durrel Line. Still absent from the narrative directly — four campaigns into Timeline B, the world is developing entirely new magical categories independent of the erased Durrel bond.

Institutions. The Deep-Drop Inversion Purge is recorded as a new rite — the Order of Anchors now has a documented technique for handling Veil-class echoes specifically, expanding its institutional toolkit beyond the emergency responses of B1 and B2.

Canon status: follows the character register directly. New rite recorded: the Deep-Drop Inversion Purge.
