Timeline B · Campaign B3 / XXVIII

The Frostborn Inheritance

Age of Founding, Reforged

1–2 ARV Primary location: Silver Bastion, Vyrn's Spring

Core Premise

Weeks after the Hollowrest expedition, unnatural frost begins forming inside Caen and Lio's private quarters — clean geometric sigils unlike the Hollowrest anomalies. The frost responds not to Lio, but to Caen, revealing a truth erased in Timeline A: Caen is of the Frostborn.

Party & Key Figures

  • Lio Serrin
    First Anchor of Timeline B.
  • Caen (Kellan) Vance
    First Stabiliser Thread; revealed as a surviving Frostborn descendant, a sub-line of Icebinder magic predating the Seals.
  • The Six Travellers
    Arden, Rian, Zeth, Talren, Solan, Marrek — act as stabilisers and witnesses.
  • Seraphine Vale
    High Paladin of Silver Bastion; guides the political and historical integration of the lost Binder line.

Act-by-Act

The Frost-Sigil in Silver Bastion

Frost appears overnight in Caen and Lio's quarters, forming deliberate patterns. Resonance tests confirm Caen is the source. A dormant Binder door opens beneath their bedchamber, revealing the Frostborn Vault.

The Road North & Vyrn's Spring

Frostborn sigils lead the party into the northern highlands to Vyrn's Spring, a hidden enclave of surviving Frostborn descendants. The settlement recognises Caen's lineage instantly, offering history and ancestral memory long lost to the Mind's predations.

Recognition & Return

Caen formally claims his Frostborn heritage with Lio as his stabilising partner. The Spring entrusts him with ancestral sigils and a watcher-stone, forming the first stable Bastion-Frostborn diplomatic link in history.

Final World State

Silver Bastion gains the Frostborn Vault and becomes the first tri-lineage centre in Timeline B — housing Heartborn (Lio), Stabiliser Thread (Caen), and now Frostborn heritage. Seraphine updates the Order's doctrine: Binder lines may awaken through living inheritance, not relics alone.

Canon status: follows the character register directly; this campaign is where the Kellan → Caen renaming becomes explicit in the source material.
