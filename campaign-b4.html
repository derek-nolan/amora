Timeline B · Campaign B4 / XXIX

The Veil That Never Was

Age of Founding, Reforged

2 ARV (a few weeks after B3) Primary location: Silver Bastion, Anchor Quarters

Core Premise

In the quiet weeks following the Frostborn revelations of B3, a new anomaly takes shape inside Silver Bastion — not in the Heart Seal, not in Hollowrest, but in the private quarters of Anchor and Thread. A Veil-class echo, unknown in Timeline B until now, manifests inside Lio and Caen's room. It isn't hostile in the traditional sense — it seeks completion, attempting to bind itself into a triad formation with the dyad, misreading their emotional synchrony as an incomplete circuit. This is the first appearance in Timeline B of the magic Taren founded the Dominion on back in Timeline A's Campaign II, except here there was never a Dominion for it to serve.

Party & Key Figures

  • Lio Serrin PC First Anchor of the Reforged Cycle; performs the first inversion purge in Timeline B, deepening his emotional authority and resonance sensitivity.
  • Caen Vance
    First Stabiliser Thread, Frostborn lineage. Absorbs the echo to shield Lio, survives the resulting corruption-layer intact, and comes out of it officially recorded as "dual-heritage stabiliser plus echo-resistant."
  • The Six Travellers
    Arrive only after the purge concludes; assist in stabilising the remaining resonance and confirm the echo's signature differs meaningfully from anything Veil-related in their own timeline.
  • Seraphine Vale
    High Paladin; oversees the post-incident review and blocks a Council attempt to classify the dyad as "research assets," protecting Lio and Caen's operational autonomy.

Act-by-Act

Act I — The Quiet Stirring

Lio and Caen wake to subtle resonance disturbances over several nights — faint pulses, emotional static, geometric shifts around the bed and floor — and initially write it off as leftover Frostborn resonance or simple dyad intensity, until a full echo-shadow forms in the centre of the room. The echo studies them rather than attacking, attempting to "complete" some imagined structure. Early pulses reveal it isn't Veil in the Dominion sense at all — this is a pre-Dominion entity drawn to resonance itself, not to any particular ideology or command structure.

Act II — Corruption of the Stabiliser-Thread

The echo misreads Caen's innate stabiliser role as an invitation to bind, partially overlaying his stabiliser-thread with a corruption-layer — not possession, but a thickening and distortion of his resonance that leaves him conscious but constrained. Lio descends into a deep-drop inversion, a controlled emotional plunge, to reach beneath the corruption and understand what the echo actually wants. For the first time, the dyad performs a full inversion purge: Lio overloads the echo with chosen resonance and deliberate emotional alignment, while Caen uses the stabiliser-thread itself to anchor the backlash. The echo collapses and dissolves into inert resonance dust.

Act III — Aftermath, Wards, and Institutional Acceptance

The Six arrive post-purge to help stabilise what's left, and Seraphine personally conducts the review, verifying there's no lingering corruption anywhere in Caen's stabiliser-thread. Testing confirms the dyad's handling of the incident was clean, mutual, and effective. Bastion architects and resonance scholars classify the room as Resonance Zone 14 — safe but intensely imprinted — and Rian and Solan install new warding protocols across the Anchor Quarters, formally acknowledging that dyad resonance itself can attract unwanted metaphysical attention. The campaign closes with the dyad reaffirmed, the institution adjusted around them, and Veil Echoes officially recognised as a genuine Timeline B phenomenon.

World State After Campaign B4

Geography. No territorial changes, but Silver Bastion gains a newly protected site: Resonance Zone 14, the Anchor Quarters themselves, now warded and actively monitored for further echo activity. Menge is explicitly flagged as unrelated to the incident — the echo is confirmed not linked to Frostborn or Hollowrest frost-reflection patterns at all.

Magic. Veil Echoes are formally classified as an independent metaphysical phenomenon in Timeline B — not Dominion-related, predating or paralleling it, and seeking triadic resonance structures rather than control or corruption. Two new mechanics come directly out of this campaign: the Deep-Drop Inversion Purge (an extremely effective but extremely risky rite — full anchor vulnerability and heavy thread strain on the stabiliser, but capable of fully destroying a Veil Echo) and Corruption-Layer Mapping, the finding that stabiliser-threads can hold non-lethal corruption and recover completely so long as dyad trust stays intact through the process.

The Durrel Line. Still absent from the narrative directly — five campaigns into Timeline B, the world keeps developing entirely new magical categories independent of the erased Durrel bond.

Institutions. The Order of Anchors expands its mandate again, establishing formal Echo Response Protocols, Dyad Resonance Monitoring, Ward Layering Standards for anchor living quarters, and emotional synchrony advisories (framed as guidance, not restriction). B4 confirms something structurally important about the Order itself: its role is stabilisation, not rule, with emotional resonance treated as a genuine central pillar rather than an afterthought.

Canon status: follows the source account directly. New rite recorded: the Deep-Drop Inversion Purge.
