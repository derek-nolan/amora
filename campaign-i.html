Timeline A · Campaign I

The Heartwardens

Age of Founding

0–27 ARV Primary location: Silver Bastion, Highever Duration: seven months

Opening World State

The very beginning of recorded history. Highever's Silver Bastion holds the faith of Luminara and the first order of paladins, the Heartwardens. Evermere is a coastal sanctuary; Brevair a crown ally; Menge a frozen frontier of zeal and Binder conflict. The world is small, pious, and still mostly whole — shaped already by the Mage Calamity, the Mage Wars, and the Oath Wars, which belong to the pre-game past and account for the suspicion surrounding arcane power that the new order is built to answer. At the campaign's opening, only the Heart Seal has been directly encountered; the wider system of Seals hasn't yet been mapped or stabilised. Everything that later ages will formalise starts here as instinct.

Party & Key Figures

  • Zethyr Durrel PC
    Lightbound paladin; Heartwarden founder. Bonded to Taren. Founding creed: "Through Heart, Balance."
  • Taren Durrel
    Veil champion; co-founder of the cycle. Lover of Zethyr.
  • Seraphine Vale
    Grand Marshal of the Paladin Council and senior Silver Bastion authority; supervised the first Seal work. Later executed during the rise of the Dominion.
  • Arienna Voss (also recorded as Arienna Lume)
    Magister of the Collegium Arcanum; Flamebinder. Linked the Bastion to the mage college and joined the founding ranks; later pursued research that brought her into conflict with Taren.
  • Aldric Fen
    Heartwarden chaplain and Bastion staff; gave the new order its religious and pastoral voice. Later executed by Taren.
  • Lyra & Thane
    Silver Bastion initiates, still learning what the institution would become. Later rose through the Veil order and became Generals of the Veil.
  • Orell
    Antagonist whose plot closed the Age of Light. Exact office, motive, and network unresolved in the surviving record.
  • Kellan
    Named member of the first-cycle cast. Later killed by Zethyr; whether he was Zethyr's separately-remembered best friend remains unresolved — the record deliberately doesn't merge the two identities without evidence.

Act-by-Act

Months One & Two — Silver Bastion

Surviving records alternate between describing Zethyr and Taren as trainees and as newly appointed Heartwarden captains; both versions place their shared beginning at Silver Bastion, beneath the temple of Luminara, where the unstable Heart Seal was discovered. Their first successful contact created the psychic bond that would define both campaigns — an immediate channel of feeling, judgement, and will between them. Silver Bastion wasn't occupied by the pair alone: Seraphine Vale served as Grand Marshal, Aldric Fen gave the order its chaplaincy, and Lyra and Thane were initiates still learning what the institution would become. Kellan belonged to the named early cast, though the surviving indexed extracts don't preserve his precise office or his full relationship to Zethyr. The College and the Bastion were linked through Arienna Voss, who supervised or assisted the earliest Seal stabilisations — not a decorative contact, but someone whose scholarly path would later entangle with Taren's study of the Veil.

Month Three — The First Balancing Ritual

The first balancing ritual succeeds. The Durrel bond manifests visibly and the Heart Medallion reawakens — and beneath that victory lies the first trace of the Dark Filament, absorbed into the medallion before anyone understands what it will become. The Vision of Balance reveals that Light and Shadow are interdependent, a truth that can support restraint or justify terrible ambition depending on who interprets it. This is the first appearance of what later ages will formalise as the Veil.

Month Four — The Other Seals

The party stabilises the Aether, Flame, and Stone Seals and begins the wider work later summarised as negotiating with Brevair, pacifying the frost crisis in Menge, and protecting Evermere's archives. Arienna's Flamebinder heritage matters directly to this expansion; the Icebinders of Menge are still treated as a dangerous lost line, and the Frost Seal crisis becomes part of the Heartwardens' rise to continental trust. By the end of this phase, Zethyr and Taren have earned recognition as co-leaders of the Heartwardens. The order's banner is crimson, white, and gold; its remembered oath: "In heart, there is light; in light, there is restraint."

Month Five — Evermere

Evermere provides the campaign's human pause. Zethyr and Taren marry there and strengthen the bond in a place later ages will remember as their sanctuary. The marriage isn't hidden or shameful — the danger isn't the existence of their love, but the way the magical link begins turning private devotion into usable power. Later records say the Chains of the Veil began forming unconsciously during this period: the first hint of what Campaign II will make explicit.

Months Six & Seven — Orell's Plot

The Age of Light ends through Orell's plot. The current indexed project record preserves the outcome more clearly than the scheme itself: Zethyr and Taren uncovered and ended it, and doing so required their first taking of life. That death shatters the moral certainty with which they had begun. Campaign I therefore closes not at the height of triumph, but with faith fractured and the founding pair newly aware that their bond could make violence feel necessary and shared. Orell's exact office, motive, and network are not present in the surviving indexed extracts and have not been invented here. The same is true of the exact name of the character remembered in the project as Zethyr's best friend — the gap is recorded explicitly so the character isn't silently replaced by Taren or Kellan.

World State After Campaign I

Geography. Silver Bastion stands as the Heartwardens' fortress-temple in Highever. Evermere is confirmed as a coastal sanctuary and the site of Zethyr and Taren's marriage. Brevair is a crown ally; Menge remains a frozen frontier, its Frost Seal crisis just beginning to draw continental attention.

Magic. The Heart Seal is discovered and partially stabilised. The Aether, Flame, and Stone Seals are also stabilised this campaign. The Heart Medallion reawakens and, unknown to anyone at the time, absorbs the first trace of the Dark Filament — the seed of what becomes the Veil in Campaign II.

The Durrel Line. This is the founding bond: Zethyr and Taren Durrel, psychically linked through the Heart Seal's first balancing ritual. Every later appearance of "the Durrel pattern" — commanding figure paired with devoted anchor — traces back to this campaign. The bond is not yet a Chain; that transformation begins in Campaign II.

Institutions. The Heartwardens are founded as both paladins and proto-bureaucracy — love as chain, obedience as miracle, though neither is intentional yet. This era ends with the foundations laid for a more centralised regime: the Dominion is not yet born, but its seed is planted.

Canon status: expanded from chat-mined chronicle reconstruction. Orell's exact office and motive, and the identity of Zethyr's separately-remembered best friend, are not present in the surviving record and have not been invented here.
