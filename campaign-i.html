Timeline A · Campaign I

The Heartwardens

Age of Founding

0–27 ARV Primary location: Silver Bastion, Highever Duration: seven months

Opening World State

The very beginning of recorded history, though not the true beginning of the Heartwardens themselves. The permanent Heart watch was established generations earlier around Tarenus Durrel, but the later order had lapsed by the time this campaign opens, its Seal duties untended and its purpose half-forgotten. Highever's Silver Bastion still holds the faith of Luminara, and it is here that Zethyr and Taren revive the Heartwardens rather than found them from nothing. Evermere is a coastal sanctuary; Brevair a crown ally; Menge a frozen frontier of zeal and Binder conflict. The world is small, pious, and already shaped by older wars whose real chronology is only recovered much later. At the campaign's opening, the Heart Seal is the party's direct frame of reference; the wider Great Seal system has not yet been remapped or understood by this generation. Everything later ages formalise begins here as rediscovery and instinct.

Party & Key Figures

  • Zethyr (later Durrel) PC
    Lightbound paladin; revives the lapsed Heartwardens as High Commander. Takes the Durrel name at his Month Five marriage to Taren. Founding creed of the revived order: "Through Heart, Balance."
  • Taren Durrel
    Veil champion; Captain of the revived Heartwardens, and descendant of the older Durrel Heart tradition. Lover of Zethyr.
  • Seraphine Vale
    Grand Marshal of the Paladin Council and senior Silver Bastion authority; supervised the first campaign-era Seal work. Later executed during the rise of the Dominion.
  • Arienna Lume (also recorded as Arienna Voss)
    Magister of the Collegium Arcanum; Flamewarden. Linked the Bastion to the mage college and joined the revived order's ranks; later pursued research that brought her into conflict with Taren.
  • Aldric Fen
    Heartwarden chaplain and Bastion staff; gave the revived order its religious and pastoral voice. Later executed by Taren.
  • Lyra & Thane
    Silver Bastion initiates, still learning what the institution would become. Later rose through the Veil order and became Generals of the Veil.
  • Orell
    Antagonist whose plot closed the Age of Light. Exact office, motive, and network unresolved in the surviving record.
  • Kellan
    Named member of the first-cycle cast. Later killed by Zethyr; whether he was Zethyr's separately remembered best friend remains unresolved — the record deliberately does not merge the two identities without evidence.

Act-by-Act

Months One & Two — Silver Bastion

Surviving records alternate between describing Zethyr and Taren as trainees and as newly appointed Heartwarden captains; both versions place their shared beginning at Silver Bastion, beneath the temple of Luminara, where the unstable Heart Seal was encountered. Their first successful contact created the psychic bond that would define both campaigns — an immediate channel of feeling, judgement, and will between them. Silver Bastion was not occupied by the pair alone: Seraphine Vale served as Grand Marshal, Aldric Fen gave the order its chaplaincy, and Lyra and Thane were initiates. Kellan belonged to the named early cast, though the surviving indexed extracts do not preserve his precise office or his full relationship to Zethyr. The College and the Bastion were linked through Arienna Voss, whose scholarly path would later entangle with Taren's study of the Veil.

Month Three — The First Balancing Ritual

The first balancing ritual of this campaign succeeds. The Durrel bond manifests visibly and the Heart Medallion reawakens — and beneath that victory lies the first trace of the Dark Filament, absorbed into the medallion before anyone understands what it will become. The Vision of Balance reveals that Light and Shadow are interdependent, a truth that can support restraint or justify terrible ambition depending on who interprets it. This is the first appearance of what later ages formalise as the Veil.

Month Four — The Other Seals

The party stabilises several pressures known in the campaign record as Aether, Flame, and Stone and begins the wider work later summarised as negotiating with Brevair, pacifying the frost crisis in Menge, and protecting Evermere's archives. Arienna's Flamewarden heritage matters directly to this expansion. The party encounters the northern Frost tradition in crisis — a branch of the legitimate Icewarden lineage has fallen into the faction remembered as the Icebinders, spreading destructive frost through Menge. The campaign-era records use a mixture of names for the wider Seal system; later B16/B17 research establishes the current eight-Seal registry and should not be retroactively confused with every term this generation used.

Month Five — Evermere

Evermere provides the campaign's human pause. Zethyr and Taren marry there and strengthen the bond in a place later ages remember as their sanctuary. The marriage is not hidden or shameful — the danger is not the existence of their love, but the way the magical link begins turning private devotion into usable power. Later records say the Chains of the Veil began forming unconsciously during this period: the first hint of what Campaign II makes explicit.

Months Six & Seven — Orell's Plot

The Age of Light ends through Orell's plot. The current indexed project record preserves the outcome more clearly than the scheme itself: Zethyr and Taren uncovered and ended it, and doing so required their first taking of life. That death shatters the moral certainty with which they had begun. Campaign I therefore closes not at the height of triumph, but with faith fractured and the founding pair newly aware that their bond could make violence feel necessary and shared. Orell's exact office, motive, and network are not present in the surviving indexed extracts and have not been invented here. The same is true of the exact name of the character remembered in the project as Zethyr's best friend.

World State After Campaign I

Geography. Silver Bastion stands as the Heartwardens' fortress-temple in Highever. Evermere is confirmed as a coastal sanctuary and the site of Zethyr and Taren's marriage. Brevair is a crown ally; Menge remains a frozen frontier.

Magic. The campaign generation restores practical access to Heart and stabilises several other pressures under the terminology available to it. The Heart Medallion reawakens and, unknown to anyone at the time, absorbs the first trace of the Dark Filament — the seed of what becomes the Veil in Campaign II.

The Durrel Line. This is the founding bond of recorded Timeline A: Zethyr and Taren Durrel, psychically linked through the Heart work at Silver Bastion. Every later appearance of the Durrel pattern traces back to this relationship in the surviving historical record, even though B17 later proves that Heartbinding and the Great Seals themselves are much older than the pair.

Institutions. The Heartwardens are revived as a paladin and custodial order rather than created from nothing. The Dominion is not yet born, but the psychic bond and Dark Filament establish the conditions from which it emerges in Campaign II.

Canon status: expanded from chat-mined chronicle reconstruction. Orell's exact office and motive, and the identity of Zethyr's separately remembered best friend, are unresolved and have not been invented. Later understanding: Campaigns B16–B17 recover a current registry of Heart, Flame, Stone, Storm, Life, Shadow, Light, and Frost and establish that all eight Great Seals were permanently set centuries before Campaign I. Campaign I's Aether/Air and other legacy names therefore remain valid historical terminology for what this generation believed or recorded, not proof of additional Great Seals outside the later eight.
