Timeline A · Campaign XI

Clause XI / The Null Relic

Age of Clause

c. 6550 ARV Primary location: Veylaris Rest, Bastion Saren

Opening World State

One century after the Choir's extinction. Republics of trade and law, centred on Veylaris Rest — Evermere's latest incarnation as a citadel of contracts — and the emergent Lyricum, which codifies magic as jurisprudence.

Party & Key Figures

  • Riven Mareth
    Rogue and relic broker ("Whisperknife") of Veylaris Rest; ancestry reaches back to the Concordist wardens of Taren Durrel's era. Partner to Cassian Vale.
  • Cassian Vale
    Ex-watchman turned mercenary; moral anchor of the company. Partner to Riven Mareth.
  • Ilyen Vael
    Hidden Ledger agent; co-authors Clause XI with Mareth.
  • Leora Vance
    Lyricum archivist and scholar; ally to Riven and Cassian.
  • Keth Damaris
    Provost of Evermere, drawn into the null-relic incident.
  • Marcell Aune
    Broker and middleman, working with Keth, dealing with Riven.

Act-by-Act

The Mareth Line Re-emerges

Through contraband archives recovered along the Lantern Chain, Mareth uncovers that the ancient Ley-Heir lattice — a metaphysical inheritance once engineered by the Dominion — had merely lain dormant, not destroyed.

Bastion Saren

Guided by Lyricum defectors and Ledger operatives, Mareth's company traces the pulse to the frozen fortress Bastion Saren, a pre-Dominion relay built to distribute the Heart Seal's law. Within its core they find the incomplete clause that once governed succession between mortal and echo.

The Breathing Law

Mareth and Ilyen Vael complete the code, re-align the Bastion's engines, and re-ignite the Ley-Heirs. The result is articulated as Clause XI — "Memory shall breathe, but not command" — replacing static Choir recursion with adaptive consciousness.

Final World State

The dormant Ley-Heir network is successfully re-established through mortal channels; Clause XI becomes the foundation of post-Choir legal resonance, and the Republic Inquest is chartered to monitor harmonic inheritance. The Null Relic is ultimately unmade — the world chooses to live with its mistakes instead of erasing them — and the reactivated lattice restores the metaphysical conditions under which the Durrel echoes may again appear.

Canon status: follows the character register directly; several figures are doc-seeded rather than confirmed on-screen.
