Clause XI / The Null Relic

Age of Clause — First Cyberpunk Era

c. 6550 ARV Primary location: Veylaris Rest, Bastion Saren

Opening World State

One century after the Choir's extinction — though it's now been millennia since the Choir itself fell in Campaign V; this is the local era's own reckoning of time since the Severing of Campaign X. Republics of trade and law centre on Veylaris Rest, Evermere's latest incarnation as a citadel of contracts, and the emergent Lyricum, which codifies magic as jurisprudence.

Act-by-Act

The Mareth Line Re-emerges

In the century following the fall of the Choir, the governance of resonance passed from faith to republic. The Lyricum of Evermere codified magic as jurisprudence, while the Hidden Ledger safeguarded the erased doctrines of the Seals. Through contraband archives recovered along the Lantern Chain, Riven Mareth uncovers that the ancient Ley-Heir lattice — a metaphysical inheritance once engineered by the Dominion to preserve its will — had merely lain dormant, not destroyed. Fragments of its pulse, later termed the Verdict Current, still flicker beneath the northern ice.

Bastion Saren

Guided by Lyricum defectors and Ledger operatives, Mareth's company traces the pulse to the frozen fortress Bastion Saren, a pre-Dominion relay constructed to distribute the Heart Seal's law across living vessels. Within its core they discover the incomplete clause that once governed succession between mortal and echo.

The Breathing Law

In completing that code, Mareth and Ilyen Vael re-align the Bastion's engines and re-ignite the Ley-Heirs — a network binding sentient will to the world's harmonic field. This restoration culminates in the articulation of Clause XI, later known as The Breathing Law: "Memory shall breathe, but not command." Clause XI replaces static Choir recursion with adaptive consciousness, allowing the world's memory to circulate through mortal agency rather than divine machinery. Every participant in the Bastion Saren ritual becomes an anchor-node, carrying a fragment of the reawakened pattern.

World State After Campaign XI

Geography. Bastion Saren is sealed as a stabilized relay for the new lattice. Veylaris Rest is confirmed as a free republic of merchants, smugglers, and relic brokers, rebuilt on Choir foundations.

Magic. This is the pivotal campaign for the Ley-Heirs: the dormant network first created in Campaign II and scattered at Zethyr's Severance is successfully re-established through mortal channels for the first time in millennia. Scholars of Evermere note that this reactivates the same metaphysical architecture that once housed the souls of the Ley-Heirs — the structure through which the echoes of Zethyr and Taren Durrel had historically re-manifested. Though no manifestation is recorded at the time, the lattice's renewal makes recurrence theoretically possible again: the cycle of Heart and Veil no longer sealed, but breathing.

The Durrel Line. No living Durrel appears, but Riven Mareth's ancestry is explicitly traced back to "the Concordist wardens of Taren Durrel's era" — confirming the founding age's institutions left descendant lineages well beyond the Durrel name itself.

Institutions. The Republic Inquest is chartered to monitor harmonic inheritance. Clause XI becomes a jurisprudential principle enshrining doubt and transparency as virtues — the backbone ethic for later civic experiments, including Concord law in Campaign XV. The Null Relic is ultimately unmade; the world chooses to live with its mistakes instead of erasing them.

Canon status: follows the character register directly; several figures are doc-seeded rather than confirmed on-screen.
