Timeline A · Campaign XI

Clause XI / The Null Relic

The Lantern Chain to the Edge of the Republic

Age of Clause — First Cyberpunk Era

c. 6550 ARV Primary location: The Gate → Bastion Saren → Veylaris Rest → Evermere → Menge Expanse

Opening World State

One century after the Choir's extinction. Republics of trade and law centre on Veylaris Rest, Evermere's latest incarnation as a citadel of contracts, and the emergent Lyricum, which codifies magic as jurisprudence. Riven Mareth and Cassian Vale flee a collapsing capital, The Gate, after uncovering relic-law tampering — the opening act of a campaign that will take them the length of the continent and back.

Party & Key Figures

  • Riven Mareth PC Human Rogue ("Whisperknife"); ends the campaign as Clause Zero — the Breath Between Laws
    Relic broker and cipher-thief from Veylaris Rest, unknowingly bound to the world's living law. Pragmatic, sardonic, capable of deep loyalty, haunted by lineage — descended from the Mareth line, Heralds and Witnesses of Taren Durrel's forgotten law.
  • Cassian Vale
    Ex-watchman turned mercenary, Riven's long-time partner and conscience. Protective, stubborn, grounded — lost his badge defending Riven years ago in Veylaris Rest.
  • Leora Vance
    Lyricum scholar who memorised fragments of the old Doctrine; a pragmatic idealist documenting truth without letting politics warp it. Later marked with an echo-scar and becomes custodian of the Clause Zero record.
  • Ilyen Vael
    Hidden Ledger operative and descendant of the ancient Editors. Sharp, secretive, pragmatic — her loyalty leans toward balance, not faction. Co-authors Clause XI and, later, the Breath-Bridge.
  • Verrin Mael
    Hidden Ledger archivist and smuggler operating from Veylaris Rest's Old Choir Quarter; provides access to the Lyricum freight tunnels and acts as broker of information.
  • Keth Damaris
    Provost of Evermere's Lyricum; intended to remove a dangerous relic from politics but unleashed its danger instead. Captured, then allied with once his true intent is understood.
  • Marcell Aune
    Broker of Veylaris Rest, middleman between scholars and smugglers; confirms he holds only the transport shell of the relic, not the relic itself.
  • Maela Vael
    Editor-scholar of Evermere; aids the group's Archive infiltration and co-anchors the Breath-Bridge ritual.
  • Renna Varr
    Captain of the Eel's Promise; smuggler and Ledger ally who provides transport and cover during the Evermere arc.

Act-by-Act

Act I — The Lantern Chain

Riven, Cassian, and Leora flee The Gate's collapse and join a Lyricum caravan heading north along the Lantern Chain, fighting off River Wardens — a fanatical sect worshipping "the Voice beneath the Ice" — and meeting Ilyen Vael along the way. During the journey, Riven's bloodline awakens the Verdict Pulse, the first sign that his ancestry carries more than family history. In Evermere, the group discovers the Hidden Ledger beneath the city; Riven reads a forbidden Mareth text and glimpses the Voice for himself.

Act II — Bastion Saren

Guided by visions of the Voice, Riven and Ilyen lead the group to Bastion Saren, a frozen pre-Dominion relay fortress in the Menge Expanse, while Leora documents what she calls Clause Zero. At the fortress, they awaken its dormant mechanism and forge Clause XI — "Memory shall breathe, but not command" — the Breathing Law that stabilises reality across the re-ignited Ley-Heir network. Riven chooses restraint over power at the moment of completion, a choice that shapes everything that follows.

Act III — The Republic's Shadow

Evermere destabilises in Bastion Saren's wake; Ilyen's Hidden Ledger cell is compromised, and Keth Damaris flees with a null-relic fragment. The group pursues, captures Keth, and learns of his plan to trade the fragment through broker Marcell Aune. They avoid Inquest patrols via smuggler routes, confirm a decoy at Hallow's Bend, and meet Aune directly — securing an alliance once it's clear he holds only the transport shell, not the relic itself.

Act IV — Unmaking the Quiet

Returning to Veylaris Rest under cover of darkness, the group meets Verrin Mael at a Ledger safe house in the Old Choir Quarter. She reveals the only way to stabilise the null-relic: bring it into the Lyricum's central anchor and use the city's resonance engine to contain or destroy it. The infiltration succeeds. Faced with the relic's instability, Riven orders its destruction; Leora and Ilyen channel the Lyricum anchor's power, and the ritual unmakes the null-relic, silencing the world for several heartbeats. Every lamp across the harbour gutters; the Ley-grid fails. Each participant is left with an echo-scar — a momentary silence that precedes great ley shifts. The act saves the world from collapse, but plunges Veylaris Rest into darkness.

Act V — The Republic's Shadow, Concluded

After sealing the Clause Zero vault, the group debriefs with Verrin. Together they confirm a truth erased from modern Republic history: the Mareths were Heralds of Taren Durrel, the ascended Lawgiver whose consort Zethyr summoned the Light and sacrificed himself to end the Dominion and destroy Taren. Clause XI, the Breathing Law, was written only a century after the Veil's fall, ushering in the Republic Age — and Riven learns of the Twelve Herald Houses, and the part his bloodline still plays as Witness of forgotten laws.

Act VI — The Breath and the Blood

The group infiltrates the Vance Archive in Evermere, recovers a Clause Zero Codex fragment, and escapes an Inquest sweep with help from Editor-scholar Maela Vael and smuggler-captain Renna Varr. After resting at Renna's Cove, they decrypt the Codex and uncover the design of the Breath-Bridge — a ritual to restore mercy to the Breathing Law without collapsing it. At Evermere's Northern Sluice, Riven, Leora, and Ilyen perform the ritual; the ley shifts, and compassion returns, subtly, to the world.

Act VII — The Bridge of Mercy

The group reaches the Menge Expanse, where a living Clausal Court — one of the original Twelve — lies beneath a site called Taren's Breath. The node offers Riven a choice: erase, merge, or stand apart. He chooses to merge Clause Zero with the Twelve, binding mercy into the Breathing Law's foundation while remaining human. The network accepts; twin auroras of gold (Law) and blue (Light) ignite as the Dominion cycle restarts and new Ley-Heirs begin to stir.

World State After Campaign XI

Geography. Bastion Saren is sealed as a stabilised relay for the new lattice. Veylaris Rest is confirmed as a free republic of merchants, smugglers, and relic brokers, rebuilt on Choir foundations — though its harbour goes dark for a time after the null-relic's destruction. The Menge Expanse shows its first signs of new life after centuries of ice.

Magic. This is the pivotal campaign for the Ley-Heirs: the dormant network first created in Campaign II and scattered at Zethyr's Severance is successfully re-established through mortal channels. Clause XI becomes a jurisprudential principle enshrining doubt and transparency as virtues. By the campaign's end, the Breathing Law carries a permanent harmonic of mercy, and new Ley-Heirs of Law and Light begin to awaken — the first confirmed Ley-Heir activity since the original cycle's scattering.

The Durrel Line. Riven Mareth's ancestry is confirmed as Herald and Witness lineage tied directly to Taren Durrel — proof the founding age's institutions left descendant lineages well beyond the Durrel name itself. The campaign also restores a suppressed truth: modern Republic history had erased Zethyr's role in ending the Dominion, remembering only Taren as a lone Lawgiver myth.

Institutions. The Republic Inquest is chartered to monitor harmonic inheritance. The Twelve Herald Houses (Mareth, Vael, Vance, Serin, Avenar, Tarell, Rhos, Dorn, Selene, Korin, Kethar, Verris) are confirmed as the founding lineages behind the Republic's institutions — Witnesses, Editors, Recorders, Judges, and more, each descended from a different facet of the old law. The Null Relic is ultimately unmade; the world chooses to live with its mistakes instead of erasing them, and Riven Mareth is remembered as Clause Zero — the Breath Between Laws.

Canon status: substantially expanded from a fuller campaign compendium — this is now the most detailed record on the site of any single Timeline A campaign. The "new Ley-Heirs of Law and Light" awakening at this campaign's close isn't picked up again explicitly in later campaign records; treat it as an open thread rather than a contradiction if it doesn't resurface by name in Campaign XII onward.
