Timeline A · Campaign XI

Clause XI / The Null Relic

The Lantern Chain to the Edge of the Republic

Age of Clause — First Cyberpunk Era

c. 6550 ARV Primary location: The Gate → Evermere → Bastion Saren → Veylaris Rest → Menge Expanse

Opening World State

Roughly a century after Campaign X's Severing in the site's working ARV chronology, the old theocratic and machine orders have given way to republics of trade, law, archive, and relic commerce. Veylaris Rest is a free coastal republic rebuilt on Choir foundations; Evermere houses the Lyricum and one of the Hidden Ledger's cells; the Republic Inquest is already active, policing ley disturbances and forbidden Choir-tech. Riven Mareth and Cassian Vale begin by fleeing a collapsing Gate after uncovering relic-law tampering — the first movement in a campaign about whether memory can survive without becoming command.

Party & Key Figures

  • Riven Mareth PC Human Rogue ("Whisperknife"); ends the campaign as Clause Zero — the Breath Between Laws
    Relic broker and cipher-thief from Veylaris Rest. Pragmatic, sardonic, suspicious of destiny, and fiercely loyal to Cassian. During the campaign he learns the Mareths were remembered as Heralds and Witnesses of Taren Durrel's law.
  • Cassian Vale
    Former Veylaris Rest watchman turned mercenary, Riven's long-time lover, partner, and conscience. Protective, stubborn, grounded; he lost his badge defending Riven years before the campaign.
  • Leora Vance
    Lyricum scholar and archivist who memorised fragments of the old Doctrine. A pragmatic idealist determined to record truth without letting institutions rewrite it; later keeper of the Clause Zero record.
  • Ilyen Vael
    Hidden Ledger operative and descendant of the Editors. Sharp, secretive, and pragmatic; becomes Riven's principal counterpart in the work around Clause XI and the Breath-Bridge.
  • Keth Damaris
    Provost of Evermere's Lyricum. His attempt to remove the null-relic from politics instead exposes its danger. Pursued and captured by Riven's group, he becomes a reluctant source once his intent is understood.
  • Marcell Aune
    Veylaris Rest broker and middleman between scholars and smugglers. He holds the transport shell rather than the null-relic itself and ultimately provides a safer route through the Republic's underworld.
  • Verrin Mael
    Hidden Ledger archivist and smuggler operating from the Old Choir Quarter; gives the party access to the Lyricum freight tunnels and provides the practical route to the anchor chamber.
  • Maela Vael
    Editor-scholar of Evermere who masks the Vance Archive breach, provides a temporary Editor mark, and co-anchors the Breath-Bridge.
  • Renna Varr
    Captain of the Eel's Promise, smuggler and Ledger ally; provides transport, cover, and a safe cove during the Evermere arc.

Act-by-Act

Act I — The Lantern Chain

Riven, Cassian, and Leora flee The Gate and join a Lyricum caravan heading north along the Lantern Chain. River Wardens — a fanatical sect worshipping the "Voice beneath the Ice" — attack along the route, and the group meets Ilyen Vael. Riven's bloodline awakens the Verdict Pulse, the first clear sign that the Mareth inheritance is more than family mythology. In Evermere, the group discovers the Hidden Ledger beneath the city; Riven reads forbidden Mareth material and glimpses the Voice for himself.

Act II — Bastion Saren

Guided by visions of the Voice, Riven and Ilyen lead the party into the Menge Expanse to Bastion Saren, an ancient frozen relay fortress whose exact construction era is not securely preserved in the surviving records. Leora documents the material they come to call Clause Zero. Inside the Bastion, Riven and Ilyen awaken its dormant mechanism and articulate Clause XI — "Memory shall breathe, but not command" — the Breathing Law. The relay and the Ley-Heir lattice answer. Riven refuses the chance to turn that access into personal rule, choosing restraint over possession.

Act III — The Republic's Shadow

Evermere destabilises in Bastion Saren's wake. Ilyen's Hidden Ledger cell is compromised, and Keth Damaris flees with a null-relic fragment. The group pursues, captures Keth, and learns of his intended exchange through Marcell Aune. They avoid Inquest patrols by taking smuggler routes, expose Keth's decoy at Hallow's Bend, and meet Aune directly. The encounter establishes that the broker possesses only the relic's transport shell, not the relic itself, and turns a potential enemy into a route home.

Act IV — Unmaking the Quiet

Back in Veylaris Rest, the party enters the Old Choir Quarter and meets Verrin Mael at a Ledger safe house. Verrin's plan is brutally simple: bring the null-relic into the Lyricum's central anchor and either contain it there or destroy it before someone learns to weaponise its silence. Leora builds a temporary stabiliser, Ilyen maps the freight tunnels, Cassian prepares Keth as bait, and Verrin arranges extraction. The infiltration succeeds.

Riven orders the relic destroyed. Leora and Ilyen channel the Lyricum anchor and the null-relic comes apart. For several heartbeats the world goes silent; harbour lamps gutter, the local ley-grid fails, and the participants are left with echo-scars from the absence. The act saves the city from the relic's collapse while plunging Veylaris Rest into darkness.

The ruined anchor chamber also yields evidence the earlier site summary had failed to carry forward: a null-glass shard, a cracked ward crystal, Keth's note reading "Clause XI = I + X (First and Last)", and a half-melted object the campaign record calls the Thirteenth Seal. Its physical recovery in Campaign XI is now confirmed; the surviving record does not securely establish what the name means, and it does not make the object one of the ancient Great Seals.

Act V — The Republic's Shadow, Concluded

After sealing the Clause Zero vault, the group debriefs with Verrin. Leora, Ilyen, and Verrin identify the Mareths as Heralds and Witnesses of Taren Durrel's remembered law and recover a Republic-era account of twelve Herald Houses. They also restore a history the Republic had flattened: Taren did not stand alone in the founding myth; Zethyr's role in calling the Light and ending the Dominion had been suppressed. Riven's place in the old pattern is therefore not a crown or a command. It is witness.

On a rooftop over Veylaris Rest, Riven and Cassian turn the entire metaphysical crisis into the sort of joke only the two of them could make — imagining a Clause XII for their relationship, mercy, late mornings, and less rain. The joke matters because the campaign's central question has become personal: if law cannot make room for an ordinary life, it is not yet breathing.

Act VI — The Breath and the Blood

The group returns to Evermere and infiltrates the Vance Archive, recovering a Clause Zero Codex fragment before an Inquest sweep closes around them. Maela Vael hides the breach and provides an Editor mark; Renna Varr carries the group to a cove where Leora, Ilyen, and Riven decrypt the fragment. It describes the Breath-Bridge — a corrective within the Breathing Law that can restore mercy without dismantling structure.

At Evermere's Northern Sluice, the party performs the Bridge. Riven serves as Witness; Ilyen and Maela co-sign as Editors; Leora guides the recovered text. The ley shifts. The change is not an imposed command to be compassionate — the world simply regains the capacity to remember mercy as part of its own judgement.

Act VII — The Bridge of Mercy

The final journey returns the party to the Menge Expanse. Beneath Taren's Breath they find a living Clausal Court, one of the Twelve, where Mareth relics and Choir alloy answer Riven's mark. The Court offers three choices: erase, merge, or stand apart. Riven chooses to merge Clause Zero with the Twelve while remaining mortal and free.

The network accepts. Clause Zero becomes a permanent harmonic of mercy inside the Breathing Law. Twin auroras — gold for Law, blue for Light — ignite above the ice, and new paired Ley-Heirs begin to stir. The campaign's own final compendium calls this the Dominion Cycle restored; that label refers to the reappearance of a paired Law/Light pattern, not to the return of Taren's political Dominion or the restoration of guaranteed dynastic rule.

World State After Campaign XI

Geography. Bastion Saren is dormant after its reactivation, still present beneath the northern ice rather than destroyed. Veylaris Rest remains a free coastal republic of merchants, smugglers, scholars, and relic brokers, although the Unmaking temporarily darkens its harbour and cripples the Lyricum anchor. The Menge Expanse is thawing enough for new flora and altered auroras to be noticed.

Magic and law. Clause XI — the Breathing Law — is active, and the later Breath-Bridge and final merge permanently add Clause Zero's mercy harmonic to it. The Ley-Heir lattice is again capable of producing paired heirs, but Campaign X's Severing still matters: recurrence is no longer a guaranteed right to rule. Campaign XI ends with new Law/Light heirs beginning to awaken, not with Taren and Zethyr themselves returning and not with a restored empire.

Riven Mareth. Riven survives as a mortal Living Bridge rather than becoming a god, monarch, or permanent machine component. The Witness Mark remains; so does his life with Cassian. His legacy is Clause Zero — the Breath Between Laws — a corrective that makes compassion durable without making obedience compulsory.

Institutions. The Republic Inquest already existed throughout the campaign as a state agency monitoring ley disturbances and forbidden Choir-tech; the Bastion awakening, null-relic blackout, and Witness signatures intensify its attention rather than create it. The Hidden Ledger survives through covert cells. The Lyricum is divided between those who would codify the Breathing Law and those who fear turning it into another scripture of control.

The Twelve Houses. Late-campaign records name Mareth, Vael, Vance, Serin, Avenar, Tarell, Rhos, Dorn, Selene, Korin, Kethar, and Verris as twelve Herald Houses associated with Witnesses, Editors, Recorders, Judges, architects, messengers, guardians, stewards, healers, navigators, translators, and heretics. Their exact ancient origin is filtered through Republic-era recordkeeping; the campaign confirms that Riven learns this scheme, not that every later institutional genealogy is independently proven.

The Thirteenth Seal. A half-melted object identified in the campaign record as the Thirteenth Seal is recovered after the null-relic's destruction. That resolves the old question of whether Campaign XI contained an object by this name. Its origin, function, and the meaning of the title remain unresolved; it is not evidence for a thirteenth member of the ancient eight Great Seals.

Source and continuity note: this reconstruction follows the dedicated Campaign XI Summary — Final and Amora Campaign XI Compendium — Final, including their later addenda and final epilogue, ahead of older compact world summaries. Two source tensions remain visible rather than being silently repaired. First, an older XI framing calls the campaign "one century after the Choir's extinction," while the site's master dating places Campaign V around 2500 ARV and Campaign XI around 6550 ARV; the relative phrase is therefore treated as unresolved archival wording, not used to move either campaign. Second, older summaries disagree over Bastion Saren's construction era, calling it both pre-Dominion and Choir-era; this page confirms the fortress and its role in XI without assigning an unsupported exact origin date. The dedicated XI record also confirms the physical discovery of the object called the Thirteenth Seal, superseding the site's previous assumption that it existed only in a later relic table.
