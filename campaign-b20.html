The Way Home

The Storm Great Seal Recognises Its Warden

mid-343 ARV — a few weeks after Campaign B19, within the same modern year

Core Premise

The Way Home picks up a few weeks after Storm Walker, once Benjamin Strake has had time to try living an ordinary life between increasingly extraordinary expeditions. He and Galen Vardane are openly together, Pella Ossel and Rian Vesker are becoming a stable field pair as well as a new couple, and the Farbound Lodge is already confronting B19's practical consequence: the Badlands interior is no longer sealed behind a permanent storm wall, so more people are heading east. The campaign opens with a rescue rather than another Great Seal crisis — delver Kerris Dane is missing beneath a ruined Alderwick merchant site — and only afterward turns to the deliberate question B19 left open: what happens when Ben returns to Storm with no emergency forcing the contact. At the Hall of the Open Sky, the black star-pool finally becomes physically traversable, and at the Storm Great Seal itself, Ben receives a recognition B19 explicitly did not give him — he becomes its present Warden, not through bloodline, but through demonstrated action and care. With the Great Seal visitors gone, B20 spends its second half on the adventure Ben actually wants to keep having: recovering enchanted equipment from North Fountain's lost armoury and breaking into Deep Market's sealed treasury, then investing the resulting wealth back into the guild that raised him.

Where this sits relative to B19. B20 is a direct continuation of B19, not a cleanup campaign for an unfinished repair. B19 already completed the principal Storm discovery: the Great Seal was found, its historical function understood, and its accumulated atmospheric burden substantially relieved. B20 does not reveal a hidden second Storm Seal or another chain of necessary repairs. The one boundary it does move is precise — Ben ended B19 having directly contacted the Great Seal but not having been formally recognised as its Warden. B20 changes that state only when he returns after the crisis has passed and asks the Seal to admit his companions.

Party & Key Figures

  • Benjamin "Ben" Strake PC
    Human Ranger, Level 3 → 4, Storm Walker. Continues as a professional Farbound delver based in Greymount, carrying his silver hair, electric-blue eyes, and instinctive Storm sense forward from B19. Accepts Storm's recognition without treating Warden as a promotion above Farbound, a hereditary claim, or a reason to stop taking ordinary rescue and treasure contracts.
  • Galen Vardane
    Twenty-nine, Greymount road-and-gate officer and Ben's openly established boyfriend. Cannot routinely justify multi-day deep-Badlands expeditions through his civic role, so the relationship develops around separation and return — Ben spends nights at his North Ward flat and comes home to him after each expedition.
  • Pella Ossel
    Nineteen, now a fully proven Farbound field delver and Ben's little-sister figure. Mobile, aggressive, and independently useful in danger; receives the Fleetstep Boots at North Fountain and completes a long-postponed second date with Rian during the campaign.
  • Rian Vesker
    Twenty-one, Farbound rope, climbing, and terrain specialist. Repeatedly handles bracing, extraction, and anchor logic while remaining combat-capable; receives the Wayfarer's Axe and later the Cloak of Protection. His place on the team stays earned independently of dating Pella.
  • Maud Halcro
    Farbound Guildmaster and one of Ben's parents' closest friends. Handles the institutional consequences of the campaign — records, guild risk, and the practical use of the treasury windfall — that Ben deliberately doesn't play out himself.
  • Tessa Draith
    Farbound quartermaster and medic. Works with Maud to convert the expedition's abstract treasure value into medical stock, climbing gear, rescue capability, and secure storage.
  • Nicholas "Nick" Soren
    Human Sorcerer 3, Western Icewarden Custodian, and returning protagonist of The Second Measure. Present because his Western Frost custody and Great Seal research make him useful, not because B20 transfers protagonist authority to him — Storm's eightfold manifestation acknowledges Frost through him without granting him authority over the Frost Great Seal.
  • Ser Nora Durrel
    Young Durrel Warden and descendant of Jack Durrel's later line, introduced in B17. Serves as a living Heart-linked witness during the Great Seal return; Heart responds to her ancestry without appointing her Heart's Warden.
  • Professor Halden
    Senior Highever Collegium archaeologist. Remains evidence-first throughout, helping separate what B20 actually proves about the Inner Way, the Great Seal network, and Warden recognition from what it merely suggests.

Act-by-Act

Act I — After the Storm

B20 opens after B19 has already changed the map. The Badlands remain physically ruined and dangerous, but the permanent extreme storm regime is substantially calmer, and Farbound has more requests than before. Ben is not immediately sent into another crisis — he spends ordinary time with Galen, including a date and nights at the North Ward flat, their relationship openly monogamous and settled enough that the tension now comes from Ben's work taking him farther away, not from any uncertainty about whether they're together.

Act II–III — The Great Seal Visitors, and the Kerris Dane Recovery Claim

Nick Soren, Ser Nora Durrel, and Professor Halden arrive in Greymount after receiving B19's Storm Great Seal report. Ben walks them through the full field sequence, and the language matters throughout: Marker Sixteen is the Western Pressure Station, not the Great Seal; the Shelter manifestation is local Storm magic, not the Great Seal; Storm as a world-force is distinct from the Storm Great Seal that holds it within measure. Before the visit becomes a research trip, Farbound takes a practical Alderwick contract — delver Kerris Dane is missing beneath a merchant ruin after a collapse severed his rope. Ben leads Pella and Rian, joined by Nick, Nora, and Halden, since the site lies inside the rediscovered city and lets the specialists see modern field conditions firsthand.

Act IV–V — The Merchant Ruin, and Rescue Before Salvage

Survivors report Kerris separated by a collapse and his safety rope cleanly cut. At the ruin the party finds another recovery crew, old surviving wards, and a large six-legged plated creature loose in the machinery rooms. Ben chooses Kerris over the tempting open strongroom: Rian braces the unstable structure, Nora holds the creature off the casualty, Pella clears the route, Nick supports with magic, and Halden stays with Kerris once he's clear. Ben marks the creature with Hunter's Mark and drives Storm Walker lightning through his bow attacks to kill it. Kerris and bound survivor Torren Pike are both recovered alive, and the rival recovery crew is left as a local complication rather than escalated into a recurring rival faction.

Act VI — Ironcall and the Redhand

A civic disc recovered from the ruin opens a concealed Alderwick cache of old coin, jewellery, documents, and surviving equipment. Ben claims Ironcall, a blackened +1 longsword with lodestone-themed magic that can call itself back to his hand and manipulate unattended metal, along with the matching Redhand Bracer, which improves his defence when he isn't using a shield. Both are treated explicitly as high-fantasy adventuring items — not Seal keys, route controllers, or pieces of forgotten Great Seal infrastructure. Kerris returns alive to Greymount, and the first B20 delve closes as a successful rescue with useful treasure rather than the first link in a forced ruin-to-ruin chain.

Act VII — Back to the Shelter

Ben shows Nick, Nora, and Halden the parts of the B19 route relevant to Great Seal interpretation, beginning at the Shelter of the Open Sky. Inside, the impossible drizzle and open-sky effect remain, but the site feels calmer after B19. When Ben reaches toward the silver-blue manifestation that transformed him, it recognises him without repeating the transformation and produces a Stormstep Shard — a one-use crystal allowing a bonus-action Misty Step in a gust of silver-blue lightning.

Act VIII — The Inner Way Opens

At the Hall of the Open Sky, the surviving inscription remains exact — Hall of the Open Sky · Public Refuge in Storm · The Inner Way Is Not for Refuge — and B20 finally tests the black star-pool physically. Ben steps through and emerges at an isolated ridge waystation several hours east by ordinary travel; once he establishes the route, the rest of the party can follow. The waystation yields an emergency cache of potions, a Feather Fall scroll, and a nonmagical Alderwick dagger Ben gives to Pella, and from its own pool Ben senses a second active route farther east. The party verifies exactly three traversals — Hall to ridge waystation, ridge waystation to an eastern Badlands pool, and eastern pool back to the Hall — and B20 is deliberate that this is not an unlimited transit map or a complete ancient network.

Act IX — Storm Admits the Party

The eastern route shortens the approach to the Great Seal, which remains a vast living ring of rain, lightning, pressure, and wind — intact and substantially relieved since B19's release. It opens a calm path for Ben as before, but this time he asks it to bring his friends in. The Seal widens the corridor and admits Pella, Rian, Nick, Nora, and Halden, so what follows is independently witnessed rather than experienced by Ben alone.

Act X — The Storm Warden

Ben was not the Storm Warden at the end of B19. On this return, after the crisis has passed, the Great Seal recognises him directly and specifically as its current Warden — a recognition reflecting demonstrated custodial action rather than hereditary succession. He found Storm under dangerous strain, understood it was containing rather than causing the Badlands' weather, released pressure without breaking the Seal, and returned once there was no emergency forcing the contact; the Seal chooses the person who has already been doing the work. The ancient Storm Warden lineage remains entirely unresolved — it may survive, be fragmented, or be lost, and Ben's parents remain ordinary Farbound delvers rather than retroactive ancestors. Storm grants him Warden's Passage: once per long rest, as a bonus action, he can teleport up to thirty feet to a visible unoccupied space and may bring one willing creature standing close to him. He also retains an enduring sense of the Great Seal's direction and can recognise active Storm-linked thresholds such as the verified Inner Way pools.

Act XI — Eight Around an Empty Centre

Nick's presence produces a Frost-linked response; Nora's produces a Heart-linked one — acknowledgements of existing custodial and ancestral connections, not promotions or transfers of Great Seal authority. Around Storm, eight equal positions become visible around an empty centre: Storm bright and active, Frost and Heart answering, the other five positions present but quiet. This directly confirms the recurring geometry first documented in B17 — the Highever comparative apparatus, the Silver Bastion Heart chamber, and the Ridge evidence — as a real, witnessed structure for the first time. The centre remains unresolved: not declared a ninth Great Seal, not a Way-hand office, not a throne.

Act XII–XIII — What Storm Remembers, and a Modern Correspondence Network

At Ben's request, the Seal shares a limited nonverbal witness — living Alderwick before the Shattering, then the catastrophe fragmenting into magical systems failing and Storm holding what it can. Frost answers Nick with a brief impression of custody and preservation; Heart answers Nora with protection and endurance — answering echoes inside Storm's own contact, not complete archives of other Great Seals. Back in Greymount, Ben, Nick, Nora, and Halden agree on a deliberately modest arrangement: important Great Seal changes and significant records should be copied and passed between Greymount/Storm, Western Frost, Durrel Ridge, and Silver Bastion/Heart rather than becoming isolated for generations again. No point is declared superior, and nobody claims to have restored the exact ancient institution. Nick and Halden return toward Highever, Nora toward Durrel Ridge, and the visiting protagonists leave as friends and useful contacts rather than becoming Ben's permanent research committee.

Act XIV — Pella and Rian

With the Great Seal visitors gone, Ben makes good on earlier teasing and pushes Pella and Rian to stop postponing their second date. They go that afternoon and return visibly happy, describing it simply as very good. B20 doesn't force a formal label before they choose one — Pella remains Ben's little-sister figure, Rian remains a core teammate in his own right, and the new romance stays warm without becoming the reason either of them is in the field.

Act XV–XVII — Choose a Treasure Hunt, and North Fountain

Halden's translated records leave two concrete adventure leads: North Fountain, which once received enchanted military items transferred from Crown Hall, and Deep Market, whose surviving warning says not to enter the lower treasury "without hunters." Pella wants the dangerous-sounding vault; Rian wants the clearer reward; the group decides together and takes North Fountain first. They relocate the ruin, frighten off a few unknown local creatures rather than fighting them, and fully disable a linked pressure-plate and projectile defence protecting the lower armoury. Of five item recesses, four still hold enchanted equipment; the fifth is empty, and an old armoury punch proves the missing item was legitimately issued out before the site's final sealing — deliberately not another theft mystery. Ben claims the Northwind Bow and Lionguard Shield, Pella the Fleetstep Boots, and Rian the Wayfarer's Axe, alongside potions, enchanted arrows, and other valuables.

Act XVIII–XX — Deep Market, Without Hunters, and Six Vaults

At Deep Market the next morning, fresh tracks show unidentified small clawed creatures using the outer ruin — a species B20 deliberately leaves unidentified rather than treating as kobolds or any other stock creature. Ben, Pella, and Rian frighten them off with a coordinated intimidation display rather than a fight, then find the creatures have been avoiding a bronze-faced door marked Lower Treasury. The ancient warning proves literal: the entry requires hand, eye, and bow conditions once satisfied by an authorised guard. With the sighting mechanism failed after twelve centuries, Pella finds a manual override, Ben holds it, and Northwind puts an arrow through the recessed target to open the doors safely. Inside, six named vaults — Coin Reserve, Gem and Jewellery Reserve, Merchant Securities, Emergency Stores, Restricted Deposits, and Special Custody — yield liquid coin and trade bars, gems and jewellery, potions, and, in Restricted Deposits and Special Custody, a Bag of Holding, a Chime of Opening with seven uses remaining, and a Cloak of Protection that goes to Rian.

Act XXI — Invest in Farbound

Across both sites the trio recovers 608 gp in immediately liquid coin and trade bars before the added value of gems, jewellery, and antiques. Ben proposes a modest personal split — he, Pella, and Rian each take 100 gp — with the remaining 308 gp and the saleable treasure voluntarily assigned to a Farbound improvement fund. It's the group's own choice, not something imposed by Maud, and she and Tessa take over the spending — medical stock, ropes, climbing gear, rescue equipment, storage, and possibly further magical communication — without turning Ben into guild treasurer.

Act XXII–XXIII — The Three Who Came Home, and I'm Back

The Lodge celebrates without treating Ben as its sole hero — Maud raises a cup "to the three who went out, and the three who came home." Before leaving for the night, Ben returns to his room, where his parents' sketch, their route map, and a framed image of Galen sit together; where he habitually touches the frame and says "I'll be back" before an expedition, he touches it again at campaign's close and says, "I'm back." At Galen's green door, Ben reports the successful expedition, the personal share, and the decision to invest the rest in Farbound — reducing his growing list of identities to the one that matters most privately: Storm Warden, Ranger, delver, treasure hunter, and Galen's boyfriend.

World State After Campaign B20

Benjamin Strake. Human Ranger 4 / Storm Walker, twenty-five, with 40 max HP and all three attunement slots filled by Ironcall, the Redhand Bracer, and Northwind Bow; Lionguard Shield requires no attunement. Is the current recognised Storm Warden by direct Great Seal recognition — a B20 development, not something that should be read back into B19. Remains a Farbound delver: Warden does not make him owner of Storm, ruler of the Badlands, or administrator of a restored ancient institution. Retains Warden's Passage and the unused Stormstep Shard. Openly monogamous with Galen Vardane.

Storm Great Seal / Storm Warden. Storm remains intact, active, stable, and substantially relieved after B19; B20 does not create a second Storm crisis. Storm Warden recognition reflects direct Great Seal choice through demonstrated action and custody, not proof of hereditary bloodline — the ancient Storm Warden lineage remains a wholly separate, unresolved genealogical question, and office and lineage can now diverge in the modern era.

Great Seal network. Eight equal Seal positions around an empty centre are directly manifested for the first time within Storm: Storm active, Frost and Heart answering in the presence of Nick and Nora, five other positions present but quiet. The empty centre remains unresolved and is not a ninth Great Seal, Way-hand office, or other institution by default. A modest modern correspondence network now runs between Greymount/Storm, Western Frost, Durrel Ridge, and Silver Bastion/Heart, with Highever useful for copied scholarship — a new cooperative arrangement, not a restored ancient hierarchy.

The Inner Way. The Hall of the Open Sky's black star-pool is confirmed as an active physical magical threshold. Verified B20 traversals are limited to three: Hall to ridge waystation, ridge waystation to an eastern Badlands pool, and eastern pool back to the Hall. Its builders, full route map, and wider relationship to other ancient networks remain unresolved — this is not an unlimited transit system.

Alderwick. The city remains overwhelmingly unexplored despite B19 and B20's rediscoveries. B20 adds a confirmed modern recovery site, the Hall/ridge/eastern Inner Way route, North Fountain's lower armoury, and Deep Market's lower treasury to Farbound's practical map layer, demonstrating that Alderwick can support conventional high-fantasy delving — traps, treasure, monsters, magical loot — without every discovery becoming Great Seal infrastructure.

Farbound Guild. Materially stronger after the North Fountain and Deep Market recoveries: a Bag of Holding and Chime of Opening as shared expedition gear, and 308 gp plus saleable treasure reinvested into medical, rescue, and climbing capability, managed by Maud and Tessa rather than by Ben.

Open threads carried forward. The modern survival, fragmentation, or extinction of the ancient Storm Warden lineage; the exact function of the empty centre in the eightfold pattern; the wider extent and access rules of the Inner Way beyond the three verified routes; the long-term operation of the new custodial correspondence network; the remaining Great Seals and the five quiet positions witnessed within Storm; and the overwhelming majority of ruined Alderwick, still unmapped.

Canon status: follows the source account closely. B20 directly resolves the Warden-recognition question B19 deliberately left open — see the Canon Ledger for the specific corrections and locks, the Magic Codex for Warden's Passage and the eightfold Great Seal geometry, and the Atlas for the Inner Way and Alderwick's newly recovered sites.
