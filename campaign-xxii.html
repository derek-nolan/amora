Timeline A · Campaign XXII

The Shattered Quiet

4CE begins — 1,000-year jump

9650–9660 ARV Primary location: Caelwick Frontier Post, East Vanmere

Opening World State

Talen Marric's Severance in Campaign XXI unwound the Heart-Veil and ended three millennia of recurrence. The world stepped abruptly into quiet: no ley-heirs, no hum beneath stone, no Chorus, no Dominion cadence, no concordance law. Amora reverted to a fragile medieval drift, governed by roads, rivers, toll-gates, oaths, and appetite — not harmonic infrastructure. The Null Compact spread inconsistently; many regions adopted it only in theory. Yet the Severance didn't wipe the slate clean: in scattered pockets where the ritual never fully reached, Dominion substrates lingered beneath the earth — silent, inert, but not dead. One such fault lay beneath the frontier post of Caelwick.

Act-by-Act

The Shard Chamber

A patrol uncovers a buried scriptorium under Caelwick — a Dominion administrative vault fused with Veil stabilisers, its melted sigils and crushed halls hiding one surviving Shard-Pattern Relay. Arden, Caelen, Kett, and Lysa descend and find the relic: a Dominion mirror-engine that should have died with the Heart-Veil.

The Pattern Wakes

The relay responds to Caelen's will — not because he's an heir, not because of destiny, but because Dominion design always answered structure, discipline, and desire for order. The shard's dormant logic begins shaping new behavioural architecture around the group, accidentally. Arden's responsiveness forms a second anchor point; a non-ritual dyad-like imprint begins to build. Historians later call this moment the first Quiet Fault.

The First Signature

A second descent to the Under-City Pumps lets the pumps answer the shard's pulse — harmless for centuries until disturbed by will. The relay records a two-person pattern imprint: not a ley-heir, not a chain, something new. At the Decision at Caelwick, Caelen proposes expanding the survey to other continents; the group agrees, unaware of what has begun.

World State After Campaign XXII

Geography. Caelwick Frontier Post (population ~2,400, in East Vanmere) is confirmed as a rainswept charter outpost built atop layered foundations — current frontier town, Veil-era pump networks, and collapsed Dominion scribe-vaults. Its population remains unaware a sub-surface shard still dreams below.

Magic. Dominion logic is rekindled, accidentally — not the cycle, not the heirs, but a remnant algorithm still able to mirror will. This is a direct counterpoint to Campaign XXI's claim that the recursion was over: proof the Severance didn't prevent recurrence by intent, only by ritual mechanism. Future surveys will reveal similar sub-surface Quiet Fault pockets across the world.

The Durrel Line. No Durrel figures appear; Arden and Caelen's imprint is explicitly framed as new — "not a ley-heir, not a chain, something new" — the first major pattern-recurrence in the record with no claimed lineage connection to the Durrel name at all.

Institutions. Caelwick resumes daily life, unaware of its place in history. A tiny, accidental shard-pattern migrates forward a thousand years through archival circuits, waiting for reactivation. Caelen's story will end in death and Severance in Campaign XXIII — but his shard-coded echo survives, carrying Arden and Caelen's imprint into the Fourth Cyberpunk Era. The world of 4CE will learn that dominion can reappear as behavioural firmware inside armor and law.

Canon status: follows the character register; this campaign is set at the threshold before the actual 1,000-year jump — the full reinstantiation in 4CE happens at the opening of Campaign XXIII.
