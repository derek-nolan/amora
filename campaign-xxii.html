Timeline A · Campaign XXII

The Shattered Quiet

4CE begins — 1,000-year jump

9650–9660 ARV Primary location: Caelwick Frontier Post, East Vanmere

Opening World State

A generation after the Severance unwound the Heart-Veil and ended three millennia of recurrence. Amora reverted to a fragile medieval drift — governed by roads, rivers, and oaths, not harmonic infrastructure. But the Severance did not wipe the slate clean: in scattered pockets, Dominion substrates lingered beneath the earth.

Party & Key Figures

  • Arden Vale
    Rogue and investigator; co-discovers the Shard Chamber beneath Caelwick and forms half of an unintended shard-pattern imprint.
  • Caelen Marrith
    Paladin Captain whose will the Shard Relay responds to — the primary vector of the Dominion-pattern's reactivation.
  • Lysa Thorne
    Charter Scout; first to observe behavioural anomalies around the shard activation.
  • Kett Ironrun
    Dwarven merchant-archivist who recognises Dominion architecture and documents the Shard Relay.

Act-by-Act

The Shard Chamber

A patrol uncovers a buried scriptorium under Caelwick. Arden, Caelen, Kett, and Lysa descend and find the Shard-Pattern Relay — a Dominion mirror-engine that should have died with the Heart-Veil.

The Pattern Wakes

The relay responds to Caelen's will — not because he's an heir, but because Dominion design always answered structure, discipline, and desire for order. Arden's responsiveness forms a second anchor point; a non-ritual dyad-like imprint begins to build.

The First Signature

A second descent lets the pumps answer the shard's pulse. The relay records a two-person pattern imprint — not a ley-heir, not a chain, something new. Caelen proposes expanding the survey, unaware of what has begun.

Final World State

Ley-heirs are gone and the Dominion is dead, but its architecture survives. A tiny, accidental shard-pattern migrates forward a thousand years through archival circuits, waiting for reactivation. Caelen's story will end in death and Severance — but his shard-coded echo survives, carrying Arden and Caelen's imprint into the Fourth Cyberpunk Era.

Canon status: follows the character register; this campaign is set at the threshold before the actual 1,000-year jump — the full reinstantiation in 4CE happens at the opening of Campaign XXIII.
