Timeline A · Campaign XXII

The Shattered Quiet

4CE begins — 1,000-year jump

9650–9660 ARV Primary location: Caelwick Frontier Post, East Vanmere

Opening World State

A year after Talen Marric's Severance unspooled the Heart-Veil, ending the ley-heir cycle and returning destiny to mortals, the world hasn't fully stabilised. The Null Compact (Consent, Reversibility, Witness) spreads unevenly; old Dominion corridors lie as dormant ruins; the great powers no longer listen for hums beneath the stone. The map is reverting to a quieter, medieval world defined by roads, rivers, toll-gates, and appetite — not ley-lines. And yet cracks in the Severance remain: sites untouched by Talen's rite still hold sub-surface charge — echoes, not heirs.

Party & Key Figures

  • Arden Vale PC Co-discovered the Shard Chamber; forms half of the unintended shard-pattern imprint
    Quick-tongued, clever wanderer with a survivor's instinct; accidentally aligned with the traits Taren's ancient pattern once sought — loyalty, desire, responsiveness, and a dangerous willingness to follow someone stronger.
  • Caelen Marrith Paladin, Captain of the Charter Guard
    Charismatic and composed, with an instinct for leadership — and something darker: a quiet hunger for order, for shaping a world drifting after the Severance. Treats the frontier as a place to rebuild structure, not merely keep peace.
  • Lysa Thorne
    Charter Warden Scout; practical, skeptical, the first to note the strange behaviour of Caelen around the shard chamber.
  • Kett Ironrun
    Dwarf merchant-archivist; keeper of odd relics salvaged from collapsed Dominion sites, skeptical but curious. Serves as guide to the ruins.

Act-by-Act

Act I — The Frontier Inquiry

Arden, Caelen, Lysa, and Kett investigate Dominion ruins below Caelwick, a rainswept trade-republic outpost built atop an unnamed Dominion-era scriptorium — roads muddy, archives gutted, guild charters pinned with sunset dates. A collapsed vault reveals a glowing crystalline relay, impossible after the Severance.

Act II — The Shard Chamber

The group attempts to study the node — a crystalline matrix later called the Last Shard Node, one of a Dominion-era design meant to mirror a wielder's will into structured command, disabled at the Severance. When Caelen approaches, the relay activates with a low pulse, mirroring his intent. Arden notices, innocently, how keen Caelen becomes in understanding the relic.

Act III — The Pattern Wakes

Exploration of deeper vault levels triggers further activation. The scriptorium's Dominion routines begin reconstructing behavioural logic around the group — drawing from Caelen's will. Not because he's an echo, not because of destiny, but because the world mirrors intent, exactly as the Nulls warned back in Campaign XXI.

Act IV — The Charter Gap

Caelen realises he's operating without oversight — a missing charter renewal leaves his patrol legally unaccountable. He begins making choices that quietly diverge from civic duty.

Act V — The Withdraw & Debrief

The team surfaces, unsettled. Caelen appears calm but reflective — he recognises the relic responds only to him. Arden notices Caelen becoming more protective, more decisive, more certain.

Act VI — The Pumps & Second Descent

A return below reveals the Under-City Pumps — ancient Veil-era water engines fused with Dominion stabilizers, harmless for centuries until disturbed — now reacting to the shard's activation. Caelen begins interpreting the ruin not as a collapse but as a system waiting for structure.

Act VII — The First Signature

The shard records a pattern imprint — not bloodline, not command, but the beginning of a dyad-like resonance formed accidentally, through proximity, trust, and shared action in the ruins. It is not a ley-heir. It is not a cycle reborn. It is something new: a Dominion system rebuilding itself around the nearest strong will — Caelen's.

Act VIII — Decision at Caelwick

The group prepares to survey other continents for further shard remnants. Caelen proposes expanding beyond Vanmere; he wants insight into what survived the Severance. The team agrees, unaware of what has begun. Over the following months, historians later identify this as the beginning of the Shattered Quiet — the subtle reawakening of Dominion logic not by decree or echo, but by accident, affection, and intent, exactly as the Nulls warned was possible. A thousand years later, this tiny unintended pattern survives through archival circuits and forgotten Dominion nodes, and awakens Caelen and Arden in a new age — not as echoes, not as heirs, but as survivors of a pattern older than they ever understood.

World State After Campaign XXII

Geography. Caelwick Frontier Post (a rainswept charter outpost built atop layered foundations — current frontier town, Veil-era pump networks, and collapsed Dominion scribe-vaults) is confirmed. Its population resumes normal trade, unaware a sub-surface shard still dreams below.

Magic. Dominion logic is rekindled, accidentally — not the cycle, not the heirs, but a remnant algorithm still able to mirror will. This directly counters Campaign XXI's claim that the recursion was over: the Severance didn't prevent recurrence by intent, only by ritual mechanism. Historians later identify this as the first of multiple "Quiet Fault" sites where the Severance never fully took.

The Durrel Line. No Durrel figures appear; Arden and Caelen's imprint is explicitly framed as new — "not a ley-heir, not a chain, something new" — the first major pattern-recurrence in the record with no claimed lineage connection to the Durrel name at all.

Institutions. The Null Compact is proven stressed — the accidental activation shows the Null ethic wasn't enough to prevent the world from mirroring strong will. Caelen's story will end in death and Severance in Campaign XXIII — but his shard-coded echo survives, carrying Arden and Caelen's imprint into the Fourth Cyberpunk Era.

Canon status: follows the source account directly; this campaign is set at the threshold before the actual 1,000-year jump — the full reinstantiation in 4CE happens at the opening of Campaign XXIII.
