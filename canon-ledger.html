Timeline A · Campaign XXV

The Broken Anchor

Fracture → Temporal Reboot

9750–9800 ARV Primary location: Academy of Resonant Studies → Silver Bastion, Age of Founding

Opening World State

Twelve years into an age convinced the Severance, the Quiet Recursion, and the Resonant Veil had finally stabilised magic into ethical resonance. That illusion is about to end. Resonance anomalies beneath the Academy of Resonant Studies are dismissed as background noise — until Arden recognises the signature.

Party & Key Figures

  • Solan
    Emergent anchor and center of a six-point formation; dyad-tethered with Marrek. Carried through to the Age of Founding.
  • Marrek
    Stabiliser hunted across eras; the fracture repeatedly targets his thread. Dyad-tethered with Solan.
  • Arden Vale
    First Safe Anchor of 4CE; recognises Caelen's shard-signature and leads the severance ritual.
  • Zeth Caden & Talren Vale
    The world's first fully stable Living Dyad; stabilise the fracture formation.
  • Rian Thale
    Emotional stabiliser; balances Arden during the Caelen echo confrontation.
  • Caelen Echo
    A behavioural shard-core composite — half Dominion, half Widow, half Neon circuitry — fixated on claiming Marrek's thread. Destroyed.

Act-by-Act

The Fracture Beneath the Academy

Resonance noise near the Academy's lower sigil arrays turns out to be a Dominion behavioural fragment identical to the one that once claimed Arden's life — Caelen Marrith's shard-core, still alive as broken code, targeting Marrek across all ages.

The Six-Point Resonance Formation

A six-person anchor formation — balanced around Solan, stabilised by Marrek, reinforced by Zeth, Talren, Arden, and Rian — allows Arden to deploy a medieval-era Severance Rite meant to cut command-logic from living souls. The echo collapses.

Temporal Collapse

The unity seal they perform on the dying shard produces a full seal for one heartbeat, catastrophic overload the next, then total temporal implosion. All six are expelled from the fracture and arrive — physically, alive — in the Age of Founding, at the training grounds of Silver Bastion.

Final World State

Timeline A ends: the events of Campaigns I-XXIV remain real, but sealed in a closed temporal layer. Timeline B begins the moment the six open their eyes — Amora's history resets to the moment before the rise of the Heartwardens and the Dominion. Zethyr and Taren are teenagers again. The Caelen line is permanently concluded, with no shard, imprint, or command-logic residue left behind. Arden, Rian, Zeth, Talren, Solan, and Marrek carry resonance theory into the world's beginning.

Canon status: follows the character register directly. This is the hinge point of the entire chronicle — see Timeline B, Campaign B1, for what happens next.
