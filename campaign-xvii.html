Timeline A · Campaign XVI

The Corridor Crown

Third Cyberpunk Age

8240–8245 ARV Primary location: Highever ↔ Tarenus ↔ Port Annex

Opening World State

Still the Third Cyberpunk Age; Concord already exists. This arc extends law from city to corridor and coast — Neon Concord's proof-not-panic ethic becomes habit across an entire route.

Party & Key Figures

  • Kaeso Vyr
    Rogue/paladin hybrid and Consort — an unknowing Zethyr-echo who awakens the reconstructed Taren ("Coren") and enters a consensual Chain-bond with him.
  • Coren
    A reconstructed Taren-type echo; Veil Sovereign who re-forges the Chain with Kaeso, expanding from rooms to crowds via Cadence rites.
  • Eryx
    Sanctuary Keeper at the Red Lattice; holds the 'unwinding words' that can recall hazed vows.
  • Kade Loric
    Broker and complicating contact who uncovers Kaeso's Lattice visits.
  • Josten ("Subject J")
    Old House finance antagonist, neutralized during a black-box operation.

Act-by-Act

New Standards

Doors Law, Quiet Orders, Festival Sixteen, Consort Annulus, and Cadence Court emerge as the corridor learns to inhale on the Consort's count and release on the Crown's punctuation. The method is procedural, not miraculous.

Riding the Chain

Opposition tries to "ride" the Chain's black-silver signature to stage counter-quiet flash mobs. Coren baits and folds them into Court-First on the steps — a corridor that feels inevitable without being a throne.

Coastal Copying

Harbor satellites adopt cadence by choice; the myth of the two echoes (Crown and Consort) is acknowledged, yet firmly bounded by appeal, ledger, and optics — a modern re-reading of the old Zethyr/Taren bond that refuses mandate while preserving partnership.

Final World State

Corridor Cadence — Doors Law, Quiet Orders, Festival Sixteen, Consort Annulus, and Cadence Court — is recognized as binding municipal procedure across Highever, Tarenus, and the Port. The Chain functions as city habit, not compulsion: rooms answer the Consort's First Step, and the Crown merely punctuates.

Canon status: follows the character register; Coren here is a separate reconstructed echo from Coren Veyne of Campaign IV.
