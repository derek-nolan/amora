Timeline A · Campaign VI

The Restorer

Age of Reawakening

c. 5500 ARV Primary location: Evermere Collegium

Opening World State

After machine-faith disasters, the world hunts for older, gentler magic. Evermere stands as a Collegium; the ruins of Concord and Choir tech sleep beneath new cities. Magic returns in weak pulses through damaged leylines and half-remembered shrines.

Party & Key Figures

  • Kael Duren
    The Restorer; a Heart-shard seeker who refuses to treat the returning currents as either divine command or exploitable infrastructure.

Act-by-Act

A Shard, Heard Rather Than Read

Kael recovers a Heart Shard and listens to it as memory rather than instruction. Collegium scholars argue over access, profit, faith, and public safety around him.

Distributed Custody

The party helps determine which discoveries can be shared and which need guardianship, replacing the old idea of a chosen ruler with distributed custody: vows matter because people choose them, not because the world enforces them.

The Collegium Equilibria

Kael never finds a perfect answer, and the faint bond he senses in the ley never provides one — and that incompleteness becomes the age's virtue. The Collegium Equilibria grows from these decisions.

Final World State

Amora begins to believe magic can follow relationships without turning them into chains. The idea that magic should follow choices and vows rather than decree becomes a stabilising doctrine for later ages, and the Collegium Equilibria's traditions root themselves directly in Kael's motto: "Through Memory, Rebirth."

Canon status: follows the character register; a short campaign with a single confirmed principal figure.
