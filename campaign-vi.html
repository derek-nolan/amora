Timeline A · Campaign VI

The Restorer

Age of Reawakening — "Through Memory, Rebirth"

c. 5500 ARV Primary location: Evermere Collegium

Opening World State

After the machine-faith disasters that closed the Age of Silence, the world hunts for older, gentler magic. Evermere stands now as a Collegium of scholars, and beneath its new streets, the ruins of Concord and Choir technology sleep quietly, waiting to be rediscovered — or left alone.

Party & Key Figures

  • Kael Duren PC "The Restorer." Rediscovers a Heart Shard from the earliest recorded age, and reframes magic around his own creed: "Through Memory, Rebirth."
  • Collegium scholars
    Support and study Kael's rediscovery, debating how widely the reawakened power should be shared.
  • Rival factions
    Want the Heart Shard for power or profit rather than restoration — the pressure that shapes Kael's choices throughout.

Act-by-Act

The Rediscovery

Kael's work centres on a genuinely old find: a Heart Shard surviving from the very earliest age of Amoran magic, long predating the machine cults and Choir engines that came after it. His approach reframes magic entirely — not machine-bound, not empire-bound, but relationship-bound, tied to the people who hold and share it rather than any single institution.

Consent and Custody

As word of the Shard spreads, Kael and the Collegium face real pressure from rival factions who want it for power or profit rather than restoration. The campaign plays out through that tension — deciding how widely to share the rediscovered power and what safeguards to build around it, deliberately echoing Taren and Zethyr's own history while trying hard not to repeat their mistakes. Shared custody, genuine consent, and distributed guardianship all get tested here for the first time as deliberate design choices, not accidents.

The Collegium Equilibria

Kael's motto — magic following choices and vows rather than decree — becomes the root doctrine of what's later formalised as the Collegium Equilibria. It's a stabilising philosophy that later ages return to again and again whenever institutions start drifting back toward command-based magic instead of consent-based magic.

World State After Campaign VI

Geography. Evermere's Collegium becomes the institutional seat of Kael's doctrine, distinct from its later incarnations under Concord and beyond.

Magic. The Heart Shard is confirmed as genuinely ancient — older than the Choir, older than the Dominion — and its rediscovery establishes relationship-bound magic as a real, documented alternative to both machine-bound and empire-bound systems.

The Durrel Line. No Durrel figures appear directly, but the campaign deliberately plays with echoes of the Taren/Zethyr dynamic, this time consciously trying to avoid repeating their specific mistakes through shared custody and consent rather than command.

Institutions. The Collegium Equilibria and the later Collegium tradition both root themselves directly in Kael's founding motto. The idea that magic should follow choice and vow rather than decree becomes a stabilising doctrine referenced across several later ages.
