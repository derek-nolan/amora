Timeline A · Campaign VI

The Restorer

Age of Reawakening

c. 5500 ARV Primary location: Evermere Collegium

Opening World State

After the Choir went dark, civilisation didn't recover all at once. Magic returns in weak pulses through damaged leylines, half-remembered shrines, and relics whose purpose no living scholar fully understands. Evermere stands as a Collegium; the ruins of Concord and Choir tech sleep beneath new cities.

Party & Key Figures

  • Kael Durren — Completed (doc)
    The Restorer; a Heart-shard seeker who refuses to treat the returning currents as either divine command or exploitable infrastructure. Foreruns later Wardens.

Act-by-Act

A Shard, Heard Rather Than Read

Kael Durren became the Restorer because he refused to treat the returning currents as either divine command or exploitable infrastructure. He recovers a Heart Shard and listens to it as memory rather than instruction. Around him, Collegium scholars argue over access, profit, faith, and public safety.

Distributed Custody

The party helps determine which discoveries can be shared and which need guardianship. Their work replaces the old idea of a chosen ruler with distributed custody: vows matter because people choose them, not because the world enforces them.

The Collegium Equilibria

Kael never finds a perfect answer, and the faint bond he senses in the ley never provides one — and that incompleteness becomes the age's virtue. The Collegium Equilibria grows from these decisions, rooting itself in Kael's motto: "Through Memory, Rebirth."

World State After Campaign VI

Geography. Evermere is confirmed standing as a functioning Collegium in this era, its scholars actively debating custody of returning magic — the ruins of the Concord and Choir eras lie dormant but not gone beneath it.

Magic. A foundational doctrine shift: magic begins to be understood as something that should follow relationships and choices rather than decree or infrastructure. This is the first explicit statement of the principle that later Timeline B independently rediscovers through Resonance and the Order of Anchors.

The Durrel Line. No Durrel appears directly this campaign — Kael Durren's name-echo (see the Canon Ledger's note on Dur- names as Durrel derivatives) is the closest connection, suggesting the line persists in altered form even when not centred in the narrative.

Institutions. The Collegium Equilibria is founded, and its later Collegium traditions root themselves in Kael's motto. The idea that magic should follow choices and vows rather than decree becomes a stabilising doctrine for every age that follows — a direct rebuke of the Choir's forced equality in Campaign V.

Canon status: follows the character register; a short campaign with a single confirmed principal figure.
