Core Premise

A Brother's Oath is the fourth directly played Oath Wars & War of Ash and Glass prequel, moving the deep-history chronology forward roughly two years from The Price of Command and shifting its viewpoint from Matt Harcourt to his nineteen-year-old younger brother Mark. Where B23 showed Glass command technology bent into a private coercive derivative inside an already-established relationship, B24 shows the same wartime technology used from the very beginning of one: Mark falls genuinely in love with Darian Rell, a newly arrived Caldreth recruit secretly assigned by Glass handler Sabine Rook to use that relationship as a route toward Matt Harcourt and Tarenus Durrel. Darian conceals Glass-laced personal fittings in their shared domestic life, conditions Mark's responses, and uses his genuine trust to obtain a copied military dispatch and a run of authentic signatures for Bellweather Trading & Provisioning — a merchant front moving concealed Glass components under humanitarian cover. The scheme unravels through Darian's own ambition: he murders his handler Sabine Rook and finance associate Edrin Pell to seize control of the operation, and is finally exposed when financier Lady Veyla Marrow confronts him at a customs-house meeting. Mark breaks the immediate conditioning himself, discloses the compromise to his own chain of command, and spends the campaign's back half undoing the damage in the field: holding Greyfen Crossing against a Glass siege wagon, stopping his own brother from executing a bound prisoner, freeing forcibly altered Fellbrook quarryman Tomas Fenwick from an external wagon controller, and arresting the team meant to force open an unidentified ancient doorway beneath Kelder's Reach.

Where this sits relative to B18, B21, B22, and B23. B24 is played after B23 but is chronologically the fourth deep-history prequel, roughly two years after B23's own c. -922 ARV setting and about 1,263 years before B22's later c. 343 ARV present. The Seven Refusal remains roughly a year ahead (c. -919 ARV); the Great Seal setting cycle remains roughly six to seven years ahead (c. -914 to -913 ARV), with Heart set last at Silver Bastion and Tarenus Durrel's first permanent Heart watch following immediately after; and the Shattering remains roughly fifteen years ahead (c. -905 ARV). Tarenus is already a Heartbinder sworn to Devotion and a Paladin 4 by B24, but B24 does not make him Heart Warden before that later Heart setting. B23's local victories — Voss's Rookwatch and Eastvault programme dismantled, Joren Cassel dead, Kelder's Reach's old command network destroyed — all remain genuine and are not undone by B24's discovery of an unrelated subterranean target at the same site. B24 does not alter B22's modern c. 343 ARV Corvane / Vesperhold state in any way — it advances deep-history canon, not a new chapter of the Age of Rediscovery.

Party & Key Figures

  • Mark Harcourt PC
    Human, Paladin 2 → 4, Oath of the Crown (Law, Loyalty, Courage, Responsibility). Nineteen, openly attracted to men, a Caldreth garrison paladin and the younger brother of B23 protagonist Matt Harcourt. Wants to be taken seriously beside his brother's reputation and makes real independent decisions once given road duties. Closes the campaign Level 4 with STR 16, HP 32 (provisional), AC 19, and his Level 4 feat still unselected.
  • Darian Rell Lover / Antagonist
    Twenty-one, introduced as a confident newly assigned Caldreth junior paladin. Genuinely attracted to Mark and genuinely attracted to possessing him — recruited by Sabine Rook to use that relationship as a route to Matt Harcourt and Tarenus Durrel, he conceals Glass-laced fittings in their shared domestic life, obtains a copied dispatch and coerced Bellweather Trading authorisations through Mark, and murders Sabine Rook and Edrin Pell to seize control of the scheme himself. Recaptured alive at Greyfen; ends the campaign bound and awaiting Caldreth trial.
  • Matt Harcourt
    Twenty-four, Paladin 4, Oath of Devotion, Mark's older brother. Genuinely angry on Mark's behalf and responsive to Mark's warning rather than deaf to it, Matt nearly executes the bound Darian at the northern service road before Mark stops him — both brothers are formally reprimanded afterward. Now in a romantic relationship with Ronan Vale, roughly a year old by B24.
  • Tarenus "Taren" Durrel
    About twenty-five, Paladin 4, Oath of Devotion, already a Heartbinder but not yet the permanent Heart Warden. Remains with Elira Vane, not engaged. Authorises only a limited introduction for Sabine Rook to northern settlements — not the unrestricted Durrel access the Glass operation actually wanted.
  • Elira Vane
    Taren's girlfriend, independent scout and archer. Takes part in the Greyfen approach and the Blackthorn Hollow pursuit, reading wagon tracks to confirm the Glass convoy's route. No engagement, marriage, or children are established for her and Taren at B24's close.
  • Ronan Vale
    Caldreth roadwarden, archer, and tracker — openly Matt's boyfriend by B24, roughly a year into the relationship that began as an unresolved attraction at the close of B23. Fights at Greyfen, assists the prisoner transfer, and follows the party on the Kelder's Reach pursuit.
  • Sabine Rook Antagonist
    Darian's Glass handler and recruiter, assigned to exploit Mark's closeness to Matt and Taren. Murdered by Darian at a riverside storehouse when she tries to retain control of the operation and of Mark.
  • Tomas Fenwick
    A Fellbrook quarryman abducted and forcibly altered into a living breaching weapon — an implanted chest crystal, smaller growth fragments, and a collar linking to an external wagon controller. Freed by Mark's natural-20 Arcana identification of a safe isolation catch; survives, recovers, and begins voluntary movement again, though the chest crystal remains. Editorially corrected from a live-play duplicate of B23's distinct Crown paladin Garran Holt, who is unchanged.
  • Lady Veyla Marrow
    A dangerous, capable representative of the financing interests behind the Glass merchant network. Confronts Darian with proof of the hidden shipments and Mark's compromised authorisations at a customs-house meeting, then takes him into temporary custody. Her own employer and the full funding chain are left unidentified.
  • Edrin Pell
    A Caldreth finance and counting-house associate in the Bellweather merchant web. Murdered by Darian when he moves to summon help; Mark is not shown learning of this killing on screen. No established relation to B23's unnamed Crown soldier Pell.

Act-by-Act

Act I — Caldreth, the Younger Harcourt

B24 opens with Mark serving ordinary eastern-road garrison duty alongside Darian Rell, a newly assigned Caldreth recruit introduced to him for routine patrol work. Mark's nineteen-year-old desire to be taken seriously — rather than simply Matt Harcourt's little brother — grounds his early decisions, and his attraction to Darian begins as his own genuine feeling rather than a magically manufactured one.

Act II — The Old Mill and the Glass Rider

An early assignment crosses an unnamed toll bridge and the Old Mill, distinct from both Greyfen and the later Rimeholt mill. During a road-camp stop, a Glass cavalryman attempts to identify and take Mark alive under a cavalry order marked "THE YOUNGER HARCOURT. ALIVE" — Darian covertly speaks to the rider after arranging time alone away from Mark, an operative contact rather than a coincidence.

Act III — Bellweather Lane

Mark and Darian's relationship deepens into a shared home on Bellweather Lane. The domestic baseline is real: they flirt, build a life together, and the Glass programme that follows exploits an existing bond rather than inventing one from nothing. Concealed within intimate objects — wrist fittings, clothing contacts, collar-like equipment — Darian's hidden Glass components begin narrowing Mark's ability to refuse, recognise wrongdoing, or freely disclose it at chosen moments.

Act IV — The Copied Dispatch

Using the conditioning, Darian induces Mark to copy a classified Caldreth military dispatch. The act is a genuine security compromise — Mark's hand, but not his free choice — and its full downstream consequence is not established; no specific lost battle or casualty is attributed to it.

Act V — Bellweather Trading and Provisioning

Through the merchant front Bellweather Trading & Provisioning, Darian obtains Mark's authentic signature and personal seal for northern transport paperwork, framing the arrangements as ordinary commercial necessity or a foundation for their shared future. Some of the ostensibly humanitarian cargo conceals Glass components. Mark also recommends Darian's handler Sabine Rook to Tarenus Durrel; Taren grants only a limited introduction to northern settlements, not the unrestricted Durrel access later Greyfen paperwork will claim as its objective.

Act VI — The People Behind the Ledgers

The merchant scheme has concrete faces beyond Darian: Sabine Rook coordinates and handles him; Oren Vask manages shipping papers; Maela Thorne works caravan movement; Garrick Venn holds northern warehouse and trade contacts; Edrin Pell keeps the financial accounts; and leatherworker Corvin Hale is coerced under threat into manufacturing the concealed Glass fittings. Their culpability is not uniform — Corvin acts under duress, and the network's exact hierarchy above Sabine is never fully charted.

Act VII — Sabine Rook Does Not Return

Behind Mark's knowledge, Darian's relationship with his handler fractures. At an old riverside storehouse, Darian confronts Sabine over control of the operation and of Mark, murders her, conceals the killing, and takes records that may help him command the merchant structure himself. Mark initially hears only that the association has broken down.

Act VIII — The Second Murder

Darian subsequently kills finance associate Edrin Pell at his counting house when Pell moves to summon help, and removes ledgers and correspondence. This murder is established DM-side continuity; Mark is not shown receiving an equivalent on-screen confession for it, and the public record initially treats Pell as missing.

Act IX — The Customs Confrontation

Lady Veyla Marrow, representing the financing interests above Darian and Sabine, confronts Darian at a customs-house meeting with proof of the hidden shipments and Mark's compromised authorisations. Mark succeeds on a decisive Wisdom save, destroying Darian's hand-held Glass controller himself, resisting the conditioning, and disclosing the compromised acts to his own command. Darian admits the manipulation and Sabine's murder in this scene — not Edrin Pell's killing. Marrow takes Darian into temporary custody; her own principal is left unidentified.

Act X — Five Days

Mark is placed on protected leave pending chaplain and command assessment rather than immediately returned to the field. Captain — or Ser — Kerr, Mark's commanding officer, authorises the recovery period and later the Greyfen operation; his exact relationship to B23 roadwarden Kerr Dain is never established either way. Five days of protected recovery pass before the Greyfen operation begins.

Act XI — Greyfen Crossing

Caldreth's field force assaults Greyfen, a Glass fort and intake station preparing Mark's planned conditioned return as a durable route to Matt and Taren. At the stone river crossing, Mark clears an elderly carter, directs two soldiers to brace a timber barricade across the narrow bridge, and frees a neglected tollhouse ballista. When the Glass convoy's wagon-mounted siege apparatus turns its violet destructive energy against the bridge's stone supports, Mark's following shot is a natural 20 for 38 piercing damage, destroying the siege weapon outright. The bridge survives with serious structural damage; the carter escapes unharmed.

Act XII — The Northern Service Road

Darian, bound in a separate prison wagon after the battle, is driven toward a northern service road by a desperate escort; the damaged wagon overturns at a narrow bend. Matt reaches him first and nearly kills the restrained prisoner outright. Mark stops his brother, then strikes the bound Darian unconscious himself — both Harcourts face formal discipline afterward for violence against a restrained prisoner, with no automatic paladin oath loss on either side.

Act XIII — Blackthorn Hollow and the Checkpoint

Greyfen's recovered archive reveals a second consignment already departed before dawn, due at Kelder's Reach before the next watch change. Riding to intercept, the party reaches Blackthorn Hollow, a road fork offering a fast main route and a secluded eastern track through old border watchtowers; Elira reads fresh wagon tracks confirming the convoy took the main road. At the northern checkpoint, the escort presents Mark's authentic but coerced authorisation — the lieutenant begins to accept it until Mark personally invalidates his own signature and orders the wagon seized. Two mounted escorts are defeated or surrender; the four-horse wagon is taken into Caldreth custody.

Act XIV — Tomas Fenwick

The intercepted wagon carries Tomas Fenwick, a Fellbrook quarryman forcibly implanted with a chest crystal and smaller growth fragments, restrained by a collar linking to an external control box — intended as a living asset to breach a sealed doorway at Kelder's Reach. Mark's natural-20 Arcana check identifies a protected isolation catch; throwing it severs the external command without another roll. Tomas regains voluntary movement, though the implanted crystal itself remains and he is placed under healers' care.

Act XV — Kelder's Reach and the Return

The five companions reach Kelder's Reach's northern storehouses ahead of the planned delivery. A grey-haired officer, a clerk, and two guards — the local receiving team for Tomas's forced breaching attempt — surrender to the arriving Caldreth force before the ancient sealed doors can be forced open; the doors remain closed and unidentified. Back in Caldreth, the compromised Bellweather authorisations are formally cancelled, Mark is reinstated to ordinary duty after chaplain clearance and a formal reprimand, and he receives his Level 4 milestone. Darian's letter to Mark remains unopened in the record; the decision about it, like the trial itself, belongs to Mark and to later history.

World State After Campaign B24

Mark Harcourt. Paladin 4, Oath of the Crown, STR 16, HP 32 (provisional on unchanged Constitution), AC 19, Level 4 feat/ASI unselected. Alive in Caldreth, reinstated to ordinary duty after a formal reprimand for striking a bound prisoner, his Crown oath intact. Formerly in love with Darian Rell; retains sole control of whether he ever answers Darian's letter.

Darian Rell. Alive, recaptured at Greyfen, transferred into Caldreth custody and awaiting trial on serious charges. Not killed, acquitted, redeemed, or reconciled with Mark by B24's close. His own known hand-held Glass controller is destroyed; no second surviving instrument is established.

Sabine Rook and Edrin Pell. Both dead, killed by Darian. Sabine's murder is confessed to Mark directly; Edrin's is established DM-side continuity that Mark is not shown learning on screen. Neither death is avenged by a further magical ritual or hidden successor claim.

Tomas Fenwick. Alive, freed from the wagon's external command, regaining voluntary movement and recovering under medical care by the three-week epilogue. The implanted chest crystal is not removed or cured — disconnecting the command is not the same as healing the body.

Greyfen, Blackthorn Hollow, and the northern checkpoint. Greyfen's intake station and archive are captured; its siege apparatus is destroyed and its bridge stands, though damaged. The checkpoint and Blackthorn Hollow fork remain ordinary, functioning Caldreth-controlled road infrastructure after the convoy's interception.

Kelder's Reach. B23's liberated upper fortress and neutral lower civilian sanctuary remain exactly as they were — this campaign does not reopen or reactivate the destroyed B23 command network. The newly discovered northern-storehouse loading hall and its ancient sealed doors are secured and guarded, their builders, purpose, and contents still unresolved.

Hard separations carried forward. Darian's personal conditioning, Greyfen's siege and intake apparatus, and Tomas's implanted breaching system are three distinct observed applications of wartime Glass technology, not one universal controller. None of them is established as Joren Cassel's surviving C-17 design, a Great Seal component, or a Way-network device. See the Canon Ledger for the complete B24 hard-lock set.

Open threads carried forward. Lady Veyla Marrow's ultimate financier and the surviving Glass merchant network beyond Greyfen and Bellweather; Captain Kerr's identity relative to B23's Kerr Dain, and Edrin Pell's relative to B23's unnamed soldier Pell; the contents of Darian's unopened letter and his eventual trial outcome; Tomas Fenwick's long-term medical prospects; and what, if anything, lies beyond Kelder's Reach's still-sealed ancient doors.

Canon status: follows the source account closely. B24 is a deep-history prequel and does not alter the modern later c. 343 ARV state established by B22 — see the Canon Ledger for the hard separation between Darian's personal conditioning, Greyfen's siege and intake apparatus, and Tomas Fenwick's implanted breaching system, the Magic Codex for the full technical registry, and the Atlas for Caldreth, Greyfen, and the eastern theatre's connected pre-Shattering geography.
